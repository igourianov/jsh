AI is the new autocorrect. With all the old issues.

I myself use AI as an editorial tool for my emails and LinkedIn posts, so this isn't about hating on AI. But you have to keep it on a tight leash, so that your output doesn't get filled with "duck". You have to learn to de-slop your content. Or rather teach your agent to not write like LinkedIn's built-in GPT.

AI is great for applying tone and correcting for proper English, but it's terrible at creating original thought. And most people by now can tell the difference.

I get instant gag reflex when I read:
• Here's what nobody tells you
• The uncomfortable truth is
• The X is not about A. It's about B.
• Or using the single stetement paragraphs, as if it adds weight

Update your system prompt. Teach your agent to write without filler words and noise. Always verify output before sending. Treat AI as your editor, not as your shadow writer.

EM dash is fine. The overpunctuated structure with condescending tone, as if your target audience is a 5 year old, is not.
