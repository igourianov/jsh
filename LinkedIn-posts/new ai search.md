LinkedIn's new AI job search is... disappointing.

Try this: ask it to combine multiple search terms, exclude specific companies, or sort results by date.

It can't do any of these basic things that traditional search handles easily.

This isn't AI search → it's a chatbot wrapper on a search API.

Here's the lesson: slapping "AI" on your product without understanding user needs doesn't create value. It creates frustration.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

What we actually need from job search:
  • Boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT)
  • Company/recruiter filtering
  • Proper sorting options
  • Search refinement capabilities

Not a conversational interface that can't execute basic queries.

AI should enhance product experiences, not replace working functionality with something worse.
