## Company

Fellow is an AI meeting assistant (Series A, backed by Craft Ventures, iNovia Capital and Felicis Ventures) that records, transcribes, summarizes and helps act on meetings. Positioned for security/compliance-sensitive teams (financial services, legal, healthcare) with zero-day retention options, redaction tools and a compliance portal; HIPAA, GDPR and SOC 2 certified. Integrates natively with Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, and syncs to Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce and other workflow tools. Customers include Shopify, HubSpot, WarnerMedia, Tucows and Dynatrace. Founded by the team behind Fluidware (acquired by SurveyMonkey).
