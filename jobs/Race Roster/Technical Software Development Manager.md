## Company

Race Roster is an endurance event registration platform founded in London, Ontario in 2011, now operating as ASICS Apps Canada (Race Roster North America Corporation), a subsidiary of Japanese multinational ASICS Corporation. Race Roster serves event directors with a dashboard for participant signups, donations and event revenue, plus a social marketing layer that compensates participant referrals. ASICS Apps is the broader umbrella covering endurance event technology (Race Roster), the ASICS Runkeeper mobile app and other digital fitness products. Small footprint (51-200 employees, 94 on LinkedIn) within a large parent company focused on the digital health and fitness industry.
