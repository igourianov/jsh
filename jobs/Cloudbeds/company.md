# Cloudbeds

**Company Type:** SaaS/Product
**Stage:** Scale-up (Late-stage, pre-IPO)
**Size:** ~725 employees
**Remote Policy:** 100% remote-first since founding in 2012, team spans 40+ countries

## Quick Take
- Strong product-market fit with 26,000+ properties in 150 countries, clear market leader in hospitality PMS
- Genuinely remote-first culture with 12+ years of distributed team experience
- Concerns around job security and recent layoffs reported by employees on Glassdoor

## Company & Product

Cloudbeds is an all-in-one hospitality management platform founded in 2012 by Adam Harris (CEO) and Richard Castle (COO). The platform serves independent hotels, hotel groups, hostels, boutique hotels, and resorts with property management, revenue management, distribution, and guest experience tools.

**Key Products:**
- Property Management System (PMS) - core operations
- Channel Manager - connects to 300+ OTAs (Booking.com, Expedia, Airbnb)
- Revenue Intelligence - pricing optimization
- Cloudbeds Signals - AI model trained on hospitality data for insights and automation
- CRM, Digital Marketing, Websites, Reputation Management

**Business Model:** SaaS subscription with 350+ integration partners. Revenue reported at $84.8M in 2024.

**Founding Story:** Co-founders were avid travelers who kept encountering hoteliers doing everything right but being let down by outdated technology. Built the company on the belief that "hoteliers deserve better."

## Company History

- **2012:** Founded in San Diego, remote-first from day one
- **2017:** First acquisition
- **2020:** Continued growth through pandemic as hotels modernized tech
- **2021:** Series D funding of $150M led by SoftBank Vision Fund (total raised: $253M)
- **2022:** Acquired Whistle (live texting solution for hotels) and one other entity
- **2024:** $84.8M revenue; partnership with Mirai for hotel distribution/marketing
- **2025-2026:** Awards from HotelTechAwards; partnership with Traversing.ai for AI contact center

CEO Adam Harris has stated M&A is not their growth strategy - they prefer organic growth through product improvement.

## Engineering Culture

Cloudbeds maintains an engineering blog at technology.cloudbeds.com where software engineers, UX designers, and product managers share technical content. The blog demonstrates investment in knowledge sharing and transparency.

**Open Source:** 23-25 public repositories on GitHub including:
- API wrappers (Python, PHP)
- Sample applications and integrations
- Testing tools (phpunit-testlistener-vcr)

The GitHub organization is relatively modest for a company this size, suggesting most engineering work is proprietary rather than open-source focused.

## Red Flags

- **Layoffs:** Multiple Glassdoor reviews mention sudden layoffs with minimal notice. Employees report "lots of layoffs that are unpredictable" and feeling job insecurity.
- **Compensation Inconsistency:** Pay varies widely between markets and depends on individual negotiation rather than transparent pay bands.
- **Interview Process:** Reports of unprofessional hiring process using third-party technical interviewers and poor recruiter communication.
- **Benefits Disparity:** Not all benefits are available globally, which could be frustrating for international employees.
