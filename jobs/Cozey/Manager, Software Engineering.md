Cozey is looking for an Engineering Manager who combines hands-on technical work with people leadership. The manager will collaborate across engineering, product, design, and operations to deliver business outcomes for Cozey's online furniture platform.

- Define and drive technical direction aligned with company strategy

- Identify technical and operational gaps proactively and develop solutions with the team

## Company

Cozey is a Montreal-based, online-first direct-to-consumer furniture company founded in 2020. They design and sell modular sofas, sofa-beds, tables, storage solutions, and accessories.
