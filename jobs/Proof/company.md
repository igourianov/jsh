# Company Research: Proof Technology

**Company Type:** Legal Tech SaaS Platform \
**Stage:** Series B Startup (Founded 2017) \
**Remote Policy:** Remote-first with offices in Boulder CO, Denver, Detroit, NYC

## Quick Take

- Series B legal tech startup ($30.4M raised Jan 2024) modernizing service of process and e-filing with 10%+ annual growth
- Glassdoor rating of 3.2/5 raises concerns about work-life balance (2.5/5), compensation (2.7/5), and culture (2.9/5)
- Engineering role offers modern tech stack (Go, React/TypeScript) in growing legal tech market but with startup risk and culture concerns

## Company & Product

Proof Technology (proofserve.com) is a legal services platform founded in 2017 by Eric J. Voogt, Esq., an attorney who experienced firsthand the inefficiencies of traditional process serving. The platform digitizes and automates service of process nationwide, connecting 3,000+ law firms with a vetted network of professional process servers.

**Core Products:**
- Service of Process (nationwide digital platform)
- Skip Tracing (locating defendants)
- E-Filing solutions
- Site Visits and verification services
- API integrations with legal case management systems (FileVine, Litify, Clio, MyCase)

**Notable Clients:** Holland & Knight, Perkins Coie, Cordell & Cordell, Fisher Phillips

**Company Size:** ~196 employees (51-200 range) with headquarters at 4845 Pearl East Circle, Boulder, CO 80301. Additional offices in Denver (HQ), Detroit (ops hub), and New York (ops hub). Sustained 10%+ annual growth serving the legal services market.

## Engineering Culture

**Tech Stack (from job posting and website):**
- Backend: Go, Ruby, Java, JavaScript
- Frontend: React, TypeScript, Next.js
- Architecture: Service-oriented, event-driven architectures
- Infrastructure: Message brokers (RabbitMQ, Kafka), CI/CD automation
- Content: Storyblok CMS

**Engineering Practices:**
- Focus on scalable, maintainable solutions
- Security and compliance emphasis (legal industry requirements)
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Remote-first engineering teams across multiple time zones
- Management of offshore contractors

**Team Structure:**
- 34 open positions including 3 engineering roles (indicating active hiring/growth)
- Engineering teams include backend, frontend, and full-stack roles
- Remote distributed teams with offshore contractor components

**No Dedicated Engineering Blog:** Search did not reveal a public engineering blog or significant technical thought leadership presence. Limited visibility into engineering practices and culture beyond job postings.

## Tech Stack & Architecture

The platform uses modern web technologies with Next.js frontend, React/TypeScript for UI components, and a backend built on Go, Ruby, Java, and JavaScript. Infrastructure emphasizes service-oriented and event-driven architectures with message brokers (RabbitMQ, Kafka). The company prioritizes automated testing, CI/CD practices, and secure software delivery frameworks appropriate for legal industry compliance requirements.

**Scale Challenges:** Serving 3,000+ law firms nationwide with real-time tracking, document management, and API integrations with major legal software platforms. The platform must handle compliance, audit requirements, and secure document delivery at scale.

## Product & Market Position

Proof positions itself as "the leading technology company in the legal services industry" and "the world's first on-demand process serving platform." The company modernizes a traditionally paper-based, manual process with instant access, real-time tracking, and technological integration.

**Market Position:**
- First-mover advantage in on-demand legal service platform
- Strong integration partnerships with major legal software (Clio, FileVine, Litify, MyCase)
- Serving mid-size to large law firms, collections agencies, government agencies, property management

**Innovation Focus:** Recent product launches include Chrome Extension for Filevine users (Feb 2025), API development, and expanding integrations. Company emphasizes "constant innovation" as core value.

## Concerns for Engineering Managers

⚠️ **Significant Red Flags:**

1. **Low Work-Life Balance (2.5/5)** - Critical concern for sustainable management role
2. **Compensation Below Market (2.7/5)** - "Pay is extremely low compared to tech industry standards" per reviews (though job posting shows $210k-$250k CAD which appears competitive)
3. **Culture & Morale Issues** - Leadership explicitly told managers not to prioritize culture or morale; multiple senior leadership departures
4. **Leadership Turnover** - VP of Ops, COO, and Head of Customer Success departed; potential instability
5. **Career Growth Concerns** - Operations leadership described as unapproachable and unsupportive
6. **Limited Engineering Visibility** - No public engineering blog, limited tech thought leadership, harder to assess engineering culture quality
7. **Startup Risk** - Series B stage means still proving business model; not yet profitable or exit-ready
8. **Offshore Management** - Role requires managing offshore contractors across time zones, adding complexity

**Moderate Concerns:**

- Only 53% employee recommendation rate vs typical tech companies (70%+)
- Below-average ratings across most Glassdoor categories
- Small engineering team size (~3 open eng roles out of 34 total) may limit resources
- Legal tech domain requires compliance/security overhead
- Remote-first with distributed teams requires strong async communication

**Positive Signals:**

- Recent Series B funding ($30.4M) provides runway
- 10%+ sustained growth indicates product-market fit
- Strong client roster (major law firms)
- Modern tech stack (Go, React/TypeScript)
- Solving real problem in underserved market
- Remote-first culture with flexibility
