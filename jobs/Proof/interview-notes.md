# Interview Notes: Proof - Senior Engineering Manager

## Questions for Recruiter

1. **Why is this role open?** - New headcount, backfill, or reorg?
2. **Who would this role report to?** - How long has the engineering leadership been in place?
3. **Offshore team structure** - Which locations and time zones?
4. **Team structure under this role** - How many direct reports? Are they ICs, tech leads, or managers?
