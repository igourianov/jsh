# Empire Life

- **Company Type:** Product (life insurance, wealth management, group benefits)
- **Stage:** Enterprise (subsidiary of E-L Financial, 98.3% owned)
- **Size:** ~900 employees (other sources up to ~1,150)
- **Remote Policy:** Kingston, ON HQ. Director posting is Remote, Canada. Developer postings say applicants across Canada may be considered

## Quick Take
- Stable, conservative Canadian insurer, top 10 by size, $19.6B assets. Majority owned by a holding company, so no funding or exit risk
- Mid-modernization: back office moving from COBOL/Easytrieve mainframe to cloud-native microservices (Java/Kotlin, Python, Go). Matches the Director Back Office Development role directly
- Glassdoor is positive on balance and culture, but tech pay is reported as uncompetitive. Stability is the main selling point

## Milestones
- 1923: founded in Toronto by Milton Palmer Langstaff
- 1929-1936: mergers with Commonwealth Life and Mutual Relief Life, HQ moved to Kingston
- 1968: E-L Financial acquired 94%
- 1987: acquired Montreal Life and Dominion of Canada life operations
- 2023: 100th anniversary
- 2025-12: acquired 80% of Specialty Life (lead generation and direct sales of life and health insurance)
- 2026 Q2: common shareholders' net income $83M vs $32M in Q2 2025, with company-wide strategic investment in technology systems called out as an offset

## Company & Product
Individual insurance (term, permanent, critical illness), wealth management (segregated funds, RRIFs, mutual funds via Empire Life Investments) and group benefits. Over 610,000 individual customers and 150,000 group plan members. Total assets over $19.6B as of 2024-12-31. President and CEO is Mark Sylvia, CTO is Chris Volk.

## Engineering Culture
No public engineering blog or GitHub organization found. Modernization is the stated engineering theme: decoding business rules from legacy code and proving parity with legacy output before cutover. Not verified beyond job postings.

## Tech Stack
- Legacy: COBOL, Easytrieve on mainframe
- Target: cloud-native microservices in Java/Kotlin, Python or Go. Cloud provider not stated in postings
- Event-driven and Kafka per the Director posting (see screening file)

## Team Health
- Glassdoor: 4.2/5 from ~188 reviews, 79% recommend
- Work/life balance 4.3, culture and values 4.2, career opportunities 3.8
- Software developers rate work/life balance and compensation 4.7 each
- Pros: remote flexibility, collaborative teams, good benefits and bonuses
- Cons: "tech salaries are not competitive, it's a great place if your main value is stability". Diversity efforts described as superficial. One older review calls it very top heavy
- Small sample for IT specifically. Did not read individual reviews in full

## Business Stability
Backed by E-L Financial, profitable, investing in technology systems. Q4 2025 profit fell on higher expenses (technology spend). No layoffs or restructuring found in search. Desjardins completed a Guardian Capital acquisition in 2026, unrelated to Empire Life.

## Red Flags
- Compensation may sit below the market for tech leadership. Director range is unstated, estimated $180-220k CAD
- Mainframe modernization carries delivery risk and long timelines
- Top-heavy reputation in older reviews
- Expense pressure from technology investment may draw cost scrutiny
