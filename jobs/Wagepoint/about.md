# Wagepoint - Company Research

## Overview

**Founded:** 2012 \
**Founder:** Shrad Rao \
**Headquarters:** Calgary, Canada \
**Status:** Acquired by PSG (September 2020) \
**Employees:** 135-200 (reports vary; fully remote) \
**Customers:** 20,000+ small businesses, accountants, bookkeepers across North America

## History

- **2012:** Founded by Shrad Rao
- **2020:** Acquired by PSG (Private Equity) for $10M
- **2022:** Acquired KinHR (August 2022)
- **2025:** Major leadership changes
  - January 2025: Neil Carew appointed as COO
  - August 2025: Ben Richmond joined as President & CEO (former Managing Director North America for Xero)

## Funding

- **Total Raised:** $10M
- **Last Round:** September 9, 2020 ($10M)
- **Investors:** 4 institutional investors including:
  - Extreme Venture Partners
  - Timia Capital
  - BDC

## Product

**Core Product:** Cloud-based payroll management solution for small businesses

**Key Features:**
- Payroll deductions calculation
- Direct deposit processing
- Time & attendance tracking
- Pay stub generation
- Retirement plan management
- Government remittance handling

**Product Roadmap:**
- **Wagepoint 2.0** in development - promises faster processing

## Engineering & Technology

## Key Insights for Engineering Org Hire

**Opportunities:**
- Wagepoint 2.0 rebuild presents greenfield/modernization opportunities
- Expanding engineering team suggests growth phase
- New CEO from Xero may bring product-led growth expertise
- PE backing (PSG) provides funding stability
- Small enough for individual impact, large enough for resources

**Considerations:**
- Private equity ownership (acquired 2020) - typical 3-5 year hold period may mean exit planning
- Recent leadership changes (CEO in Aug 2025) - new strategic direction likely
- Customer base focused on small businesses - different scale challenges than enterprise
- Canadian company (Calgary HQ) - timezone, currency, benefits considerations

**Product Domain:**
- Payroll is adjacent to HCM (candidate's background in Dayforce HCM is highly relevant)
- Regulatory complexity (tax, compliance) similar to HCM compliance work
- Small business focus vs candidate's enterprise background - different customer segment

**Tech Stack Alignment:**
- TypeScript/React aligns with candidate's frontend experience
- AWS aligns with candidate's cloud experience (Azure → AWS transition needed)
- Kubernetes experience is directly transferable
- Distributed systems experience is relevant
