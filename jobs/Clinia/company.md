# Clinia

- **Company Type:** SaaS / Health AI Platform
- **Stage:** Startup (Series A)
- **Size:** ~25 engineers, ~50 total (estimated)
- **Remote Policy:** Remote-first ("Work From Anywhere"), HQ in Montreal

## Quick Take
- AI-powered health search and data infrastructure company with a clear niche and strong strategic partner (TELUS Health)
- "Liberated enterprise" culture with radical transparency (open finances, open compensation) and flat hierarchy
- Small engineering org (~25 people) with Director of Engineering role open, suggesting leadership growth phase
- Compensation and benefits rated lower on Glassdoor (3.8/5), typical of early-stage Canadian startups

## Milestones
- 2016: Founded in Montreal by Simon Bedard, Etienne Soulard Geoffrion and Felix La Rocque Carrier
- 2019: First client (Dialogue)
- 2021: Partnership with Google and Waze
- 2023: $10M Series A led by TELUS Ventures ($7.8M from TELUS); strategic partnership with TELUS Health
- 2024: B Corporation certification; SOC 2 Type II certification

## Company & Product
Clinia builds health-grade infrastructure and APIs that enable organizations to deploy AI-powered search, generative Q&A, summarization and agentic workflows across fragmented health data. The platform adds a "health context layer" on top of existing data, powering use cases like provider search, patient cohort search, clinical summarization and patient education.

Key platform components:
- Search API (semantic + keyword search across structured/unstructured health data)
- Generative API (AI-driven health applications, multi-turn conversations)
- Health Data Fabric (unified health information integration)

The platform is FHIR-compatible, SOC 2 Type II compliant, HIPAA/GDPR/PIPEDA compliant, with PHI masking and granular access controls. Clinia claims their health-specific models outperform state-of-the-art large models on medical benchmarks.

Primary customers appear to be health systems, insurers, employers and health tech companies. TELUS Health is the anchor strategic partner.

## Engineering Culture
- "Liberated enterprise" model: ideas over hierarchy, no emphasis on titles
- Radical transparency: all company information including finances and compensation accessible to everyone
- Teams lead their own recruitment with Talent & Culture support
- B Corp certified, signaling values-driven culture
- 34 GitHub repositories: primary languages are Go, TypeScript/JavaScript and Python
- Open source projects include an AI framework for Go, API client libraries (Python/Go/JS), OpenAPI codegen tooling, and embedding benchmarks (MTEB)
- Hiring process is 6 stages: resume review, culture fit (20 min), technical interview with peers, technical test, final regroup, offer

## Tech Stack
- **Backend:** Go (primary), Python
- **Frontend:** TypeScript/JavaScript (React likely, given frontend roles)
- **AI/ML:** Custom health-specific models, embeddings, semantic search, NLP, generative AI
- **Infrastructure:** Cloud-based (likely GCP given Google partnership history), SOC 2 compliant
- **Data:** FHIR-compatible health data fabric, structured + unstructured data
- **Tools:** OpenAPI for API design, Metabase for analytics, Ory Kratos for identity management
- **Equipment:** MacBooks for all engineers

## Business Stability
- $10M+ Series A (closed Dec 2023), led by TELUS Ventures with existing investors reinvesting
- Total funding: ~$12.1M
- Anchor partnership with TELUS Health provides revenue stability and market access
- B Corp certification and SOC 2 Type II suggest organizational maturity beyond typical Series A
- No reports of layoffs, restructuring or negative news found
- Risk: heavy reliance on TELUS Health as strategic partner. If that relationship weakens, business impact could be significant.
- Risk: Series A funding from late 2023 means they may need to raise again in 2025-2026 depending on burn rate.

## Red Flags
- Compensation rated 3.8/5.0 on Glassdoor. For a Canadian startup this is common but worth validating salary bands.
- Very small review sample on Glassdoor (8 reviews). Hard to draw definitive conclusions about culture.
- Director of Engineering role open (posted Jan 2026) could mean the previous one left, or the role is newly created for a growing org. Worth probing in conversation.
- "Liberated enterprise" and flat hierarchy sound appealing but can create ambiguity around decision-making and accountability at scale.
