# Riva

- **Company Type:** SaaS/Platform (CRM Data Integration)
- **Stage:** Scale-up (private, subsidiary of Omni Technology Solutions, Inc.)
- **Size:** ~120 employees across 6 continents (107 in Canada)
- **Remote Policy:** Fully remote with optional hubs in Edmonton AB, Toronto ON, and Pictou NS
- **HQ:** Edmonton, Alberta, Canada
- **Founded:** 2008

## Quick Take
- Stable, profitable niche player in CRM data integration for regulated industries (finance, insurance, life sciences). 15+ years in business with 650+ enterprise customers.
- Family-founded company (Aldo Zanoni co-founder, now Executive Chairman). New CEO Stephane Zanoni appointed April 2025. Small-to-mid size with consistent recognition as a top Canadian employer.
- Glassdoor rating of 3.6/5 (42 reviews) with mixed signals. Some reviews cite toxic dynamics and limited compensation, while others praise flexibility and team culture.

## Milestones
- **2008:** Founded in Edmonton, Alberta as Riva CRM Integration
- **2022-2025:** Named Alberta's Top 75 Employers four consecutive years. Also recognized as Canada's Top Small and Medium Employers and Best Employers for Recent Graduates.
- **2025 (April):** CEO transition. Stephane Zanoni became CEO; co-founder Aldo Zanoni moved to Executive Chairman.
- **2025:** Named to FinTech Global's InsurTech100 list. Forrester Consulting study documented 352% ROI.
- **2025:** Rebranded/repositioned as "Riva" with AI-driven platform capabilities

## Company & Product
Riva is a CRM data integration platform that captures emails, meetings, and calendar data to create a unified "Customer 360" view. The core product syncs data bi-directionally between CRM systems (Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics 365) and communication tools (Outlook, Exchange, Gmail, Google Workspace).

Key differentiators:
- Deep support for regulated industries with strict compliance requirements (SOC 2 Type II certified, 2,000+ security reviews completed)
- Pass-through architecture that doesn't store customer data
- Support for up to 150,000 simultaneous users and 5M+ calendar appointments/month
- Largest single deployment: 65,000+ end users
- Flexible deployment: multi-tenant cloud, dedicated cloud, or on-premises

Revenue comes from enterprise SaaS subscriptions. Over 200 global financial service organizations are customers.

## Business Stability
- **Funding:** No external funding rounds identified. Listed as "unfunded" on Tracxn. Subsidiary of Omni Technology Solutions, Inc., suggesting parent company backing.
- **No layoffs reported** in available sources.
- **Revenue:** Not publicly disclosed. 650+ enterprise customers in regulated industries suggests a stable revenue base.
- **Growth signals:** Consistent employer awards, new CEO appointment, AI platform pivot, InsurTech100 recognition, Forrester ROI study all suggest the company is investing in growth.
- **Risk:** Small company in a competitive space (Salesforce native tools, competing sync solutions). Dependence on Salesforce ecosystem.

## Red Flags
- **Mixed Glassdoor reviews:** Some reviews mention toxic workplace, bullying, and racism. While a minority, these are concerning signals that warrant probing during interviews.
- **Family ownership dynamics:** Founded and chaired by Aldo Zanoni, with family members in leadership. This can create power dynamics that limit meritocratic advancement.
- **No engineering visibility:** Zero public engineering content, no open source, no tech blog. This could indicate engineering is viewed as a cost center rather than a strategic asset.
- **Compensation concerns:** Multiple reviews cite limited pay and vacation. Edmonton-based compensation may be below market for remote-capable roles.
- **Small company scale:** ~120 employees means limited career ladder and potential for single points of failure in engineering leadership.
