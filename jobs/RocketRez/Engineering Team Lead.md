## Company

RocketRez builds a cloud ticketing and business management platform for tours and attractions: tour operators, theme parks, zoos, aquariums and immersive experiences. It combines ticketing, timed entry, capacity controls, memberships, events, point of sale, retail, food and beverage, inventory and eCommerce in one system, sold in person, online and through partner channels. Targets mid-market to enterprise attractions. Founded in 2011 by John Pendergrast, headquartered in Steinbach, Manitoba, with staff in Canada and the US.
