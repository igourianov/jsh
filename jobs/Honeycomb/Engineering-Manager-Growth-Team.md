## Company

Honeycomb is an observability platform that helps engineering teams debug production systems using distributed tracing and high-cardinality data exploration. Unlike traditional monitoring tools that silo data across logs, metrics and traces, Honeycomb unifies all telemetry data into a single queryable store, returning results in seconds. The platform uses machine learning (BubbleUp) to surface outliers and anomalies. Customers include HelloFresh, Slack, LaunchDarkly and Vanguard. Series D funded, 200+ employees. Named to Forbes' America's Best Startups 2022 and 2023.
