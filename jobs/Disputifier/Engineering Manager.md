## Summary

Disputifier's first Engineering Manager, reporting directly to the CTO. The role exists to take people and process load off the CTO. You own 6-8 mid-to-senior engineers, a mix of in-house and nearshore, fully distributed across North American timezones with no core hours.

The team's central piece of work is migrating the product off a legacy PHP codebase onto a modern Rails stack (Ruby 4, Rails 8) on Aurora MySQL and AWS, with a server-rendered front-end. Exact team scope is stated as still open and will flex toward the hire's strengths.

The posting is explicit that this is a people-and-process job: roughly 80% management, with low expectations on direct code contribution. Coding is encouraged if wanted but is not the job. Technical involvement runs through code review, mentoring, architecture discussion, scoping and evaluating solutions.

Experience bar is low: 1+ years of direct people management and ideally 3+ years of engineering background before management. Rails is preferred with stated flexibility. They want someone fluent with AI coding tools who can judge the output rather than rubber-stamp it, and they say so pointedly ("this is not a slop-factory").

## Company

Disputifier is a fintech startup serving e-commerce merchants. Its platform automates chargeback prevention and chargeback response, replacing a manual dispute process with an automated workflow that reduces chargebacks and raises win rates. Pricing is performance-based, so revenue is tied to customer outcomes. The product integrates into the e-commerce stack and is described as rapidly evolving. Team is fully distributed. Engineering is small enough that the CTO has been carrying all management directly.

InTandem Talent is the recruiting partner running this search, not the employer. It is a small talent advisory firm (2-10 employees) that describes itself as embedding into client teams rather than operating on commission-based agency placement.
