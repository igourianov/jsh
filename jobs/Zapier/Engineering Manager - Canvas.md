Position Overview
=================
Zapier seeks an Engineering Manager to lead the Canvas team within its Enterprise Zone, overseeing critical platform infrastructure that provides visibility, dependency mapping, and collaborative management of automation assets at enterprise scale.

Location & Employment
=====================
- Location: San Francisco, California (Remote/Telecommute)
- Employment Type: Full-time
- Applicant Location Requirements: United States

Compensation
============
- Salary Range: $187.8K–$281.6K (USD) or CA$187.8K–CA$281.6K (Canada)
- Additional Benefits: Equity, Bonus

Key Responsibilities
====================
The manager will lead a team of six cross-functional engineers while:
- Collaborating with product and design partners on customer value delivery
- Conducting weekly one-on-ones and team meetings to monitor progress
- Keeping leadership informed through transparent communication
- Mentoring engineers and managing performance
- Participating in code reviews and establishing quality standards
- Advocating for platform investments to skeptical stakeholders
- Managing competing priorities from enterprise customers and internal teams
- Recruiting and training new engineering talent

Required Qualifications
=======================
- Minimum two years managing cross-functional engineering teams
- Demonstrated expertise in distributed systems and backend infrastructure
- Experience building foundational platforms adopted by multiple teams
- Comfort with enterprise requirements: "permissions models, compliance, security, and governance"
- Production code shipping experience with strong technical fundamentals
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
- Customer empathy and problem-solving orientation

Preferred Qualifications
========================
- Experience integrating AI into enterprise products thoughtfully
- Understanding of balancing platform investments with visible feature delivery
