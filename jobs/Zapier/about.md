# Zapier - Engineering Information

Source: https://engineering-interviewing.zapier.app/
Scraped: 2025-12-02

## Company Overview

Zapier is building "the largest fully automated system powered by AI" focused on meaningful automation outcomes. The company operates with distributed teams and emphasizes remote work culture.

**Core Values & Operating Principles:**
- Move Fast Forward
- Own the Outcome
- Delight Customers
- Default to Transparency
- Trust, communication, and values alignment

The company emphasizes that interviews evaluate cultural alignment alongside technical skills, encouraging candidates to bring authenticity and genuineness while also assessing if Zapier is the right fit for them.

## Engineering at Zapier

### Development Workflow

- **Early Collaboration**: Engineers use GitLab Merge Requests early with incomplete code to get feedback quickly
- **Default to Transparency**: Share work promptly for feedback
- **Frequent Deployments**: Smaller, frequent changes reduce outage risks and boost confidence in code quality
- **Documentation Priority**: Documentation is prioritized equally with coding
- **Tool Access**: Engineers have access to necessary SaaS tools

### Frontend Engineering Stack

- **Core Technologies**: JavaScript, React, Redux
- **Build Tools**: Babel, Webpack
- **Type Safety**: Flow, TypeScript
- **Styling**: Emotion
- **Testing**: Jest
- **Framework**: Next.js
- **Data Management**: Apollo

### Backend Engineering Stack

Backend team emphasizes "clean, Pythonic code" with proper abstractions and thorough testing:

- **Language/Framework**: Python, Django, Django Rest Framework
- **Task Processing**: Celery

### Infrastructure

Zapier's infrastructure relies heavily on AWS:

- **Orchestration**: Kubernetes (EKS clusters), managed via Terraform
- **CI/CD**: GitLab, ArgoCD, Helm
- **Databases**: RDS, Elasticache, S3, Lambda
- **Monitoring**: Datadog, Prometheus, Grafana, Sentry

## Organizational Structure

### EPD Trios Framework

Zapier's Build Organization is structured around **EPD Trios** - collaborative leadership units comprising:
- Engineering Manager
- Product Manager
- Product Designer

These three roles work together as a leadership unit.

### Hierarchical Organization

Teams are organized into three levels:

1. **Groups**: Collections of Zones focused on common product areas with shared strategic goals
2. **Zones**: Specialized divisions handling specific features or product components requiring close collaboration
3. **Teams (Squads)**: Individual units typically led by an EPD Trio

### Functional Responsibilities

**Engineering:**
- Manages execution and delivery timelines
- Ensures scalability, reliability, and security
- Progress tracked through Monthly Business Reviews

**Product Management:**
- Drives strategy and roadmaps
- Owns final decision-making authority while collaborating across disciplines

**Design:**
- Shapes user experience and translates strategy
- Plays a key role in translating and enhancing strategy
- Ensures the company's vision is reflected across all products

### Collaboration Model

The structure balances autonomy with alignment through strategic cross-functional partnerships. Teams operate with independence while remaining connected to broader organizational objectives through structured communication and shared metrics.

## Interview Tips & Preparation

### Core Interview Principles

**Communication & Clarity:**
- Understand each question before responding
- Ask for clarifications if needed - this is encouraged
- Provide direct, concise answers rather than lengthy responses

**Answer Structure:**
- Use concrete examples from past experiences
- Emphasize your specific actions and measurable results
- Include metrics to demonstrate tangible impact
- Keep responses well-organized and focused on key points

**Authenticity:**
- Share both successes and challenges genuinely
- Avoid over-polishing your narrative
- Authenticity resonates with interviewers

### Pre-Interview Research

- Review Zapier's culture and values to ensure alignment
- Prepare thoughtful questions about the role, team dynamics, challenges, and company culture
- Watch interview process overview videos provided on their site
- Familiarize yourself with Zapier's products and recent innovations

### Technical Setup

For Zoom video interviews:
- Test your background, audio levels, and lighting beforehand
- Consider using virtual backgrounds
- Use noise-suppression tools like Krisp if needed
