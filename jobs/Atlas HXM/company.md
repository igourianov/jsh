## Company & Product
Atlas HXM is the world's largest direct Employer of Record, helping companies hire and manage employees in 160+ countries without establishing local entities. Unlike competitors that use third-party partners, Atlas owns its legal entities directly in each market (the "Direct EOR" model).

Their HXM (Human Experience Management) platform provides end-to-end services: Employer of Record, global payroll, benefits administration, compliance management, visa & immigration support, talent onboarding, and HR outsourcing. The platform uses a "Glocal" model combining global technology with local expertise.

Key clients are mid-market and enterprise companies expanding internationally. The company competes with Deel, Remote, Papaya Global and Oyster HR in the rapidly growing global EOR space.

## Red Flags
- **Severe leadership instability:** Rapid CEO/CFO/CTO turnover suggests strategic confusion or board-level dysfunction
- **Recurring layoffs:** Every 4-5 months per employee reviews. Hundreds let go despite $200M funding round
- **Very low Glassdoor scores:** 2.6/5 overall with only 32% recommending. Career opportunities rated just 2.2/5
- **Small engineering team for scope:** ~48 engineers supporting a platform across 160+ countries raises questions about tech debt and capacity
- **No engineering visibility:** No blog, no open source, no public tech talks. Suggests engineering may not be a first-class function
- **US bias:** Non-US employees report being disadvantaged in reviews
- **Competitive pressure:** EOR market is consolidating rapidly with better-funded competitors
