## Summary

Engineering Manager leading a team that builds and maintains bespoke custom applications and integrations within Heart & Stroke's technology ecosystem. The role sits at the intersection of delivery leadership and technical architecture — the manager oversees the full project lifecycle (discovery through operations) while also driving a modernization agenda: engineering maturity uplift, AI-assisted tooling adoption, CI/CD robustness and technical debt reduction.

The role bridges a technical team and a range of non-technical stakeholders (Product, Fundraising, Marketing, Mission) and works closely with Platform, Data, Security and Infrastructure & Operations teams. Technical vendor management is part of scope.

- Architect and oversee delivery of custom applications and enterprise integrations
- Lead evaluation, piloting and operationalization of AI-assisted development tools
- Drive engineering process transformation: CI/CD, observability, documentation, technical debt
- Manage stakeholders across Product, Fundraising, Marketing, Mission and project management
- Coordinate and oversee technology vendors for in-scope project work
- Run agile ceremonies; manage scope, timelines and budgets across concurrent initiatives
- Lead talent: hiring, coaching, performance management, succession planning
- Ensure security, compliance and NFRs are embedded in all solutions

## Company

Heart & Stroke is a Canadian non-profit charitable foundation with over 70 years of history in cardiovascular and stroke health. The organization funds medical research, runs public health programs and advocates for policy change. It has invested more than $1.65 billion in research since 1952 and claims a 75%+ reduction in heart disease and stroke death rates in Canada over that period.

Technology at Heart & Stroke supports fundraising operations, mission delivery, marketing and internal business functions. The engineering team builds and maintains custom applications and integrations connecting the organization's technology ecosystem — a mix of bespoke development and off-the-shelf platform customization (digital experience platforms, HRIS and similar enterprise systems).
