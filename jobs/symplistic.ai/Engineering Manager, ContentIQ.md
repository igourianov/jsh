Lead the small engineering team behind ContentIQ, symplistic.ai's enterprise AI knowledge and retrieval platform. ContentIQ connects enterprise content across websites, documents, SharePoint, OneDrive and internal systems, and layers enterprise search, RAG, document processing, governance, permissions, integrations and observability on top of it, so AI agents get secure, governed access to organizational knowledge. It is positioned as the knowledge layer behind platforms like IBM watsonx Orchestrate.

Explicitly not a management-only role: the posting expects the manager to challenge technical decisions, jump into hard engineering problems and take features from concept to production. Substantial customer-facing work, assisting enterprise deployments, running technical discovery and handling customer escalations.
