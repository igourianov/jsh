The most significant gap is Node.js backend development. The job requires 8+ years with Node.js, but Ilia's backend expertise is in .NET/C# ecosystem. While both are backend technologies and architectural patterns transfer, there's a 6-12 month ramp-up for Node.js-specific patterns, ecosystem, and best practices.

⚠️ **Active Coding Expectation**
Job description states candidate "thrives on day-to-day details, coding, and troubleshooting" suggesting 30-40% of time spent writing production code. Ilia's recent role has been more management-focused with hands-on technical work through reviews, design, and architecture rather than daily feature coding.

⚠️ **Director-Level Scope**
Position is Director of Engineering (level above EM). While Ilia has the experience (managed 25 people across multiple teams), the title represents a step up and may come with broader organizational/strategic responsibilities.

## Recommendation

**Moderate-Strong Fit with Caveats**

This role is a good match for leadership capabilities, SaaS experience, cloud architecture, and general tech stack (especially C#, React, TypeScript, cloud infrastructure). However, the Node.js backend requirement and daily coding expectation create meaningful gaps.

**Best Case:** Atlas values full-stack leadership with strong architectural and cloud experience, and sees Node.js as learnable for someone with Ilia's .NET background.

**Risk:** If they need someone who can immediately contribute to Node.js codebase daily, the ramp-up time could be a concern.

The role appears to blend IC+Manager responsibilities more than Ilia's recent pure EM work, which could be either a positive (if seeking more hands-on) or negative (if preferring pure leadership).
