## Company Description

Atlas Technology Solutions is an Employer-of-Record (EOR) provider offering HR and compliance infrastructure for international expansion. The company describes itself as "For People, By People" with a globally distributed workforce across the U.S., UK, India, Colombia, China, and beyond.

## Key Responsibilities

- Deliver direction and risk mitigation with tailored solutions for cloud migrations and cloud-native development
- Partner with product owners to refine requirements for B2B SaaS architecture
- Establish and lead high-performing developer and engineering teams
- Provide technical oversight of feature development and design
- Coordinate proof-of-concept initiatives with the Director of Architecture
- Evaluate open-source and commercial solutions for third-party integration
- Establish thought leadership for multi-cloud SaaS platform vision
- Oversee scalable, portable cloud-native applications
