## Summary

Engineering Manager role leading a cross-functional pod within Thumbtack's New Hiring Experience org, specifically the Pro Feedback and Targeting team. This is 0->1 work rebuilding how pros are matched to jobs, moving from static preference forms to an AI/LLM-driven system that learns from pro behavior over time. The EM partners with a Product Manager to set technical/product strategy, manages a full-stack team (backend, frontend, iOS, Android) including Staff+ engineers, and owns execution against OKRs. High-visibility role given the strategic bet Thumbtack is placing on this initiative.

## Company

Thumbtack is a consumer marketplace app connecting homeowners with local home service professionals (300,000+ pros) for repairs, maintenance and larger projects. The company is investing heavily in AI/LLM-based matching technology to replace static preference-based pro targeting with a system that learns pro capabilities and preferences dynamically, aiming to improve job-matching quality and pro outcomes.
