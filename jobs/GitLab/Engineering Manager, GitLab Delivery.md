# Engineering Manager, GitLab Delivery

**Location:** Remote, Canada; Remote, EMEA
**Job URL:** https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/gitlab/jobs/8331155002

## About GitLab

GitLab develops an AI-powered DevSecOps Platform used by more than 100,000 organizations. The company's mission centers on enabling broad participation in software development. The organization emphasizes integrating AI into daily workflows as a productivity tool.

## Role Overview

This position involves leading a geographically dispersed team responsible for making GitLab deployment, upgrades, and operations straightforward for customers across various infrastructure types—from single-node VMs to large Kubernetes clusters.

## Key Responsibilities

- Build and lead a distributed team in a high-performance environment
- Recruit, integrate, and mentor team members aligned with company values
- Refine agile, asynchronous workflows for iterative tooling delivery
- Partner with Product Management to align engineering with business objectives
- Oversee reliability and operational simplicity across GitLab.com, Dedicated, and self-managed deployments
- Enhance customer deployment and upgrade experiences
- Leverage technical expertise in Kubernetes Operators and Helm charts to guide decisions
- Participate in incident management rotations

## Required Qualifications

- Experience managing deployment tooling or platform engineering teams at scale
- Practical knowledge of Kubernetes Operators, Helm charts, and upgrade patterns
- Understanding of cloud-native architectures, database lifecycle management, and zero-downtime upgrade strategies
- Background running consumer or enterprise-scale platforms
- Capability to investigate complex operational issues and communicate findings clearly
- Demonstrated success building and managing distributed, remote teams
- Cross-functional collaboration experience with Infrastructure, Support, and Customer Success
- Openness to candidates with diverse backgrounds and transferable skills
