## Company & Product

Founded in 2014 by John Waldmann and Rushi Patel, Homebase is "the everything app for hourly teams" providing scheduling, time tracking, payroll, HR, and communication tools for small businesses. The platform operates on a freemium model with single-location businesses using basic features free, while multi-location and advanced features require paid tiers.

The company has achieved strong market validation with 100,000+ small business customers and 2.5+ million workers using the platform. Recent recognition includes Forbes' America's Best Startup Employers 2024, Webby Awards for Best HR & Employee App (2023, 2024, 2025), and Inc.'s 2025 Best in Business for Best AI Implementation.

In 2025, Homebase significantly expanded AI capabilities, launching AI Assistants that handled nearly 2 million admin tasks within months, including automated hiring support, smart scheduling, and payroll processing automation. The company integrates with major POS systems, accounting software, and payroll providers.

## Engineering Culture

Homebase emphasizes a supportive, learning-oriented culture. Job postings highlight React.js, React Native, and modern full-stack development. The company has engineering hubs in Toronto, San Francisco, Denver, and Houston operating in hybrid mode.

Recent job openings include Senior React Native Engineer, Senior Front End Engineer (React.js), Senior Full-Stack Engineer, Staff Engineer for Core Platform, and multiple Engineering Manager roles, indicating active growth and investment in engineering talent.

Engineering reviews mention "warm and supporting culture," "keeps up with growing technologies," and "culture is great and conducive for learning." The company appears committed to technical modernization while supporting career development.
