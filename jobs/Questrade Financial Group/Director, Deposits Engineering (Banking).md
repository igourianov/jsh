## Company

Questrade Financial Group is a Canadian financial services group built around Questrade, a low-cost digital brokerage positioned as an alternative to the big banks. The group spans Questrade (securities and FX investing), Questbank, Questrade Wealth Management (managed portfolios), Community Trust Company, Zolo (real estate) and Flexiti (point-of-sale financing). Headcount is 1001-5000.

This role sits in Questbank, the group's banking arm, which is building full-service digital banking on top of vendor core banking platforms. The company markets itself heavily on AI-driven innovation and describes its ATS as using AI for application screening with human review of final decisions.
