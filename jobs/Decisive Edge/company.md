## Company & Product
Decisive Edge positions itself as "active builders" rather than traditional consultants. Services split into Building (AI-assisted full-stack development, agentic workflows, intelligent automation) and Advising (ideation, market intelligence, business planning and financial modelling, decision analysis, risk quantification, prototyping, go-to-market). Engagements follow an "ACE Framework": Activate, Coach, Execute. Methods named: Monte Carlo simulation, Bayesian estimation, Lean Startup.

Portfolio products:
- Calintro: "Career Intelligence for the Next Generation of Professionals". Career management combining training, technology and coaching for emerging professionals, with university partners. Pre-revenue, raising financing for proof of concept, pilot and initial commercialization.
- Math Mavens: platform for independent tutors.
- Quantifidence: uncertainty management tool.
