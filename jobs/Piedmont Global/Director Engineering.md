## Summary

Foundational engineering leadership role responsible for transitioning a single product team into a multi-squad organization. The Director of Engineering bridges product vision and technical execution, owning team health, performance and output. The role is heavily hands-on: contributing to the codebase, owning core libraries and architecture, performing code reviews. Also responsible for vendor management, engineering metrics (Cycle Time, Deployment Frequency, MTTR) and developer tooling including AI-assisted development.

- Design and implement transition from one central team to multiple autonomous squads
- Lead by example contributing to codebase on critical-path initiatives
- Own technical architecture, core libraries and high-level design
- Build engineering processes, SDLC standards and deployment protocols
- Track performance metrics and remove friction
- Partner with Head of Product on roadmaps
- Manage external engineering vendors
- Implement CI/CD automation and AI-assisted tooling
