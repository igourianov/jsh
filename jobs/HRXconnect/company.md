## Quick Take
- HRXconnect is a recruiting agency posting for an undisclosed client. Nothing here describes the real employer, so the client is the thing to pin down.
- The client's identity is not confirmed. The only Deloitte match found for "Canada Fast 50 2024 #5" is Relay (Toronto, 9578% growth). Relay is an online business banking fintech, so it does not fit "Enterprise Software" cleanly, and the "Fast 500 2025 #1 Enterprise Software" claim could not be verified. Treat Relay as a candidate only.
- If the client is Relay: 201-500 employees, about 58% headcount growth in 12 months, $32.2M Series B (2024) and $50M growth round (2026), Toronto engineering manager roles in the CA$207-297K range for senior levels. That would make the estimated 170-200k CAD for this role look low. Ask the recruiter for the client name before any call.
