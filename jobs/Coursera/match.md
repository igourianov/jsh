### Overall Assessment

This is a **solid match for core EM competencies** but with **notable technical and domain gaps**.

**Strengths:** The candidate's EM experience, team building track record, and technical leadership background align well with Coursera's needs. Experience scaling teams, balancing platform health with feature delivery, and fostering high-performance culture are directly transferable.

**Concerns:** The tech stack mismatch is the primary concern. While the candidate has strong full-stack experience, the Java/Scala ecosystem is meaningfully different from .NET/C#. Additionally, the lack of ML/Data Science collaboration experience and GenAI exposure may be limiting given Coursera's focus on AI-driven educational outcomes.

**Recommendation:** Worth applying. The strong EM fundamentals and proven ability to scale teams may outweigh tech stack differences, especially if Coursera values leadership over hands-on coding. However, be prepared to address:
1. How you've ramped up on new tech stacks in the past
2. Interest in EdTech and skills taxonomy domain

4. GenAI curiosity and learning initiatives
