# Recruiter Screening Questions - Coursera Engineering Manager

## Recent Changes & Stability

**Layoffs & Reorganization:**
- I noticed Coursera went through a workforce reduction in late 2024. How has that impacted the Skills and Credentials team specifically?
- With the recent layoffs, what's the current team size and structure for this role?
- How has the engineering org been restructured since the October 2024 changes?

**Leadership Transition:**
- Greg Hart recently took over as CEO in February 2025. Has there been any shift in engineering priorities or strategy under the new leadership?
- What's the vision for the Skills and Credentials team in 2025 under the new CEO?

## Team & Role Specifics

**Team Context:**
- What's the current size of the Skills and Credentials engineering team?
- How many direct reports would this role have initially?
- Is this a backfill or a new position? If backfill, why did the previous person leave?
- What's the team's current focus - more feature delivery or platform stabilization?

**Technical Scope:**
- The job posting mentions Java/Scala backend. What percentage of the existing codebase is in each language?
- Is there flexibility for candidates with strong backend experience in other languages (like C#/.NET), or is Java/Scala experience essential?
- What's the balance between hands-on technical work and people management for this role?

## Business & Product

**Product Direction:**
- How is the Skills and Credentials platform performing? Is it meeting adoption goals?
- What are the biggest technical or product challenges facing this team in 2025?
- How does this team's roadmap tie into Coursera's GenAI initiatives?

**Enterprise Business:**
- I saw that the enterprise segment has faced some challenges. How does that impact engineering priorities or resource allocation?

## Culture & Growth

**Engineering Culture:**
- How would you describe the engineering culture at Coursera today?
- What does success look like for an Engineering Manager in the first 6-12 months?
- What opportunities exist for Engineering Managers to grow into senior leadership roles?

**Work Environment:**
- What's the typical work-life balance like for Engineering Managers?
- How does Coursera support remote-first teams? Are there expectations for travel or in-person collaboration?

## Process

**Timeline:**
- What's the typical interview process and timeline for this role?
- Who would I be meeting with in subsequent rounds?
