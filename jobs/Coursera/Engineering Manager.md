# Engineering Manager at Coursera

**Location:** Canada

**Salary:** CAD $158,400–$198,000 (base salary range)

---

## About Coursera

Founded in 2012 by Stanford professors, Coursera serves "over 190 million learners and 375+ university and industry partners" across AI, data science, technology, and business education. The company operates as a Delaware public benefit corporation and certified B Corp.

---

## Why Join

Coursera seeks "inventors, innovators, and lifelong learners ready to shape the future of education." The culture emphasizes moving rapidly, simplifying processes, and measuring impact. The organization supports distributed work arrangements globally.

---

## Team Overview: Skills and Credentials

This team "builds the foundational engine that connects Coursera's content to real-world career outcomes." Key focus areas include:

- **Core Skills Taxonomy & Mapping:** Creating structured ontologies of skills, occupations, and career levels
- **Skill Progression & Signaling:** Capturing skill signals to provide learner insights
- **Scalable Architecture:** Ensuring long-term platform reliability and evolution

---

## Core Responsibilities

- **Team Building:** Partner across Product, Design, ML, Data Science, and Teaching teams

- **Hiring:** Identify gaps and build high-performing teams
- **Technical Leadership:** Provide hands-on guidance on systems design and architecture
- **Product Enablement:** Translate product needs into actionable engineering roadmaps
