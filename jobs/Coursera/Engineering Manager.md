## Company

Coursera was founded in 2012 by Stanford professors Andrew Ng and Daphne Koller to make world-class learning accessible globally. Today serves over 190 million learners and 375+ university and industry partners with online learning in AI, data science, technology, and business. Delaware public benefit corporation and Certified B Corp driven by the belief that learning can transform lives. Globally distributed team with flexible work options including remote, hubs, or co-working spaces. Virtual hiring and onboarding process enables working from anywhere. The Skills and Credentials team builds the foundational engine that defines, maps, and tracks skills across the entire learning journey, creating scalable systems for skill-based learning, credentialing, and career pathways.
