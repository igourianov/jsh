## Company

Fullscript is a health technology company founded in 2011 providing a platform for healthcare practitioners to access supplements, lab interpretations, patient analytics and education tools. Over 125,000 practitioners and 10 million patients use the platform for clinical insights and care plan adherence. The company is evolving from a transactional practitioner/patient platform toward "Care Mode," a longitudinal, person-centric model connecting patients and care teams between visits. 501-1,000 employees, remote-friendly "Wherever You Work Well" model.
