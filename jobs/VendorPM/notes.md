# VendorPM - Recruiter Call Notes

## Questions for Calyptus (Mar 25)

1. **What is the situation with current VP/Eng Apurav Chauhan?** Is this a replacement, or is the role being created alongside the existing position? What prompted the search?

2. **Why is a 10-15 person tech team using tools like GraphQL and NestJS?** These are typically scaling-stage choices. What's the platform complexity that justifies this stack, and is there architectural debt from adopting them too early?

3. **What's the nature of the AI product integration?** Is "agentic procurement" a real product initiative with customers asking for it, or a strategic vision the team hasn't started building yet? How far along is the AI work today?

4. **What does the reporting structure look like?** The JD says reporting to the COO. What is the CTO's role and scope relative to this VP/Eng position? Glassdoor reviews reference CTO-related friction. How are technical decisions arbitrated?

5. **How autonomous is the engineering org?** Several public reviews describe micromanagement and weekly priority shifts from leadership. What does the product-engineering collaboration actually look like day to day? Who owns the roadmap?

6. **What's the funding and runway situation?** Series A closed in August 2022. No Series B in 3.5 years. Is the company approaching profitability, or is a new round being planned? What's the burn rate context?

7. **What does "hybrid with mostly remote" mean in practice?** How often is in-person presence expected in the Toronto office? Is there flexibility for someone based outside the GTA?

8. **What does the engineering team structure look like today?** Are engineers organized by feature, by layer (frontend/backend), or something else? The JD mentions shifting toward "full-stack ownership." What's the current state they're shifting from?

9. **What are the near-term deliverables for this role?** What does success look like in the first 90 days? Is the priority cleaning up tech/process debt, shipping the AI roadmap, or stabilizing the team?

10. **What's the equity structure?** Options or shares, vesting schedule, strike price context. At Series A stage with no B round in sight, what's the realistic liquidity timeline?
