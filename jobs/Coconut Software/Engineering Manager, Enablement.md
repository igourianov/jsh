Engineering Manager leading one team in the Enablement Engineering pillar, which builds and runs infrastructure, data, tooling, service standards and customer implementation capabilities for every product team and some Enterprise clients. Used by 200+ banks and credit unions including RBC, Mountain America Credit Union and M&T Bank. 51-200 employees.
