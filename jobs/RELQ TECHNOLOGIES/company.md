# RELQ TECHNOLOGIES

- **Company Type:** IT Services
- **Stage:** Startup (small private consultancy)
- **Size:** 51-100 employees (51-200 on LinkedIn)
- **Remote Policy:** Posting is remote (Canada). Company-wide policy not found.

## Quick Take
- Small IT services and QA/testing consultancy with a US shell (Odessa, Florida) and, per Glassdoor, delivery staff in India (Chennai, Bangalore). Client project work, not own product.
- Thin public footprint: no engineering blog, no funding or news coverage, no GitHub organization found.
- Glassdoor is mixed and sample is tiny (about 7-10 reviews). The negative reviews describe a target-driven, high-pressure delivery culture. Canadian-site reviews are more positive.
- Key concern: a Manager role in a body-shop style consultancy with no stated team size, reporting line or compensation.

## Milestones
- RELQ TECHNOLOGIES LLC registered 2022, Odessa, Florida (D&B, Crunchbase listings).
- The company's own marketing claims "over a decade of experience". This likely traces to the older, separate RelQ Software Pvt Ltd (Bangalore, founded 1997, software testing, acquired by EDS in 2007 and later part of Mphasis/HP). The link between the two was not confirmed.
- No acquisitions, funding rounds, layoffs or leadership changes reported in the past 5 years.
- Recent postings include an SRE Lead/Architect, so it is hiring.

## Company & Product
Provides software development, testing, automation and digital transformation services to startups, mid-size companies and enterprises. Tagline "Reliable Quality Services". Quality engineering and test automation are the historical core. No own product. The posting describes the Manager role as leading cross-functional teams that build products from scratch, which in this model means client engagements.

## Engineering Culture
- No engineering blog, open source or conference presence found.
- The posting emphasizes shift-left quality, test automation, DevOps/CI/CD, platform engineering and AI-assisted development. Consistent with a QA-rooted firm moving into development services.
- Delivery model is client-driven, so practices likely depend on each client engagement.

## Tech Stack
Not published. Posting mentions cloud platforms, CI/CD, test automation and AI-assisted development tools without naming any. Not verified.

## Team Health
- Glassdoor: 3.5 overall on 10 ratings, 57% recommend. Canadian Glassdoor view: 4.1 on 6 reviews.
- Positive: approachable management, flexibility, work-life balance, diverse projects.
- Negative: high-pressure target-based environment, pay below market, limited holidays and benefits, managers pinging hourly for updates and asking staff to give clients inflated commitments, difficult exit.
- Reviews appear to come mostly from India delivery staff and freshers. Relevance to a Canadian remote manager role is uncertain.

## Business Stability
- Private, no disclosed funding or revenue. Not verified beyond directory listings.
- Small headcount and client concentration are unknown. A services firm's stability depends on its client contracts.
- No layoff reports found.

## Red Flags
- Posting omits team size, compensation, reporting line and employment type.
- Glassdoor themes of hourly status pings and over-committing to clients point to a pressured delivery culture.
- Unclear corporate identity and history (2022 LLC vs claimed decade-plus pedigree).
- Very small review sample, so the signal is weak either way.
- Canadian remote hire at a Florida/India-staffed firm: unclear whether employment is permanent, contractor or via a Canadian entity.
