Their flagship product, Roomvo, lets consumers preview flooring, rugs and other décor products in their own rooms before purchasing. They also offer QFloors, an ERP system for the flooring industry. Customers span 80+ countries and include Fortune 500 companies like Home Depot alongside local retailers. The company has 300+ employees, serves 180+ countries with 1B+ products viewed.
