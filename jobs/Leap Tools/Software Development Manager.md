## Company Overview

Leap Tools develops advanced solutions for the interior décor industry, serving Fortune 500 companies like Home Depot and retailers globally across 80+ countries. The company has been recognized as one of Deloitte's fastest-growing tech companies multiple years running.

## Product Focus

The company creates technology enabling customers to "preview products in your own room before you buy them" through AR visualization capabilities, demonstrated via their rug preview demo.

## Role Responsibilities

The position involves leading a ~12-person consumer-focused engineering team while:
- Driving development of mobile-first web applications serving millions of shoppers
- Collaborating with product and design teams on requirements and user experiences
- Managing concurrent workstreams with effective prioritization
- Establishing engineering practices supporting iteration, scalability, and reliability
- Providing technical guidance on architectural decisions
- Developing engineers through mentorship and coaching
- Recruiting and onboarding top talent
