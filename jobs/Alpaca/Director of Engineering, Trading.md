## Company

Alpaca is a US-headquartered, self-clearing broker-dealer and brokerage-infrastructure provider offering institutional-grade APIs for stocks, ETFs, options, crypto, fixed income and 24/5 trading. It powers over 9 million brokerage accounts across hundreds of fintechs and financial institutions in 40+ countries, with clients including broker-dealers, investment advisors, wealth managers, hedge funds and crypto exchanges.

The company has raised over $320M (Series D) from investors including Portage Ventures, Spark Capital, Tribe Capital, Social Leverage, Horizons Ventures, SBI Group, Derayah Financial, Elefund and Y Combinator. It is a globally distributed team of 380+ members across the USA, Canada, Japan, Hungary, Nigeria, Brazil, the UK and beyond, with a strong open-source and developer-community focus.
