## Company

Rocket Doctor is a digital health platform and marketplace focused on removing barriers to quality, accessible, and cost-effective healthcare. Their proprietary software enables doctors to run independent practices in virtual and hybrid care models, serving patients in rural and remote communities across Canada and on Medicaid/Medicare in the United States. The platform leverages LLMs, AI/ML, and wireless medical devices. Since launch, over 400 MDs have provided care through more than 700,000 patient visits across North America. Rocket Doctor recently joined Rocket Doctor AI (formerly Treatment.com AI), becoming publicly traded in Spring 2025, and is expanding rapidly.
