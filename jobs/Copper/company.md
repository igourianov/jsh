# Copper

- **Company Type:** SaaS / CRM Platform
- **Stage:** Scale-up (Series C)
- **Size:** ~300 employees
- **Remote Policy:** Remote-first, distributed across UK, Canada and US

## Quick Take
- Google-recommended CRM focused on relationship-centric, service-based businesses. Niche player in a crowded market
- New CEO (Steve Holm, Aug 2023) is driving a product-led turnaround, shifting focus from sales/marketing to product innovation
- Ruby on Rails / Ember.js stack with Kubernetes on AWS. Engineering teams are distributed and remote-friendly

## Milestones
- 2013-2014: Founded as ProsperWorks in San Francisco by Jon Lee
- 2018: Rebranded from ProsperWorks to Copper. Had raised $77M+ by this point, 12,000 customers in 110+ countries
- 2023 (Aug): Steve Holm returns as CEO (previously at Podium, Dropbox, Zendesk Sell). Company pivots toward product-led growth
- 2026 (Mar): Partnership expansion with Associate Owners Group (AOG). Wyndham Hudson promoted to COO
- Total funding: $99.5M from True Ventures, Norwest and Next World Capital

## Company & Product
Copper is a CRM built specifically for Google Workspace. It is the only CRM with "Recommended for Google Workspace" status. The platform integrates directly with Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive, eliminating manual data entry.

The product serves 30,000+ companies, primarily in professional services: agencies, consulting firms, financial services, commercial production, media and construction. Under Steve Holm's leadership, the company is narrowing its focus to be "mission-critical for relationship-centric, service-based businesses" rather than competing broadly with Salesforce or HubSpot.

Key product areas: contact/deal management, pipeline tracking, project management, email marketing, workflow automation, reporting and a Chrome extension.

## Engineering Culture
Copper's engineering organization is distributed across the UK, Canada and the US. Job postings emphasize agile processes, rapid iteration and cross-functional collaboration with Product and Design.

The Engineering Manager role description highlights: scope clarification, action-oriented culture, mentoring through regular feedback, and partnering on architecture decisions with Technical Leads.

No dedicated engineering blog was found. The company has a GitHub organization (github.com/ProsperWorks) with 53 repositories, though most show minimal recent activity. Notable open-source work includes Redis tooling (redis-ick for priority queues) and CRM SDKs in TypeScript.

## Tech Stack
- **Backend:** Ruby on Rails, Ruby
- **Frontend:** Ember.js, JavaScript
- **Infrastructure:** Kubernetes, AWS
- **Data:** Redis (with custom tooling), PostgreSQL (inferred from pgbouncer buildpack)
- **Other:** Resque (queuing), caching, search indexing tools, CI/CD pipelines
- **Deployment:** SaaS, multi-tenant cloud, mobile apps (iOS/Android)

## Team Health
- **Glassdoor:** 4.3/5 stars (85 reviews). 85% would recommend to a friend. Compensation rated 4.1/5
- **Pros:** Smart, helpful colleagues. Low-pressure environment. Collaborative culture. Free meals 3x/day with snacks. Described as "clear mission, A+ team"
- **Cons:** Some reviews mention weak product offering with "nothing new on the horizon." Concerns about customer retention. Reports of inflated Glassdoor metrics with pressure to write favorable reviews
- **Compensation:** Engineering Manager (Canada, remote): C$180K-C$195K. Unlimited vacation, stock options, parental leave top-ups, L&D programs, internet/phone allowance

## Business Stability
Copper has raised $99.5M across Series A-C from reputable investors (True Ventures, Norwest, Next World Capital). The company is privately held with no public IPO plans evident.

The CEO change in 2023 signals a strategic reset. The company is betting on product differentiation in the professional services niche rather than competing head-to-head with larger CRM players. Recent partnership expansion with AOG (March 2026) and a COO promotion suggest operational momentum.

No layoffs reported at Copper CRM in 2024-2026. (Note: a different company called "Copper" in crypto custody had layoffs in 2023. Unrelated.)

## Red Flags
- **Product concerns:** Multiple Glassdoor reviews mention a weak product with limited innovation and poor customer retention. This is a meaningful signal for an engineering leader joining
- **Glassdoor integrity:** Reports of pressure on employees to write favorable Glassdoor reviews undermine confidence in the 4.3 rating
- **Open source stagnation:** Most GitHub repos show minimal activity, with several archived. Not a sign of a vibrant engineering culture
- **Crowded market:** CRM is an extremely competitive space. Copper's niche strategy (Google Workspace + professional services) is reasonable but carries concentration risk
- **Funding plateau:** No new funding rounds reported since Series C ($99.5M total). At 300 employees, runway and path to profitability are unclear
