# MEDvidi

- **Company Type:** Telehealth Platform / HealthTech
- **Stage:** Scale-up (profitable, $26M ARR)
- **Size:** 180-300 employees (sources vary; ~50 clinical providers, rest ops/tech)
- **Remote Policy:** Remote-first (US-based). Clinical and corporate roles are remote.

## Quick Take
- Mental health telehealth platform founded in 2018, profitable with 105% YoY growth and $26M ARR
- Aggressively pivoting toward AI with dedicated medvidi.ai product line (AI Clinical Assistant, AI Prescriber planned for 2027)
- Small engineering team (outsourced development via Smart-IT). Compensation rated below average on Glassdoor (3.4/5). Mixed reviews with "unprofessional" and "mismanaged" flags alongside positive ones.

## Milestones
- **2015-2017:** Founder Vasili Razhnou launches MdBerry, Mango Clinic, EzCare Medical Clinic (healthcare entrepreneur background)
- **2018:** MEDvidi founded as a cloud-based mental health platform after operating traditional clinics in San Francisco and Miami
- **2019:** Company officially incorporated. Seed funding secured.
- **2023:** Grew to ~300 employees. Expanded to all 50 US states.
- **2024:** Total funding reached $3.83M (Seed VC - II). Investors: Verras Capital, TMT Investments, AltaIR Capital, Garage Syndicate, Igor Shoifot.
- **2025 (Sep):** Launched AI-powered healthcare solutions (AI Clinician, AI Receptionist) under medvidi.ai brand
- **2026 (Jan):** Launched AI Clinical Assistant targeting mental health workforce shortage. Handles ~10,000 visits/month. Reached $26M ARR with profitability.

## Company & Product
MEDvidi is a US-based telehealth platform for adult mental health treatment (ADHD, anxiety, depression, insomnia, OCD, PTSD, obesity). Board-certified providers (MDs, DOs, NPs, PAs) deliver video/audio consultations, prescribe medications (including controlled substances), and create personalized treatment plans.

The company does not accept insurance. It targets a $600M serviceable market within the $69B US behavioral health sector.

A separate entity, medvidi.ai, focuses on AI products for clinical workflows: AI Chart Reviewer (validates charts for SOP adherence), AI Chart Generator (auto-populates charts during visits), and a planned AI Prescriber (autonomous follow-up visits, targeting 2027, pending FDA clearance). The AI assistant reportedly saves clinicians ~8 hours/week and enables 100% chart review coverage vs. the industry standard ~5%.

## Business Stability
- **Profitable** at $26M ARR with 105% YoY growth. This is notable for a company with only $3.83M in total funding.
- Capital-efficient business model (low burn relative to revenue).
- AI pivot (medvidi.ai) positions the company for B2B expansion into health systems and provider groups.
- No layoffs or restructuring reported.
- Targeting the projected 21,000-psychiatrist shortage by 2030 as a market tailwind.

## Red Flags
- **Outsourced engineering history.** Core platform was built by an external dev shop. Unclear how much internal engineering talent exists vs. contractors.
- **Low compensation ratings** (3.4/5 on Glassdoor). For an Engineering Manager role, this is worth probing.
- **Negative Glassdoor reviews** with strong language ("Unprofessional", "Incredibly mismanaged") suggest organizational maturity issues.
- **Small total funding** ($3.83M) for a company of this size. While profitable, this limits runway for aggressive hiring or R&D.
- **Founder background:** Multiple ventures in quick succession (MdBerry, Mango Clinic, EzCare, MEDvidi) in 3 years. Pattern could indicate serial pivoting.
- **No insurance accepted.** Business model depends entirely on out-of-pocket patients, which could limit growth ceiling.
- **jQuery in the stack** alongside Angular suggests legacy code or inconsistent technical standards.
