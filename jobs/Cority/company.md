# Cority

- **Company Type:** SaaS / Platform (EHS+ - Environmental, Health, Safety, Quality, Sustainability)
- **Stage:** Private equity-owned, mature scale-up
- **Size:** ~500-1,000 employees (estimates range 850-1,000), Toronto HQ, 8 office locations
- **Remote Policy:** Hybrid/remote mix reported by employees; no official published policy found, some Glassdoor reviews mention "limited remote work flexibility"

## Quick Take
- Long-lived company (founded 1985 as Medgate) with steady growth-by-acquisition strategy, now under Thoma Bravo ownership since 2019
- Actively pushing into AI (Cortex AI, Google Gemini integration announced Dec 2025) - could mean interesting greenfield work, or bolt-on AI hype over a legacy platform
- Glassdoor signals notable turnover and compensation concerns - worth probing team stability and comp bands directly in interviews

## Milestones
- 1985: Founded as Medgate, focused on occupational health records
- Rebranded to Cority following a series of acquisitions
- May 2019: Majority stake acquired by Thoma Bravo (private equity)
- Continued acquisitions expanding into environmental, ESG/sustainability, and industrial hygiene (including Greenstone)
- January 2025: Acquired Meddbase (cloud-native occupational medicine / enterprise medical practice management software)
- December 2025: Unveiled Cortex AI - suite of embedded AI agents (13 agents across 30+ EHS use cases); announced collaboration with Google to integrate Gemini models
- April 2026: Appointed Mike Jackson as SVP of Enterprise Sales
- Recognized by Verdantix as a Leader in Green Quadrant reports for EHS software and ESG/sustainability reporting software (2025)

## Company & Product
Cority is an EHS (Environmental, Health, Safety) SaaS provider serving 1,500+ organizations worldwide, primarily large enterprises in pharmaceuticals, healthcare, manufacturing, chemicals and energy. Its flagship platform, CorityOne, is positioned as a "Converged EHS+" platform unifying safety, health, environmental, quality and sustainability management with embedded AI (Cortex AI) layered across workflows. The company has grown substantially through acquisition (Meddbase, Greenstone, and others) rather than organic product development alone, which is typical of PE-backed roll-up strategies.

## Engineering Culture
No public engineering blog, open-source presence, or technical writing was found - the company blog (cority.com/blog) is customer/marketing-facing (AI in EHS use cases, webinars, industry content), not an engineering blog. This is a notable gap for evaluating engineering practices and suggests limited public engineering brand or thought leadership. Glassdoor reviews mention supportive, low-politics teams with a degree of autonomy, but also vague leadership direction from C-suite/VP level.

## Tech Stack
Public tech stack data is sparse and mostly reflects marketing/web tooling (MadCap Flare, PHP, Google Fonts API, GenerateBlocks, Workiva) rather than the core product stack. No architecture details, cloud provider, or backend language/framework information was found via public sources - this will need to be asked directly in interviews, especially given the platform's growth through multiple acquisitions (likely a patchwork of legacy codebases from Medgate, Greenstone, Meddbase, etc., not yet fully unified).

## Team Health
- Glassdoor: 3.2/5 overall rating (193 reviews), 46% would recommend to a friend
- Positives: good work-life balance/flexibility for some roles, no-politics culture reported, supportive and helpful teams, autonomy to self-lead
- Negatives: pay below industry standards, minimal salary increases (cost-of-living only, no real career-long growth path), outdated HR policies (minimal PTO, health insurance, L&D budget), limited remote flexibility per some reviewers
- Recurring turnover complaints: frequent departures of legacy knowledge holders, slow ramp-up for new hires, difficulty maintaining momentum - a real concern for engineering leadership stability and institutional knowledge retention
- A Glassdoor review describes a layoff of about a dozen people with no notice, including some 20+ year employees; compensation/benefits ratings dropped ~10% over the following 12 months. No confirmed date found, and no mainstream tech-layoff tracker mentions Cority by name, suggesting it was small-scale and not widely reported

## Business Stability
PE-backed (Thoma Bravo, majority stake since 2019) with additional backing from Teralys Capital; total raised ~$240M. The company continues to acquire (Meddbase Jan 2025) and invest in product (Cortex AI launch, Google Gemini partnership Dec 2025), signaling active growth investment rather than contraction. No layoff news found in 2025-2026 searches. As a PE roll-up, the usual risks apply: pressure for margin/efficiency, potential for further acquisitions creating integration overhead, and typical PE exit-timeline considerations (6-8 years post-acquisition, i.e. now approaching the window where a sale or recapitalization becomes more likely).

## Red Flags
- No visible engineering blog or public technical presence - hard to independently assess engineering quality/practices
- Glassdoor turnover and "legacy knowledge holder departure" pattern suggests unstable engineering teams and possible tech debt from serial acquisitions
- Compensation reviews indicate below-market pay with minimal growth trajectory
- Multiple acquisitions (Medgate legacy, Greenstone, Meddbase) raise likelihood of fragmented tech stack/integration debt - probe directly on platform unification status
- PE ownership since 2019 nearing typical hold-period exit window - ask about ownership stability/exit plans if relevant to role longevity
