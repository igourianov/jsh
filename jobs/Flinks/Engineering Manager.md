## Company

Flinks is a Canadian embedded finance platform and leader in Open Finance in Canada since 2016. The company provides financial data connectivity, intelligence, and payments infrastructure enabling businesses to build financial experiences. The platform connects to 15,000+ financial institutions across North America, powers 1M+ monthly connections, and provides 4,500+ real-time financial insights. Customers use Flinks to streamline onboarding, verify income, assess credit risk, and enable faster payment experiences. The company serves lending, fintech, banking, insurance, and wealth management sectors. Engineering organization is approximately 50-150 people with product squads covering Data In, Data Intelligence, Payments, and Professional Services value streams.
