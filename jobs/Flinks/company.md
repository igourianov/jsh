# Flinks

- **Company Type:** Platform / Embedded Finance / Fintech Infrastructure
- **Stage:** Scale-up (majority-owned by National Bank of Canada)
- **Size:** ~135 employees
- **Remote Policy:** Fully remote (Canada-wide)
- **HQ:** Montreal, Quebec, Canada
- **Founded:** 2016 (launched April 2017)

## Quick Take
- Well-positioned for Canada's 2026 open banking mandate. Already has live open banking integrations with National Bank, EQ Bank and others.
- National Bank's 80% ownership provides financial stability but may limit startup agility and independence.
- Glassdoor reviews flag transparency and communication issues around personnel decisions, leading to turnover.

## Milestones
- **2016** - Founded by Yves-Gabriel Leboeuf in Montreal
- **2017** - Launched as Canada's first dedicated financial data connectivity provider
- **2020** - Series A of $16.2M CAD led by NAventures (National Bank's VC arm), total funding to $19M
- **2021** - National Bank of Canada committed $103M CAD ($73M secondary + $30M growth capital) for 80% preferred share equity
- **2024** - Launched "Guaranteed EFT" for real-time, risk-free account funding
- **2025** - Launched Open Banking Environment (OBE) with National Bank; Fintech Forum participation; Global Recognition Award for Innovation
- **2026** - Canada's Open Banking Phase 1 (read access) launching. Flinks is a key infrastructure player.

## Company & Product
Flinks is an embedded finance platform that unifies data connectivity, intelligence and payments through APIs and dashboards. Core products:

- **Connect** - Bank account connectivity across 15,000+ North American financial institutions (>95% connection rate, 1M+ monthly connections)
- **Upload** - Document verification with real-time fraud detection for bank statements and void cheques
- **Enrich** - Financial data analytics using 4,500+ attributes for lending, underwriting and cash flow analysis
- **Pay** - Unified payment platform for account validation, funding, loan repayments and pre-authorized debit
- **Outbound** - Open Banking API for secure data sharing with fintech partners

Major clients include Wealthsimple, EQ Bank, Stripe, National Bank of Canada, Propel Holdings and Peoples Group. The company positions itself as the Canadian competitor to Plaid.

## Engineering Culture
- No public engineering blog. Company blog is business/industry-focused, not technical.
- GitHub org (flinkstech) has 18 public repos, mostly small utilities. Go-heavy open source contributions (Freshdesk client, k6 logging extension, Selenium bindings for Go). Low community engagement (1-3 stars per repo).
- Careers page emphasizes "Challenges Over Perks" and an intrapreneurial environment.
- Developer challenge available at challenge.flinks.com.
- Practices mentioned: CQRS, Actor Model, Domain-Driven Design.

## Tech Stack
- **Backend:** .NET Core, C#, Go
- **Architecture:** Microservices, CQRS, Actor Model, Domain-Driven Design
- **Cloud:** GCP (primary), Azure (serverless functions)
- **Infrastructure:** Docker, Kubernetes
- **AI/ML:** Machine learning, NLP, neural networks (for data enrichment)
- **Data:** Distributed data architecture
- **Testing:** k6 for load testing, Selenium for browser automation
- **Monitoring:** Logz.io for log aggregation
- **Other:** Swagger/OpenAPI, Echo (Go web framework)

## Business Stability
- **Funding:** ~$122M CAD total raised. National Bank of Canada owns 80% preferred share equity.
- **Revenue:** ~$29.8M (estimated)
- **Tailwinds:** Canada's open banking mandate (Phase 1 launching 2026) is a major growth catalyst. Flinks is already the leading Canadian open banking infrastructure provider with live integrations.
- **Strategic position:** National Bank backing provides financial security and a built-in enterprise client. Reduced screen scraping at National Bank by 90% since introducing their API.
- **Risk:** Heavy dependence on a single majority owner. Regulatory timeline shifts could impact growth trajectory.

## Red Flags
- Transparency issues around firing decisions and management changes are a recurring Glassdoor theme, leading to turnover
- Limited career growth opportunities within the engineering organization
- Compensation described as below industry standards for the demands of the role
- No public engineering blog or significant open source presence suggests limited investment in engineering brand
- 80% ownership by National Bank may create a "corporate subsidiary" dynamic rather than a true startup/scale-up culture
