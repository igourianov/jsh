## Summary

Senior Engineering Manager for NerdWallet's Platform Systems team, responsible for the foundational infrastructure powering NerdWallet's consumer experience: content platform, headless WordPress CMS, product data platform, partner ingestion pipelines, publishing and click-tracking, and GraphQL gateway. The team is modernizing legacy services into scalable, decoupled systems. Role reports to Director of Engineering.

- Lead and develop the Platform Systems engineering team
- Partner with Product and cross-functional stakeholders to shape and execute the platform roadmap
- Drive modernization of legacy services toward scalable, headless, API-first architecture
- Own technical quality, team health, and day-to-day delivery execution
- Contribute to architectural decisions ensuring scalability, reliability, security, and compliance
- Manage sprint planning, timelines, and delivery commitments for high-traffic, mission-critical systems
- Proactively identify and address technical risks, dependencies, and delivery challenges
- Support hiring, onboarding, performance management, and career development

## Company

NerdWallet (NASDAQ: NRDS) is a personal finance platform helping consumers and SMBs make financial decisions with clarity. Products span credit cards, mortgages, insurance, investing, and banking comparisons. The platform serves millions of users across web and mobile, with significant emphasis on SEO-driven content and high-traffic data pipelines. NerdWallet is a remote-first company and has been recognized for workplace culture by Inc., Fortune and Fast Company. Canada team is fully remote, excluding Quebec.
