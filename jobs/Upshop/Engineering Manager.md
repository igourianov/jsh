## Company

Upshop is a SaaS provider of total store operations software for food retailers, covering forecasting, ordering, production and inventory optimization across Fresh, Center, DSD and eCommerce departments. The platform consolidates several acquired products: FreshIQ, ShopperKit, Date Check Pro and Itasca Retail's Magic Inventory Intelligence.

Over 30 years in the market. Claims 450+ retailers and 50,000+ stores globally in the job posting, while the company description on the same page says 150+ retail chains and 30,000+ stores across 9 countries and 3 continents. 201-500 employees.

Customer value proposition is increased sales, reduced shrink, improved food safety and labour efficiency for grocery chains.
