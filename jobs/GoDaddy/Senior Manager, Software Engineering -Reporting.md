## Summary

Senior Engineering Manager for GoDaddy's Commerce Reporting team, the data backbone of the Commerce platform serving hundreds of thousands of merchants. The team owns all reporting capabilities across Commerce Home, Mobile and Terminal experiences. The primary mandate is a greenfield rebuild of the next-generation reporting platform: data warehousing infrastructure, services layer and APIs. Role is 60% technical leadership (architecture, technical decisions, stack direction) and 40% people and stakeholder management across 6 engineers.

- Lead architecture and development of next-gen Commerce reporting platform

- Partner with Commerce Home, Mobile, Terminal and Platform/Infrastructure teams
- Manage delivery across multiple concurrent workstreams
- Grow team through mentorship, hiring and process improvements

## Company

GoDaddy is a publicly traded web services company serving 20+ million customers worldwide, primarily small and medium businesses. Its core products include domain registration, website building and hosting, email, and — increasingly — commerce tools for online and in-person selling. The Commerce platform powers merchant storefronts, payment processing, payouts, and the reporting and analytics merchants use to run their businesses. The Reporting team is a platform team within Commerce, supplying data infrastructure and APIs consumed by multiple merchant-facing surfaces.
