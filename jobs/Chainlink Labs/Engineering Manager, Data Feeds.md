## Company

Chainlink is the industry-standard oracle platform connecting blockchains to real-world data, enabling decentralized finance (DeFi) and institutional tokenized asset use cases. Its stack provides data, interoperability, compliance and privacy infrastructure used by major financial institutions (Swift, Euroclear, Mastercard, UBS) and DeFi protocols (Aave, Lido, GMX). Data Feeds is one of its flagship products, delivering trusted price/data feeds that underpin widely-used DeFi protocols. Revenue from enterprise adoption is converted to LINK tokens held in a strategic reserve.
