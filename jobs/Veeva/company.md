# Company Research: Veeva Systems

**Company Type:** SaaS / Industry Cloud \
**Stage:** Public (NYSE: VEEV) \
**Remote Policy:** Remote-first (Work Anywhere within 2hr timezone)

## Quick Take

- Dominant player in life sciences cloud software with $3B+ revenue and strong market position (top 20 pharma companies as customers)
- Engineering culture reviews are mixed (3.7/5) - good autonomy but concerns about micromanagement and below-market compensation
- Stock down 25% from 2025 highs; growth slowing from 16% to projected 10.8% - transition from hypergrowth to mature phase

## Company & Product

Veeva Systems is the leading cloud software provider for the global life sciences industry. Founded in 2007, the company builds industry-specific cloud applications, data solutions, and business consulting services for pharmaceutical and biotech companies. Core products include:

- **Veeva Vault** - Content management, quality, regulatory, and clinical operations
- **Veeva CRM** - Customer relationship management for life sciences sales teams
- **Veeva Data Cloud** - Reference data and analytics for life sciences

The company serves Fortune 500 pharma companies globally, with 115 customers live on Vault CRM including the top 20 pharmaceutical companies. Veeva became the first public company to convert to a Public Benefit Corporation in 2021, emphasizing mission-driven culture.

**Size:** ~7,300 employees globally (grew 1.7% YoY). Product hubs in Pleasanton (HQ), Columbus, Boston, Kansas City, NYC, Raleigh, and Toronto. Toronto is a growing hub focused on product development.

## Company History

**Founded:** January 2007 in Pleasanton, California by Peter Gassner (CEO), Matt Wallach, Doug Ostler, and Mitch Wallace. Originally named "Verticals onDemand, Inc." - rebranded to Veeva Systems in April 2009.

**Founders' background:** Gassner and team came from Siebel Systems (CRM leader acquired by Oracle), with additional experience at Salesforce and PeopleSoft. Recognized the opportunity for industry-specific cloud software in life sciences.

**Key milestones:**
- **2007** - Company founded; pioneered vertical SaaS model
- **2008** - Series A funding ($4M from Emergence Capital); Veeva CRM launched on Salesforce platform
- **2011** - Veeva Vault content management system introduced
- **2013** - IPO on NYSE (ticker: VEEV)
- **2021** - Became first public company to convert to Public Benefit Corporation; surpassed $2B revenue
- **2023** - Veeva CRM on Salesforce entered "stability mode" (final release Dec 2023, supported until Sept 2030); first Vault CRM customer win
- **2024** - Vault CRM general availability (April); Veeva Compass Suite launched as alternative to legacy data products
- **2025** - Major partnerships: Amgen (clinical trials), IQVIA (clinical/commercial collaboration), AWS (expanded 10-year relationship), Boehringer Ingelheim ("One Medicine Platform")

**Acquisitions:** Conservative acquirer - only 6 total acquisitions since founding. Most recent: Veracity Logic (clinical trial randomization) in December 2021. Peak year was 2015 with 3 acquisitions.

## Engineering Culture

**Pragmatic approach:** Veeva values "autonomy over alignment" - teams make decisions independently to ship products faster. Engineering department has 50% positive reviews, best among all departments.

**No dedicated engineering blog:** The company has product/industry blogs but no public technical engineering blog sharing architecture or practices.

**Open source:** Veeva maintains 24 GitHub repositories, primarily SDK tools and API libraries for their platform:
- VAPIL (Vault API Library) - Java REST client
- Vault Toolbox - Developer tools with Monaco editor
- Vault SDK sample projects
- CRM MyInsights examples

**Methodologies:** Agile environment with emphasis on clean code, OOP principles, and design patterns.

## Tech Stack & Architecture

**Primary stack:**
- **Languages:** Java (primary), Python, PHP
- **Frameworks:** Spring Boot, Spring MVC
- **Databases:** MySQL, PostgreSQL, DynamoDB (SQL and NoSQL)
- **ORM:** Hibernate/JPA
- **Cloud:** AWS (primary) - IAM, Cognito
- **Security:** HashiCorp Vault, Metasploit, Cortex XSOAR

**Architecture:** Full-stack distributed cloud infrastructure for cloud-native applications. Enterprise SaaS platform serving life sciences industry with strict compliance requirements.

**Mobile:** iOS and Android development for field sales applications.

## Product & Market Position

**Market leader:** Dominant position in life sciences vertical cloud. Customers include top 20 global pharma companies.

**Business model:** Subscription SaaS with professional services. Strong recurring revenue base.

**Competitors:** Salesforce Health Cloud, IQVIA, Oracle Health Sciences, but Veeva has strong industry-specific moat.

**Innovation:** Investing in Veeva AI for industry-specific applications. Recent focus on Vault CRM migration.

## Engineering Team Health

**Glassdoor ratings:**
- Overall: 3.6/5
- Software Engineers: 3.7/5 (105 reviews)
- Work-life balance: 3.2-3.4/5
- Compensation: 3.0-3.5/5
- 67% of engineers recommend to a friend (+16% in past 12 months)

**Pros mentioned:**
- Good engineering culture and autonomy
- Remote work flexibility with full WFH equipment provided
- Noble mission in healthcare/life sciences
- Smart, high-performing teammates
- More holidays than typical US companies ("Veeva break")
- Good benefits (HSA match, fitness reimbursement, 12 weeks paid parental leave)
- Company stock performing well

**Cons mentioned:**
- Fast-paced, can lead to burnout
- Below-market compensation for some roles
- Management becoming more micromanagement-heavy (twice-weekly 4:30pm check-ins)
- Unclear growth paths
- Team-dependent work-life balance
- PTO not particularly generous

## Business Stability

**Revenue:** $3.08B TTM (up 16% YoY). Q3 FY2026 revenue $811M with $365M non-GAAP operating income.

**Stock:** NYSE: VEEV. Down 25% from October 2025 highs ($306 to ~$232). Growth slowing from 16% to projected 10.8%.

**Growth trajectory:** Transitioning from hypergrowth to mature SaaS. Still profitable and growing, but investor concerns about deceleration.

**Layoffs:** Officially, Veeva announced in 2023 no layoffs planned for 3 years. However, anonymous claims on TheLayoff.com allege quiet layoffs and PIPs to avoid severance. Difficult to verify.

**Public Benefit Corp:** First public company to convert to PBC status. 1% charitable giving program. No non-compete agreements.

## Concerns for Engineering Managers

- **Compensation below market:** Multiple sources cite below-average pay for engineering roles. May struggle to attract/retain top talent.
- **Micromanagement trends:** Recent reviews mention increased scrutiny, mandatory check-ins, and deadline pressure - could indicate cultural shift.
- **Growth deceleration:** Slowing revenue growth may lead to hiring freezes, reduced investment, or eventual cost-cutting despite official "no layoffs" stance.
- **Quiet layoffs allegations:** Unverified claims of PIPs and quiet terminations rather than formal RIFs. Worth asking about turnover in interviews.
- **Java requirement:** Role explicitly requires 5+ years Java - significant tech stack mismatch for .NET-focused candidates.
- **Team-dependent culture:** Work-life balance and management quality vary significantly by team.
