## Summary

Technical Lead Manager for the Sodatone team, an internal analytics platform that uses AI and machine learning to identify emerging music talent. The platform aggregates data from streaming services and social media to support A&R teams in talent scouting and artist development. This is a hands-on role involving designing and implementing greenfield projects alongside the team.

- **Coding:** 30% hands-on coding, code reviews, and architectural design on greenfield projects
- Own team deliverables and objectives
- Set technical strategy and engineering best practices
- Hire, build team, and manage performance
- Career development and technical guidance to engineers
- Collaborate with business stakeholders on project management
- Own creation and delivery of innovative products

## Company

Warner Music Group is one of the three major global music companies, operating in 70+ countries. Its Recorded Music division includes labels such as Atlantic, Warner Records, Parlophone, Fueled by Ramen and others. Its publishing arm, Warner Chappell Music, manages a catalog of over one million copyrights. The Sodatone product specifically is an internal analytics platform that helped identify artists like Teddy Swims and PinkPanthress.
