Software Engineering Manager for the Global Licensing Systems team, responsible for full life-cycle music licensing systems. ## Company

Warner Music Group is a global music entertainment company operating in 70+ countries. Home to labels including Atlantic, Warner Records, Parlophone, Fueled by Ramen, Roadrunner and others. Music publishing arm Warner Chappell Music manages a catalog of over one million copyrights spanning every genre. Also includes ADA (independent community support), WMX (artist services) and Warner Music Entertainment (film and television).
