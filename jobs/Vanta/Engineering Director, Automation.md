## Company

Vanta is a Trust Management Platform founded in 2018 that helps businesses earn and prove trust through continuous security monitoring and compliance automation. Trusted by 16,000+ customers including Snowflake, GitHub, Ramp, Cursor and Icelandair. Backed by Sequoia, Wellington, Craft Ventures, Y Combinator, Goldman Sachs and others. Customers in 58 countries with offices in SF, NYC, London, Dublin, Tel Aviv and Sydney.
