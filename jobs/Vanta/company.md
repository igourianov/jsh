# Vanta

- **Company Type:** SaaS / Trust & Compliance Automation Platform
- **Stage:** Late-stage private, growth/scale-up (pre-IPO)
- **Size:** ~1,000-1,600 employees (sources vary; growing fast, engineering org targeting 300-500 engineers)
- **Remote Policy:** Remote-first in the US, with hybrid hubs in San Francisco, New York, London, Dublin and Sydney

## Quick Take
- Well-funded, fast-growing trust/compliance platform (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR automation) with 12,000+ customers and strong revenue growth ($300M+ est. ARR).
- CEO gets strong marks (80% approval), but overall engineering culture reviews are mixed: good pay and smart peers, but meeting-heavy, low-to-average work-life balance, and reports of insufficient dev-experience investment as the org scales.
- Multiple review reports of toxic pockets in engineering leadership ("engineering leadership is full of sharks", bullying/targeting of individuals) worth probing directly in interviews.

## Milestones
- 2018: Founded by Christina Cacioppo (CEO) and Erik Goldman, both ex-Dropbox.
- 2021-2022: Rapid funding rounds, reached unicorn status in 2022.
- Dec 2022: One layoff event, ~53 people affected.
- 2023: Valued at $1.6B.
- 2024: Raised at $2.45B valuation.
- Jul 2025: Raised $150M Series D-type round at $4.15B valuation (raised despite reportedly "not needing the money").
- 2026: ~1,600 employees, considered "on the IPO trajectory" per investors (Sequoia).

## Company & Product
Vanta builds a trust management / compliance automation platform that helps companies continuously prove security and compliance (SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, and others) instead of treating audits as periodic fire drills. It integrates with 300+ third-party tools to automate evidence collection. Customer base is 12,000+ companies, skewing toward startups and scale-ups needing to pass security audits to close enterprise deals. Business model is subscription SaaS.

## Engineering Culture
Tech blog (vanta.com/all-categories/engineering) is active and covers real technical problems: AI verifier tooling for auditors, an AI code-review agent evolved into a production on-call agent, sandboxing the "Vanta Agent" for production use, and posts on authorization-as-a-platform and fine-grained access control at scale. This suggests genuine engineering investment in AI-assisted tooling and platform/access-control work, not just marketing content.

Employee reviews describe engineering peers as strong ("smartest and most hardworking people I've worked with") and management as generally receptive to letting engineers pursue interesting problems. Countervailing reports describe a meeting-heavy culture and multi-day development cycles as the org scales past ~300 engineers toward 500, attributed to underinvestment in developer experience/internal tooling.

## Tech Stack
Backend: TypeScript / Node.js. Frontend: React. Heavy integration-engineering surface (300+ third-party integrations) and evidence-pipeline infrastructure. Recent engineering blog content signals active use of LLM/agentic AI systems in-product (AI verifier, AI code-review/on-call agent, agent sandboxing).

## Team Health
Glassdoor: ~3.0-3.3/5 overall (rating varies slightly by source/snapshot), 3.0-3.1 work-life balance, 3.2-3.3 culture & values, 3.1-3.2 career opportunities. CEO approval is notably higher at 80%, and several reviews specifically praise Christina Cacioppo's leadership as thoughtful and principled. Pay is consistently described as very strong ("pays extremely well"). Work-life balance is repeatedly flagged as a weak point.

## Business Stability
Well capitalized: ~$500M total raised, $4.15B valuation as of the July 2025 round, raised at a time the company reportedly didn't need the cash — a signal of investor demand and balance-sheet strength rather than runway anxiety. Revenue estimated at $300M+ ARR. One layoff event in company history (Dec 2022, ~53 people), none confirmed for 2025-2026 in available sources, though some forum chatter (Blind) mentions recent layoffs without confirmed specifics — worth asking about directly if relevant.

## Red Flags
- Multiple Glassdoor reviews report bullying/targeting of individual employees and describe parts of engineering leadership as toxic ("sharks"), with claims the company failed to protect affected employees. Worth direct questions in interviews about team-level management style, not just the CEO.
- Developer experience reportedly not keeping pace with headcount growth (300 to 500 engineers), leading to multi-day dev cycles per some reviews — a concern for an EM role focused on velocity/DX.
- Work-life balance and meeting load are recurring complaints across review sources.
