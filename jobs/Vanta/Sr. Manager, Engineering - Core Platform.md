## Company

Vanta, founded 2018, is a trust-management and GRC (governance, risk, compliance) SaaS platform that helps businesses earn and prove trust by continuously monitoring and verifying their security posture. It started by automating security monitoring for standards like SOC 2, HIPAA and ISO 27001 and now positions itself as an "Agentic Trust Platform" combining a Trust Graph (always-on map of security/compliance posture) with an AI "Vanta Agent" acting as a 24/7 GRC engineer. Capabilities include automated compliance, streamlined audits, third-party/vendor risk management, risk management, a public Trust Center and questionnaire automation, with 400+ integrations and 1,400+ automated hourly tests. Trusted by 16,000+ customers (Snowflake, GitHub, Ramp, Cursor, Icelandair) across 58 countries. 1,001-5,000 employees with offices in SF, NYC, London, Dublin, Tel Aviv and Sydney. Investors include Sequoia, Wellington, Craft Ventures, Y Combinator, Goldman Sachs Alternatives, J.P. Morgan, Atlassian Ventures and CrowdStrike Ventures.
