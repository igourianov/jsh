## Company

Wrapbook is a vertical fintech company building a production-finance platform for the project economy, initially focused on the entertainment industry. The product unifies payroll, spend, accounting, startwork and cost management in a single system, letting finance teams onboard, pay and insure freelance and project-based workforces in real time. The company positions itself against slow legacy competitors in a stated $50B market, emphasizing a concierge support model and rapid feature velocity (175+ features in the past year). Wrapbook has raised over $30M and is backed by top-tier investors including Andreessen Horowitz, Equal Ventures, Uncork Capital, Jeffrey Katzenberg's WndrCo, A* Capital and CAA co-founder Michael Ovitz. Headcount is 201-500 with a remote-first setup across Canada and the USA.
