# Tradeify

- **Company Type:** Fintech / futures prop trading firm (evaluation-based funded trader programs, brokerage)
- **Stage:** Scale-up (privately held, no disclosed outside funding)
- **Size:** 11-50 employees per job boards (100,000+ active traders)
- **Remote Policy:** Remote (US-based company, HQ Boca Raton, Florida; posting targets Canada / Americas)

## Quick Take
- Fast-growing futures prop firm, reportedly self-funded by evaluation fees. Strong trader-side reputation (Trustpilot 4.6-4.8), scaled roughly sevenfold in a year.
- Tiny engineering org (2 open engineering roles, full-stack pay listed at $120-130k USD) supporting 100k+ traders. An EM here is likely very hands-on with heavy load.
- Key concern: revenue comes from evaluation fees, a business model that is fragile to rule changes, regulation and payout pressure, and the prop firm sector has seen 80+ shutdowns.

## Milestones
- Founded by Brett Simberkoff (CEO) and a co-founder (Mistry). Sources disagree on the year (2022 vs 2024). Reportedly four years from idea to launch.
- Grew to 100,000+ active traders after roughly sevenfold growth in a year.
- 2026-01-23: brand partnerships (UFC legend Israel Adesanya campaign, FlyQuest esports org partnership).
- 2026-05-19: launched Slay Markets brokerage and signed exclusive clearing agreement with NinjaTrader.
- 2026-06-15: acquired ChartChamps (competitive trading platform).
- Also operates a crypto arm (Tradeify Crypto).

## Company & Product
Futures-only prop firm. Traders pay for evaluation challenges and, if they pass, trade firm capital with payouts (100% of first $15k profit, 90% after, daily payouts, often within hours). No monthly subscription paths. Integrates third-party platforms (NinjaTrader, Tradovate) and now has its own brokerage and gamified trading product via acquisition. This matches the posting's platform scope: trading accounts, evaluations, third-party integrations, APIs for multiple product teams.

## Engineering Culture
No engineering blog or open source found. Careers run on Ashby. Postings stress high-performance trading dashboards, real-time interfaces and scalable backend APIs. Small team, so process and standards are likely still being built (the EM role includes setting standards and hiring practices org-wide).

## Tech Stack
From postings: React / Next.js / Vue frontend, Python and/or C# (also Node, Go) backend, PostgreSQL and Redis, Docker and Kubernetes, cloud (AWS/GCP/Azure), WebSockets, event-driven systems (Kafka / NATS / Pulsar), TimescaleDB or ClickHouse.

## Team Health
No Glassdoor data found for employees (company too small). Customer-side Trustpilot is strongly positive with 600+ reviews; recurring complaint is slow KYC onboarding with payment processor Rise. Nothing found on turnover or work-life balance. Ask directly.

## Business Stability
- No disclosed funding, layoffs or regulatory actions against Tradeify found. No CFTC action found.
- Profitable-looking growth and new investments (brokerage, acquisition, marketing) suggest cash generation.
- Sector risk: 80+ prop firms shut down 2020-2026. Revenue depends on evaluation pass rates and rules, and on clearing relationships (NinjaTrader exclusivity, Tradovate). An isolated trader lawsuit over a Tradovate platform glitch was reported against a prop firm.
- Founders stated dropping subscriptions barely moved revenue, so business is comfortable with model changes.

## Red Flags
- Very small company (11-50) for the scale of traders: on-call and incident load could be heavy on a business-critical live platform.
- Prop firm business model is controversial and regulatory-sensitive; check comfort with it.
- Hired via Jobgether intermediary with unclear comp, team size and reporting line.
- Conflicting founding year across sources.
- No employee reviews to verify culture.
