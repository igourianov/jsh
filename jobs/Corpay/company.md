# Corpay

- **Company Type:** Fintech / Payments (Cambridge division: B2B cross-border payments and FX risk management)
- **Stage:** Public (NYSE: CPAY)
- **Size:** Large enterprise. Employee count for the Cambridge division not established
- **Remote Policy:** On-site in Toronto per the posting

## Quick Take
- Cambridge is a subsidiary of a profitable public parent. Financial stability is not a concern.
- Toronto Glassdoor rating is 2.9 (32 reviews) against 3.8 company-wide. Culture 2.7, career opportunities 2.9, 41% recommend.
- Reviews describe repeated yearly layoffs or "transformations" since the rebrand from FLEETCOR. Combined with the on-site requirement, this is the main concern.

## Milestones
- 1992: Cambridge Global Payments (later Cambridge Mercantile) established in Toronto.
- FLEETCOR Technologies completes acquisition of Cambridge Global Payments (B2B international payments).
- 2021: FLEETCOR rebrands to Corpay. Cambridge merges with US-based AFEX (est. 1979) to form Corpay Cross-Border. Toronto stays the Cambridge headquarters.
- 2026-02-04: Corpay signs agreement to sell PayByPhone to Lightyear Capital. Portfolio optimization ongoing.
- 2026 guidance: revenue $5.2-5.3B, raised after Q1.

## Company & Product
Corpay is a global business payments company (fleet, corporate payments, lodging, cross-border) operating in 150+ countries. Cross-Border serves SMB and mid-cap businesses with wire, EFT, draft and check payments in 140+ currencies plus currency risk management, through 100+ correspondent banks. Proprietary payment automation technology is a core part of the offering.

## Business Stability
Strong. Public company with raised 2026 guidance, active M&A and portfolio pruning. Risk is at the team level (restructurings), not company level.

## Red Flags
- Reported recurring layoffs or "transformations" after the 2021 rebrand.
- Low Toronto ratings on culture and career growth.
- On-site only, against a remote-only target.
- Division headcount, org structure and tech practices unknown.
