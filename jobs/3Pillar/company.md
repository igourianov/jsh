# 3Pillar

- **Company Type:** IT Services (software product engineering consultancy)
- **Stage:** Enterprise (private equity backed)
- **Size:** ~1,800 employees (Built In, unverified); ~2,300 at the 2022 Jonah Group deal
- **Remote Policy:** Remote-first (per posting); Montreal-area remote for the screened role

## Quick Take
- Consultancy, not a recruiting agency. Engineers are placed on client projects, so the experience depends on the client and the project.
- Headcount reportedly shrank from the 2022-2023 peak. Glassdoor flags bench time and weak job security for people between projects.
- Glassdoor 3.1/5 with 45% recommending. Engineering talent is rated well, pay and security are not.

## Milestones
- 2006: founded by David H. DeWolf, headquartered in Fairfax, Virginia
- 2020-08: acquired Isthmus Software (Costa Rica)
- 2020-12: acquired Tiempo Development (Arizona)
- 2021-04: acquired Software Development Europe (third acquisition in a year)
- 2021: reported investment by H.I.G. Capital, following earlier private equity ownership (Built In summary only, not confirmed by primary sources)
- 2022-09: acquired Jonah Group (Toronto, 160 people, banking, financial services and insurance focus). Chenoa Information Services also listed as an acquisition
- 2024: pivot to AI-led delivery, NEXUS platform launched
- 2026: HelixAI platform listed (Built In, unverified)

## Company & Product
Software product engineering services firm. Builds software for client companies across North America, Europe, Latin America and Asia. Growth came mostly through acquisitions. Jonah Group gives it a Canadian presence in financial services. It markets its own AI delivery platforms (NEXUS, HelixAI) and has appeared repeatedly on the Inc. 5000 list.

## Engineering Culture
- Reviewers cite communities of practice, work with current technologies and a path to full-stack product engineering (a 2017 review, so dated).
- No engineering blog or open source presence was verified in this research.
- Work quality and team dynamics vary by client engagement.

## Tech Stack
Client driven, so no single stack. The screened Architect role (REQ_177) is Microsoft centric: ASP.NET, C#, SQL Server and Azure. Jonah Group's background suggests enterprise and financial services stacks.

## Team Health
- Glassdoor: 3.1/5 from ~735 reviews, 45% would recommend.
- Positives: good engineering talent, strong colleagues, interesting technology.
- Negatives: average pay, limited job security outside an active project, bench time when projects are short.
- Experience depends heavily on the client project.

## Business Stability
- Private equity backed, acquisition driven expansion between 2020 and 2022.
- Headcount reportedly contracted into early 2026, described as consolidation and right-sizing. No specific layoff event was found.
- No 2025 or 2026 press releases on acquisitions or layoffs were found.

## Red Flags
- Shrinking headcount after a PE-driven roll-up.
- Bench time and job security tied to being on a project.
- Below-market pay reported by reviewers.
- Built In figures come from an AI-generated summary not reviewed by the company. Treat as unverified.
