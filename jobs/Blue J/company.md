# Blue J

- **Company Type:** SaaS (generative AI tax research)
- **Stage:** Scale-up (Series D)
- **Size:** 51-200 employees
- **Remote Policy:** Remote within Canada, must live within driving distance of Toronto, 1-3 office visits per quarter

## Quick Take
- Well funded and growing fast: $122M USD Series D in 2025, revenue and customer base more than doubled in H1 2025.
- Glassdoor is very strong (4.8 from 106 reviews), with meeting load as the only recurring complaint.
- Concern: little public engineering signal (no engineering blog or open source found), and the stack is only partly visible through job postings.

## Milestones
- 2015: founded in Toronto as a tax prediction product (Blue J Legal).
- 2024-12: Series C led by Ten Coves Capital.
- 2025-08: $122M USD Series D led by Oak HC/FT and Sapphire Ventures, seven months after the Series C.
- 2025: expansion across North America and the UK, over 1,000 clients including Big 4, top 100 US CPA firms and Fortune 100 companies.

## Company & Product
Generative AI tax research platform for accounting and tax professionals. Content is curated by tax experts, with over 8 million tax questions answered. B2B SaaS with enterprise account structures, purchasing, integrations and collaboration features. Funds are earmarked for team growth, faster product development and market expansion.

## Engineering Culture
- Postings describe lean practices, high quality systems and shipping multiple times per day.
- Published Director interview loop: Talent Acquisition Manager, VP Engineering, technical deep dive, system design, VP Engineering leadership interview, CTO, CEO.
- Level framework exists (Director is Level 6).
- No engineering blog or GitHub organization found.

## Tech Stack
- From postings: TypeScript, Node.js, React, Python, AWS, Cloudflare, Terraform, Kubernetes, ArgoCD, Docker, GitHub, CircleCI, PostgreSQL, ElasticSearch, DynamoDB, S3, Kafka.
- Backend postings mention data pipelines, search algorithms, an internal data-mesh infrastructure, service-oriented and event-driven architectures.
- Software Developer II pays CAD 120-150k plus equity.

## Team Health
- Glassdoor: 4.8 of 5, 106 reviews, 93% would recommend.
- Themes: intentional culture, supportive team. Criticism: meeting-heavy at times.
- Unknown whether reviews skew to engineering. Ratings this high on a fast-growing company may reflect early-stage sentiment.

## Business Stability
- Two large rounds within eight months, strong revenue growth. No layoffs found in searches.
- Risks: dependence on foundation model vendors and competition from larger legal and tax research incumbents (not verified in sources). Latest news found is from 2025.

## Red Flags
No significant red flags identified. Watch items: meeting load, fast scaling pressure after the Series D and thin public information on engineering practices.
