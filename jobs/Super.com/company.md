# Super.com

**Company Type:** SaaS / Fintech
**Stage:** Scale-up (Series C)
**Size:** 200-250 employees
**Remote Policy:** 100% Remote-First (Global)

## Quick Take
- Profitable fintech/travel company with $200M+ revenue after $200M total funding
- Strong engineering culture (4.1/5 Glassdoor rating from engineers) with emphasis on best practices and work-life balance
- Successfully pivoted from SnapTravel to all-in-one financial app, now celebrating profitability in 2025
- Actively hiring at Director level to build new Core Experience organization

## Company & Product

Founded in 2016 by Hussein Fazal and Henry Shi as SnapTravel (hotel booking via SMS/messaging), the company rebranded to Snapcommerce in 2020 when expanding into e-commerce, then to Super.com in October 2022 as it pivoted into financial services. Headquarters moved from Toronto to San Francisco in 2022.

Super.com operates as an all-in-one financial app with Super+ membership described as "one of the fastest-growing programs in the world." Products include travel booking, online marketplace, cashback card (issued by MRV Banks via Mastercard) with no fees or credit checks, and credit-building features. Company has processed >$2B in total sales and reached $200M+ annualized revenue.

The company held its annual offsite in Cancun (2025) with 200+ employees under the theme "Celebrate & Elevate," celebrating profitability and planning future growth. Leadership emphasizes systematic AI integration across operations, product, and customer support.

## Engineering Culture

Engineers report positive experiences with emphasis on modern practices and growth. Glassdoor reviews highlight:
- Engineering org "diligently keeps up to date with latest standards and best practices"
- "Great architecture and open culture for technical growth"
- "Nice and honest people to work with"
- "Learned lots, great team and culture"
- Work-life balance emphasized with extra days off during summer and perks like $25 Uber Eats credits on Fridays

Some technical debt mentioned but appears manageable. No evidence of public engineering blog found, suggesting lower external visibility for engineering thought leadership. The Director of Engineering role suggests building new organizational structure for Core Experience (membership and user experience).

## Tech Stack

React (frontend), Python/Node.js microservices (backend), PostgreSQL, Redis, Snowflake (data warehouse), GitLab (version control), AWS (cloud), Kubernetes (orchestration), Datadog (monitoring), Amplitude (analytics), Material-UI (components). Architecture emphasizes distributed systems and microservice patterns.

## Business Stability

**Funding:** ~$200M total raised ($1.2M seed 2016, $21M Series A 2018, $85M Series B 2021, $85M Series C April 2023 led by Inovia Capital). Investors include Inovia Capital, Lion Capital, Telstra Ventures, Shopify president Harley Finkelstein, Steph Curry, and other high-profile backers.

**Financial Health:** Company reports profitability as of 2025 with $200M+ annualized revenue. No layoffs found in 2024-2026 tech layoff trackers. Recent all-hands celebration of profitability suggests stable financial position.

**Market Position:** Competes in consumer finance against Wealthsimple, Koho, Neo Financial. Differentiation through Super+ membership combining travel, cashback, and financial services.

## Red Flags

No significant red flags identified. The company appears stable with:
- Recent profitability milestone (2025)
- No layoffs during 2024-2025 tech downturn
- Active hiring at senior levels (Director of Engineering)
- Positive engineering culture reviews
- Strong investor backing with high-profile participants

Minor considerations:
- Some technical debt mentioned in reviews (common for scale-ups)
- Limited public engineering visibility (no prominent blog/open source presence)
