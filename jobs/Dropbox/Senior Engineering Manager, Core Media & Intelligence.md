## Company

Dropbox is a public cloud collaboration company with 700M+ registered users across 180 countries, best known for file sync, sharing and storage. Beyond the core product, the company operates a portfolio that includes Dropbox Dash (AI-powered universal search and knowledge management), Dropbox Sign (e-signature) and DocSend (document sharing and analytics). The engineering org runs exabyte-scale storage and processing across multiple data centers and is investing heavily in AI-powered, agentic experiences on top of its content platform. Dropbox operates a Virtual First model: remote-primary with non-linear schedules, async-by-default communication and periodic in-person team gatherings (Dropbox Neighborhoods, offsites). The company positions itself as a "startup mindset, enterprise scale" environment that explicitly encourages employees to use AI tooling in daily work.
