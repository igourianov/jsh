## Company

Postscript is an SMS marketing platform for e-commerce, trusted by 20,000+ Shopify and Shopify Plus stores (Brooklinen, Ruggable, True Classic, Dr. Squatch). Its mission is to make SMS the number-one revenue channel for e-commerce merchants, offering subscriber list growth, messaging strategy testing, customer service and sales/loyalty tools in one platform. Founded in 2018 as a 100% remote organization, it has 250+ employees and is backed by Greylock, Y Combinator and other top investors. The backend is primarily Python on AWS with select Go services, backed by Kafka, Postgres and MongoDB. Channels is the foundation many of Postscript's AI products build on top of.
