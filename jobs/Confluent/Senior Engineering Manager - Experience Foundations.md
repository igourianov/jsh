## Summary

Full-stack Senior Engineering Manager role leading the Experience Foundations team at Confluent. The team owns Security UI, Networking UI and Billing UI (collectively TEAR: Trusted Experiences Access & Revenue), plus shared experience foundations infrastructure including the design system, compliance support, performance and accessibility tooling.

The core challenge is evolving the foundations infrastructure from UI-only support to serving the entire Experience organization across API, CLI, Terraform, VSCode and MCP interfaces. The role also involves establishing patterns for UI engineers building security and networking features across Confluent's product portfolio (Governance, Flink, Kafka, Connect, Tableflow).

- **Coding:** 0%. Leadership and architecture focused. "Hands-on" refers to understanding systems deeply and unblocking teams, not writing production code.
- Establish reusable patterns and best practices for UI engineers across security and networking domains
- Own PM and Design partnerships for Security, Networking and Billing domains
- Drive evolution of Experience Foundations from UI-only to full Experience org support
- Partner with existing EM (direct report) to shape and execute the foundations roadmap
- Champion developer productivity through AI tooling, test automation and process improvements
- Bridge technical and product perspectives, communicate risks and trade-offs to senior leadership

## Company

Confluent (recently acquired by IBM) builds a cloud-native data streaming platform built on Apache Kafka. The platform enables real-time data movement across organizations, allowing companies to react to events as they happen rather than processing data in batches. The product serves enterprises needing real-time data pipelines, event-driven architectures and stream processing. Core products include managed Kafka, Flink for stream processing, Connect for integrations, Governance for data management and Tableflow for data accessibility.
