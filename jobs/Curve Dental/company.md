# Curve Dental

- **Company Type:** SaaS / Vertical Practice Management Software
- **Stage:** Privately held, VC-backed, profitable growth stage (not startup, not enterprise)
- **Size:** ~150-210 employees (LinkedIn range 51-200; other estimate ~207 as of 2026)
- **Remote Policy:** Not explicitly published; company has offices in Alpharetta GA, Provo UT, Calgary AB and Aberdeen Scotland, suggesting a hybrid/distributed model across North America and UK

## Quick Take
- Long-established (founded 2004), cloud-native dental practice management SaaS with over 80,000 dental professionals as customers - a real, durable niche business, not a hype-cycle startup
- Only known institutional investor is Battery Ventures (2018), no subsequent acquisition or IPO news found - suggests slow, steady growth rather than aggressive scaling or an active M&A story
- One Glassdoor review titled "Likely to get laid off..." is a yellow flag worth probing directly in interviews, though overall Glassdoor sentiment (3.9/5) is solid

## Milestones
- 2004: Founded
- 2009: Official North American market launch
- 2011: Patented direct-to-cloud digital image capture
- 2013: Added mobile access, integrated payments, appointment reminders
- 2016-2017: Launched Smart Forms; ISO and FDA certifications
- 2018: Battery Ventures invests (Series A / growth investment)
- 2021: Launched Curve GRO patient engagement product; partnered with Global Payments Integrated
- 2022: Leadership committed to reinvesting $60M into the "SuperHero" platform over 3 years; added AI voice-activated charting
- 2026: Launched Curve Pay (integrated payments) and Curve FLO AI (embedded AI workflow tools); recognized as cloud-native leader in a 2026 Dental Technology Landscape Report

## Company & Product
Curve Dental is a cloud-based, all-in-one dental practice management platform (flagship product: Curve SuperHero, formerly Curve Hero) covering scheduling, charting, imaging, billing, patient engagement and business analytics. It targets both independent single-location practices and multi-location DSOs (dental service organizations) across the US and Canada, with some UK presence (Aberdeen office). The company positions itself as the "fastest-growing cloud-based dental practice management software" and has recently pushed into AI-assisted workflows and embedded payments (Curve Pay), indicating active product expansion rather than maintenance mode.

## Team Health
- Glassdoor: 3.9/5 overall (37 reviews), 3.7 work-life balance, 3.9 culture and values, 3.7 career opportunities - generally solid figures for a company this size
- One SRE/DevOps review cited growth and professional development positively
- Pay is called "below market rate" for the role type in at least one review, offset by "stellar benefits and good PTO"
- A review titled "Likely to get laid off..." exists but content wasn't accessible - worth asking directly about recent team stability/attrition in interviews
- Small review sample size (37) means individual reviews carry more weight than at a larger company; treat aggregate rating with some caution

## Business Stability
- Privately held; only disclosed institutional backer is Battery Ventures (invested 2018), no additional funding rounds, acquisition, or IPO activity found in Crunchbase/PitchBook/Owler searches
- No public revenue figures; growth signals are all self-reported (80,000+ professionals served, $60M reinvestment commitment in 2022, continued 2026 product launches like Curve Pay and Curve FLO AI)
- Currently listed as actively hiring, with careers page claiming more open Engineering & Technical roles than any point in the past 12 months (self-reported, should be verified against actual live job postings)
- No layoffs reported in news searches, though the single "likely to get laid off" Glassdoor review title is a data point worth reconciling with that "actively hiring" claim

## Red Flags
- Sparse independent engineering signal (no blog, no GitHub, no conference talks) makes it hard to verify claimed engineering culture and tech stack from outside sources
- Single funding round disclosed since 2018 with no subsequent event (round, acquisition, exit) in 7+ years - could mean stable/profitable bootstrap-style growth, or could mean stalled growth; worth asking directly about company financial health and growth trajectory
- Small Glassdoor sample (37 reviews) including at least one negative-sounding layoff-themed review title that couldn't be independently verified
- Reported "below market rate" pay in at least one review
