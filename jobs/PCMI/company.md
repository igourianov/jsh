# PCMI

- **Company Type:** SaaS (warranty, F&I and service contract administration software)
- **Stage:** PE-backed scale-up (Thoma Bravo growth investment, 2025)
- **Size:** 300+ (per posting), roughly 200-500 in directories
- **Remote Policy:** Posting is remote (Oakville, ON listed). HQ Park Ridge, IL with hybrid schedule reported on Glassdoor.

## Quick Take
- Not a recruiting agency. Direct employer, software vendor to warranty administrators, OEMs, dealers and lenders.
- Backed by Thoma Bravo (2025), previously Equality Asset Management (2021). Expect PE-style growth, M&A integration and margin focus.
- Glassdoor is middling (3.5 overall) with pay and promotion fairness as recurring complaints, consistent with the below-floor base in the posting.

## Milestones
- 2012: PCMI founded (per company site, serving the warranty market since 2012)
- 2021-01: Equality Asset Management makes first equity investment
- 2025-03: Thoma Bravo announces strategic growth investment
- 2025-11: Acquires StoneEagle Enterprise Solutions business unit (contract and claims management software)

## Company & Product
Park Ridge, Illinois. Software for extended warranty and F&I administration. Flagship products are PCRS (policy, claim and reporting) and TruComply (lender compliance). Per the posting: 125+ customers in 14 countries, 181M+ contracts and 53M+ claims processed. Clients include Nissan Global Reinsurance. CEO is Mark Nagelvoort. Offices in the US, Canada, Poland and India per the posting.

## Engineering Culture
No public engineering blog or GitHub organization found. Posting says the company is modernizing its architecture and pushing AI across the SDLC, including Claude Code. Glassdoor reviewers mention frequent changes in technology direction.

## Tech Stack
From the posting: C#, Angular, TypeScript, JavaScript, Azure (App Services, Service Bus, Azure SQL), Azure DevOps, TeamCity, Octopus.

## Team Health
- Glassdoor company-wide: about 3.5-3.6 from 55 reviews, 67% recommend.
- Park Ridge location: 4.7 from 18 reviews. Praise for hybrid schedule, benefits and coworkers.
- Complaints: pay around 50th percentile, work-life balance, shifting technology direction, office layout. A 2025 review cites favoritism in promotions and weak leadership. Older reviews (2016, 2021) cite turnover.
- Management responds to reviews, pointing to onboarding, culture and compensation work.

## Business Stability
Stable niche vertical with sticky enterprise customers. Two rounds of institutional PE capital in four years and an active acquisition in 2025-11 indicate growth. Risks are integration of acquired product lines and PE-driven cost pressure. No layoffs found.

## Red Flags
- Compensation: reviewers and the posting both point to below-market pay.
- Technology direction reported as changing often, alongside an ongoing architecture modernization and a fresh acquisition to integrate.
- Promotion fairness and leadership quality criticized in a 2025 review.
- Niche domain (insurance and warranty) limits transferability.
