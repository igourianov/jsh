## Company

Clario, part of Thermo Fisher Scientific, is a leading provider of endpoint data solutions to the life sciences industry. Their offering combines eCOA, cardiac safety, medical imaging, precision motion and respiratory endpoints. Founded over 50 years ago (originally ERT), Clario has supported 30,000+ clinical trial opportunities in 100+ countries and contributed to over 70% of FDA drug approvals since 2015. The R&D software group builds next-generation clinical trial technologies running on field devices in regulated environments. Clario was acquired by Thermo Fisher Scientific (~$9B, announced Oct 2025, expected to close mid-2026).
