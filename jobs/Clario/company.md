# Clario

**Company Type:** Healthcare SaaS / Clinical Trial Technology Platform
**Stage:** Enterprise (Acquired - pending close)
**Size:** ~4,000 employees globally
**Remote Policy:** Remote/hybrid options available for US-based roles

## Quick Take
- Strong market position: supported 70% of FDA drug approvals over past decade, 19,000+ trials completed
- Major transition underway: $9B acquisition by Thermo Fisher Scientific announced Oct 2025, expected close mid-2026
- Employee concerns about layoffs, offshoring, and leadership churn (3 CEOs in 2 years reported)

## Milestones
- **1972**: Founded as ERT (eResearchTechnology), pioneering clinical trial data gathering
- **2009**: Bio-Technologies rebranded to Bioclinica (medical imaging focus)
- **2020**: Nordic Capital, Astorg, and Novo Holdings acquired ERT
- **April 2021**: ERT and Bioclinica merged
- **November 2021**: Combined company rebranded as Clario
- **April 2024**: Acquired ArtiQ (AI medical technology company, Belgium)
- **June 2024**: Confidentially filed for IPO, targeting $10B+ valuation
- **January 2025**: Secured ~$4B private debt financing from Blackstone
- **October 2025**: Thermo Fisher Scientific announced $8.875B acquisition (up to $9.4B with earnouts)

## Company & Product

Clario is a leading provider of endpoint data solutions for clinical trials, serving pharmaceutical, biotech, and medical device companies. The company offers the industry's only technology platform combining:

- **eCOA** (Electronic Clinical Outcome Assessments)
- **Cardiac Safety** - ECG monitoring and assessment
- **Medical Imaging** - Core lab services (nearly 2,000 oncology studies)
- **Respiratory Endpoints** - Pulmonary function testing
- **Precision Motion** - Wearable sensor data capture

The platform enables site-based, hybrid, and decentralized clinical trials. Clario operates 30 facilities across 9 countries with deep regulatory expertise, having contributed to 870+ regulatory approvals.

**2025 Revenue:** ~$1.25 billion (estimated)
**EBITDA:** ~$400 million (as of 2024 filing)

## Engineering Culture

**Team Structure:**
- 20+ agile development squads supported by platform/solution engineering teams
- Engineering managers lead small, high-performing teams building foundational libraries and frameworks

**Tech Stack:**
- **Backend:** .NET, SQL Server, Oracle, Postgres
- **Frontend:** Angular
- **Cloud:** AWS and Azure (cloud-native focus)
- **AI/ML:** Custom generative AI platform on AWS using Amazon Bedrock, Textract, OpenSearch, EKS
- **DevOps:** CI/CD pipelines, DevOps best practices emphasized

**Culture Notes:**
- Regulated environment (FDA compliance is critical)
- Focus on "technical excellence" and "developer productivity"
- Change management emphasis due to platform serving many squads
- Fast-paced with emphasis on secure application development

**Interview Process:**
- Mix of behavioral and technical questions
- Basic SQL questions and theory
- Brief coding section
- Difficulty rated 2.63/5 (moderate)

**Current Status:** Being acquired by Thermo Fisher Scientific

- Profitable enough to attract $9B acquisition

## Red Flags

**Leadership Instability:**
- Reports of 3 CEOs in 2 years
- "Terrible new c-suite" with poor communication cited in reviews
- Restructuring without clear direction

**Layoffs and Offshoring:**
- "Massive layoffs in 'upper wage' countries" reported
- Concerns about US jobs being eliminated in favor of India, Costa Rica, Hungary
- Loss of institutional knowledge acknowledged
- Morale "at all time low" per recent reviews

**Culture Concerns:**
- Some reviews describe "toxic and backstabby" environment
- Micromanagement reported
- "Zero training and zero opportunities for growth" in some areas

**Acquisition Uncertainty:**
- Deal expected to close mid-2026
- Integration with Thermo Fisher could bring additional restructuring
- Synergy targets suggest potential further cost-cutting

**Client Impact:**
- One review claims clients "dropping rapidly" due to knowledge loss from layoffs
