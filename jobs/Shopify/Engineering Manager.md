# Engineering Manager - Shopify

**Source URL:** https://www.shopify.com/careers/engineering-manager_227e8512-a274-4253-a0a1-825df6bbc0b9

**Work Arrangement:** Remote (daily work)

## Position Overview

Engineering Manager role at Shopify. The company emphasizes maintaining strong technical capabilities for engineering managers.

## Key Requirements

Before applying, candidates must confirm alignment with Shopify's core values:

- Deep commitment to work quality
- Active pursuit of growth, even when uncomfortable
- Thriving in ambiguous, changing environments
- Consistent delivery and weekly shipping capability
- Remote work preference
- Demonstrated resilience and resourcefulness
- Commitment to improving commerce accessibility

## Role Expectations

**Technical Proficiency:** Engineering Managers at Shopify maintain strong technical capabilities. Candidates participate in a "75-minute Pair Programming interview" where they lead problem-solving to demonstrate coding and analytical skills.

**Career Track Options:**
- Individual Contributor
- People Manager

**Engineering Expertise Categories:**
- Backend
- Frontend
- Fullstack
- Infrastructure
- Mobile
- Security
- Applied Machine Learning

**Experience Range:** 0-4 years, 5-7 years, 8-10 years, or 11+ years managing engineering teams

## Technical Skills

Proficiency assessment covers: Ruby on Rails, Rust, Golang, Python, TypeScript, React, JavaScript, Kubernetes, AWS, GCP, and various databases and tools.

## Evaluation Criteria

Candidates address experience across:

- Project leadership scope and complexity
- Software design and architecture involvement
- Mentorship approach and philosophy

## Application Requirements

Standard information plus:
- Resume and LinkedIn profile
- Work authorization confirmation
- NDA and privacy notice agreement
