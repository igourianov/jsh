# Shopify Screening Questions

## Question: Please give an example of a project where you led the team. We'd love to know things like what the project was, what part you played, what the outcome was and what you'd change. (Plus, any other details you'd like to share!)

I led a 7-month project to extract a critical calculation engine from our SaaS monolith and scale it horizontally. The engine processed payroll and benefits calculations for thousands of enterprise clients but was single-threaded, causing 10+ minute processing times for larger clients. I led 5 senior engineers, designing the extraction strategy and service architecture while coordinating across 6 stakeholder teams. Technically, I architected safety mechanisms including shadow mode (running both systems in parallel), automated reconciliation, and instant rollback capability. We navigated significant ambiguity—midway through we discovered 2x more dependencies than estimated and pivoted from "lift and shift" to "rebuild with backward compatibility." The outcome: calculation time dropped from 10+ minutes to under 2 minutes, zero production incidents during rollout, and Product velocity increased 3x for new features. Looking back, I'd invest more upfront in automated contract testing and timebox dependency analysis—we over-analyzed initially when iterative discovery would have been faster. The shadow mode strategy proved invaluable for building stakeholder confidence and catching edge cases without client impact.
