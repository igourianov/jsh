## Company

Poparide is a Canadian carpooling platform with a mission to fill empty seats in cars and make travel more social, affordable and sustainable for everyone in Canada. The app connects drivers traveling from A to B with passengers heading in the same direction, facilitating ride posting, booking, chatting, reviews and payments. With 2 million current members and plans to scale to 20+ million, Poparide is a profitable, intentionally lean company with a small but mighty team of 17 passionate people. The company fosters a positive, professional and collaborative culture emphasizing transparency, curiosity and collaboration. Based in GTA (Toronto/Kitchener-Waterloo area), Poparide offers base salary starting at $180,000 CAD plus profit-sharing, equity through Employee Stock Options Plan (ESOP), 20 paid holiday days per year, and 10 paid statutory days off. The role requires regular coworking sessions with the Product Team every 2 weeks minimum in the GTA region.
