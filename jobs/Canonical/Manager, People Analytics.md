## Summary

New role in Canonical's Workplace Engineering function, reporting to the Global Head of Workplace Engineering and sitting in the Data discipline inside the People (HR) department. The mandate is to run a product squad of People Data Scientists, software engineers and UX designers that turns workforce data into internal "People Products" rather than reports: predictive and prescriptive models, AI agents that surface insights to managers, and a governed people data stack. The role is expected to be technically hands-on with statistics, data wrangling, SQL and scripting, and to act as the translator between HR intent and engineering execution. Remote from AMER or EMEA, with international travel 2-4 times a year for company sprints lasting up to two weeks.

- **Coding:** 25% (estimated) | Posting states no percentage, but requires personal strength in Python/R/JavaScript, software engineering, visualization and automation alongside leading the squad
- Lead and mentor a squad of data scientists, software engineers and UX designers
- Own People Products end to end, bridging HR intent and engineering delivery
- Move people metrics from descriptive to predictive and prescriptive (e.g. attrition risk)
- Lead development of AI agents that surface real-time insights to managers
- Keep the People Data Stack secure, GDPR compliant and the single source of truth
- Identify automation opportunities that free up time for human-touch HR work

## Company

Canonical publishes Ubuntu and provides open source security, support and services spanning devices, clouds, kernels, containers, databases and AI.
