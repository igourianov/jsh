## Summary

Engineering manager to lead the reboot of Landscape, Canonical's systems management solution for Ubuntu. Not greenfield: the current generation already serves thousands of deployments, and the mandate is to step back, drop what no longer matters and re-architect for large-scale enterprise estate management across cloud, bare metal, vSphere, desktops and devices, in a mix of Ubuntu versions and flavours. The product ships both as SaaS and on-prem, with the goal of enterprises running a few Landscape instances covering their global estate. Current stack is Python, PostgreSQL, RabbitMQ, HAProxy and a ReactJS front end, with Golang used more in newer web services work. The role is described as the critical engineering leadership role for that mission: build and grow the team in region, own the roadmap with product management, and stay technically fluent in the stack the team uses. Fully remote, with 4-6 weeks of global travel per year.
