## Company

Employment Hero is an HR technology company founded in 2014, offering an "Employment Operating System" that combines hiring, HR, payroll and benefits in an all-in-one platform. The product is AI-powered, automating compliance and admin tasks across core employment processes.

The company reached a $2 billion valuation, attained unicorn status in 2022, and operates in 6 countries: Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Malaysia, the UK and Canada. It serves over 300,000 businesses and more than 2 million employees (company materials cite up to 350,000 businesses and 2.5 million users), with reported growth around 100% year over year.

Culturally, the company runs on "The EH Way": Mission First, Remote First (async communication, high autonomy), AI First, Apolitical, values-driven and high-performance. It is headquartered in Australia and holds an annual global gathering (Gold Coast, Australia in September 2026).
