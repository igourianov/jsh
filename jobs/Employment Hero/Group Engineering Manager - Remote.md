## Company

Employment Hero is an HR technology company founded in 2014, offering an "Employment Operating System" that combines hiring, HR, payroll and benefits in an all-in-one platform. The product is AI-powered, automating compliance and admin tasks across core employment processes.

The company reached a valuation of over $2 billion, attained unicorn status in 2022, and operates across six countries. It serves over 350,000 SMEs and pays more than 3 million employees, with reported growth around 100% year over year historically.

Culturally, the company runs on "The EH Way": Mission First, Remote First (async communication, high autonomy), AI First, values-driven and high-performance. It holds an annual global gathering (Gold Coast, Australia in September 2026).
