## Company

Hopper is a global travel platform powering its consumer app, website and HTS (Hopper Technology Solutions), its B2B division. By combining a travel agency, e-commerce and proprietary fintech ancillaries (Price Freeze, Cancel for Any Reason, Premium Disruption Assistance), it brings flexibility and savings to travelers. HTS now represents over 75% of the business, supercharging partners' direct channels by integrating Hopper's fintech products or powering end-to-end travel portals. Named partners include Capital One, Air Canada, Uber, Nubank, CommBank and Flair Airlines. The app has 120M+ downloads, 70% Gen-Z/Millennial users, and Hopper is the #3 largest OTA in North America. The company has raised over $750M USD of private capital and is pre-IPO. ~201-500 employees.
