## Company & Product

Nesto Group is Canada's leading provider of mortgage technology and financing solutions, managing $73B+ in residential and commercial mortgages. Founded in 2018, nesto started as Canada's first 100% digital mortgage lender and has evolved into a comprehensive mortgage ecosystem provider.

The company operates two main verticals:
- **B2C (nesto):** Digital mortgage platform for consumers
- **B2B (Nesto Cloud):** Canada's first AI-ready, end-to-end lending platform for financial institutions, credit unions, and commercial real estate lenders

In June 2024, nesto acquired CMLS Group (Canada's third-largest mortgage finance company), doubling the company size to 1,000+ employees across 12 offices. The acquisition created significant operational complexity, reflected in recent employee feedback about leadership and direction.

Founded by CEO Malik Yacoubi, CTO Karim Benabdallah, COO Damien Charbonneau, and Principal Broker Chase Belair. Backed by Diagram Ventures, Portage, NAventures (National Bank), IGM Financial, BMO Capital Partners, Fonds de solidarité FTQ, and Fondaction.

## Engineering Culture

**Team Dynamics:** Engineers emphasize strong camaraderie and cross-team collaboration. Front End Developer Kolby notes "the comradery is unbelievable, and the teams work really well together." Active mentorship culture with senior developers coaching junior staff.

**Learning Environment:** Knowledge-sharing is prioritized through peer learning across experience levels. Team members work closely with Mortgage Brokers, Underwriters, and Customer Success teams.

**Work Environment:** Hybrid model with Thursday social events. Engineers value product impact visibility - "the product we work on at nesto has an impact on people's lives every day."

**Open Source Presence:** Active GitHub organization (nestoca) with 17 public repositories, including **joy** (GitOps CLI for Kubernetes deployments), demonstrating investment in developer experience and infrastructure automation.

**Challenges:** Recent Glassdoor feedback mentions "bad leadership, unsure of direction" post-acquisition, and notes that fast-paced environment with high autonomy "could make it hard on less senior engineers."

## Tech Stack

**Backend:**
- Primary language: **Go (Golang)** - using chi router and sqlx
- Architecture: Loosely coupled, Docker-containerized microservices
- Communication: RESTful APIs and pub-sub queues

**Infrastructure:**
- Cloud: **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**
- Container orchestration: **Kubernetes (GKE)**
- IaC: **Terraform** for programmatic infrastructure management
- GCP services: Storage, Container Registry, Cloud Build, Monitoring, Logging, SQL, Pub/Sub
- Monitoring: **Datadog**
- CI/CD: **Google Cloud Build** and **Codefresh**

**Frontend:**
- **React** with **TypeScript**
- **Redux** for state management
- **Emotion** for styling

**Other Tools:**
- Development: GitHub, GitKraken, Atlassian JIRA
- CDN: Cloudflare, Amazon CloudFront
- Additional: Segment, InVision, Node.js
