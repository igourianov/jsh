## Summary

1Password seeks a Web Development Manager to lead the web development team responsible for building, maintaining, and optimizing the 1Password web platform including marketing website, landing pages, interactive experiences, web infrastructure, and supporting services. Reporting to the Director, Growth Web Experience, the role involves planning, scoping, and delivering high-impact web initiatives against business goals while owning team execution, operational rigor, and technical excellence. Responsibilities include driving reliable high-quality delivery across sprints with predictable velocity, implementing and refining agile development practices, optimizing developer workflow and removing blockers, using engineering metrics to guide planning and continuous improvement, ensuring stable and safe releases through strong QA practices, partnering with Web Experience, Design, and Growth teams to implement A/B tests, new components, SEO improvements and data-driven optimizations, managing and mentoring web developers with clarity of expectations and accountability, setting and driving performance goals focused on Core Web Vitals, accessibility standards, uptime and technical debt reduction, and ensuring compliance with security, privacy, accessibility standards and governance requirements.

## Required Qualifications

- 5-8+ years of experience in web development engineering, with 2+ years managing engineers
- Demonstrated success leading teams that build scalable, performant, secure web experiences using modern frameworks (e.g., React, Node.js) and CMS systems
- Strong technical foundation enabling effective code review, decision-making, and architectural oversight
- Experience driving predictable execution across sprints, releases, and large-scale web initiatives
- Proven ability to collaborate with non-technical stakeholders (Marketing, Design, Product, Growth)
- Ability to create healthy team environment with strong morale, trust, and professional development
- Experience with agile development practices (sprint planning, grooming, standups, retrospectives)
- Knowledge of engineering metrics (cycle time, velocity, backlog health, quality indicators)

## Optional Qualifications
