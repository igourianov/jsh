# Computrition, Inc.

- **Company Type:** Vertical SaaS/Product (foodservice & nutrition management software)
- **Stage:** Mature small business, subsidiary of Constellation Software (Jonas Operating Group)
- **Size:** ~107-125 employees, ~$24.8M revenue
- **Remote Policy:** Role posted as remote (Canada); company HQ in West Hills, CA, with listings also referencing Bedford, MA

## Quick Take
- Title assumption CONFIRMED: "Senior Director of Engineering" sits directly under an existing CTO (Alina Freger) at a ~110-person, ~$25M-revenue company. There is no VP of Engineering layer between this role and the CTO, and the whole company's leadership team uses "Senior Director" as its top individual-function title elsewhere (e.g. Senior Director of Marketing), not as a mid-tier rung under multiple VPs.
- A company this size (total headcount ~110) plausibly has an engineering org in the 20-50 range at most, spanning application engineering, R&D/AI and DevOps per the JD. That is senior-EM/Director scope, not a large-org VP-adjacent position with directors/managers reporting up through this seat.
- Classic Constellation/Jonas playbook: small, profitable, slow-growth vertical software business, decentralized operating model, minimal corporate glamour. Title inflation relative to scope is a known pattern in this portfolio (small BUs give senior titles because there's no room for a normal ladder).
- Glassdoor signal (small sample, n=15) flags weak upper management and "complete train wreck" development department — worth probing in interviews given this role reports into that same leadership chain.

## Milestones
- Founded 1981 as a foodservice/nutrition/retail solutions provider (per Jonas Software profile).
- Acquired by Constellation Software on 2007-11-30; now operates within Constellation's Jonas Software operating group.
- Former COO Kim Goldberg moved up to Group Leader at Jonas Software — an example of the internal-mobility path Constellation/Jonas uses instead of building deep local management benches.
- Current President: Scott Saklad.
- Current CTO: Alina Freger; Chief Architect/Senior Director of R&D also present in leadership — meaning R&D/architecture leadership already exists separately from this engineering-org-facing hire.

## Company & Product
Computrition builds foodservice and nutrition-management software (flagship product line often referenced as CNS4/Computrition Nutrition System) for healthcare, long-term care, higher education and other institutional foodservice operations — menu planning, recipe management, inventory control, patient meal ordering, and nutritional analysis. It has been owned by Constellation Software since 2007 and is run within the Jonas Software operating group, which follows Constellation's standard decentralized "buy and hold forever" model: acquired vertical-market software companies keep operating independently with their own P&L, minimal centralized engineering standards, and no pressure to chase hypergrowth.

## Engineering Culture
No public engineering blog, no visible GitHub org, no conference talks or published engineering practices found. This is typical for a Constellation/Jonas portfolio company — engineering culture is internal and largely undocumented externally. The JD's emphasis on introducing engineering KPIs (velocity, cycle time, defect rate, MTTR), CI/CD maturity, and architectural governance reads as "these don't really exist yet in a mature form" rather than "harden what's already sophisticated" — consistent with a legacy, under-instrumented engineering shop that a new senior leader is being brought in to modernize.

## Tech Stack
.NET/C#, React (per JD), Oracle database (per JD). Public tech-intelligence sources (Sumble, Crunchbase) confirm C#/.NET usage but had no independent detail on the Oracle/React combination beyond what the job posting itself states. No evidence of cloud-native or modern data-platform stack; this reads as a long-lived on-prem/enterprise-style stack being modernized rather than a greenfield SaaS architecture.

## Team Health
- Glassdoor: 3.1/5 overall (15 reviews), only 30% would recommend to a friend, only 26% positive business outlook.
- Negative themes: "upper management are mostly incompetent," "the president... has no idea how to run a company," "development department is a complete train wreck," wasted money, backstabbing culture, broken training promises.
- Positive themes: approachable management, work-life flexibility, some strong individual colleagues.
- Sample size is small (15 reviews), so weight this directionally, not definitively — but the tone is consistently negative on leadership/execution rather than mixed.

## Business Stability
Owned by Constellation Software (TSX: CSU.TO) via Jonas Software since 2007 — financially stable at the parent level, effectively insulated from startup-style existential risk (funding runway, sudden shutdown). Constellation's model is buy-and-hold with light-touch centralized control, so Computrition itself is not likely to be sold, downsized dramatically, or shut down, but is also not likely to receive large investment for aggressive modernization. Revenue (~$24.8M) and headcount (~110) suggest a flat, mature, cash-generative small business rather than a growth story.

## Red Flags
- Glassdoor reviews describe weak, incompetent upper leadership and a dysfunctional development department — same leadership chain (President, CTO) this role reports through.
- Title-to-scope mismatch: "Senior Director of Engineering" at a ~110-person company with an existing CTO and existing Chief Architect/Sr. Director of R&D suggests this is a working-level engineering leadership role (comparable to a senior EM/Director elsewhere) rather than a VP-adjacent strategic seat with real organizational breadth or multiple management layers beneath it.
- No visible engineering blog, open-source presence, or published modern practices — suggests immature engineering culture that the JD's "introduce KPIs / CI-CD maturity / architectural governance" language is trying to paper over as a forward-looking mandate.
- Healthcare/compliance requirements (HIPAA, FHIR, HL7) paired with an Oracle-based legacy stack and small team size increase the risk of this being a heavy tech-debt remediation role disguised as an "AI-first strategy" role.
