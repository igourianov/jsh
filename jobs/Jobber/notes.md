## Lessons

- Application forms have twice asked about willingness to be in-office 1 day/week (hub cities: Edmonton, Toronto, Vancouver, Kitchener-Waterloo) despite the posting text stating the role is fully remote across Canada. Seen on both "Fintech New Bets" and "General" postings. Expect this on future Jobber applications and clarify with the recruiter early rather than assume it's a formality.
