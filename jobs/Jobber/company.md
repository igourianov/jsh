# Jobber

- **Company Type:** SaaS
- **Stage:** Scale-up (late-stage VC)
- **Size:** 600+ employees (Canada, US, Latin America), per 2023 reports
- **Remote Policy:** Remote across Canada per postings, but application forms ask about 1 day/week in-office at hub cities (Edmonton, Toronto, Vancouver, Kitchener-Waterloo). See notes.md.

## Quick Take
- Profitable-growth narrative: 2022 revenue over $100M USD, no layoffs reported through the 2023 downturn.
- Rails monolith with React/React Native and GraphQL. Strong developer-productivity investment (Dev Acceleration team).
- Glassdoor engineering sentiment is poor (1.9/5 from 18 engineer reviews): deep management layers, long-tenured gatekeepers, fading autonomy. Small sample and dated, verify in interviews.

## Milestones
- Founded in Edmonton, Alberta (seed round 2013). Product for home service businesses (quoting, scheduling, invoicing, payments).
- 2021: Series C, $60M USD.
- 2023-02-07: Series D, $100M USD led by General Atlantic, with Summit Partners, Version One Ventures and Tech Pioneers Fund. Total raised $176M USD. Largest VC round in Edmonton history.
- Since then: expansion into Payments, AI/ML, business workflows and communications, and fintech bets (matches current postings). No 2024-2026 funding, acquisition or layoff news found in search.

## Company & Product
Field service management software for small home service businesses (lawn care, cleaning, HVAC and similar). Over 200,000 service professionals in 60 countries. Revenue from subscriptions plus payments and add-ons. Tech org spans Payments, Infrastructure, AI/ML, Business Workflows and Communications.

## Engineering Culture
- "Developer obsession" framing alongside customer obsession. Dedicated Dev Acceleration team with mission to help engineers ship faster (speed, efficiency, clarity, autonomy).
- CI/CD time cut by nearly 50% (20 to 12 minutes) while adding tests.
- Public engineering writing on DEV Community and GitHub org (getjobber). Moderate visibility, not a heavy open source presence.
- Iterative, small-bite approach to platform work.

## Tech Stack
- Backend: Ruby on Rails monolith
- Frontend: React, React Native
- API: GraphQL
- Testing: RSpec (40,000+ tests), Jest (10,000+ tests)
- Infrastructure: AWS (EC2), Cloudflare

## Team Health
- Glassdoor (software engineers): 1.9/5 across 18 reviews, compensation 2.7/5. Overall company has around 300 reviews.
- Negatives: up to 5 layers between a developer and the CTO, top-down mandates, a few long-tenured engineers gatekeeping technical decisions and leaving half-implemented initiatives, fading autonomy, disorganization.
- Positives: work-life balance described as great, plenty of support resources.

## Business Stability
Well funded (Series D 2023, $176M total) with over $100M USD revenue in 2022 and reported growth. No layoffs found. Private, so no public financials. Backed by growth equity investors, so an exit or IPO is a likely eventual driver.

## Red Flags
- Engineering Glassdoor sentiment and management layering, relevant to a management role (span of control, autonomy for managers).
- Rails monolith with entrenched senior gatekeepers may constrain change.
- Remote policy ambiguity (in-office question on forms despite remote postings).
- Reviews are a small sample and may predate current leadership.
