## Company

Assembled is a customer-support operations platform that orchestrates human agents, BPOs and AI in a single system. Its products include AI Agents that resolve cases end-to-end, an AI Copilot for agent assistance and AI-powered workforce management that optimizes both human and AI capacity. Customers include Canva, Etsy, Robinhood and Stripe. The company is backed by $71M from NEA, Emergence Capital and Stripe, with 51-200 employees. The Forecasting and Scheduling team powers a core part of the product, shaping how tens of thousands of agents get staffed and how hundreds of millions in support spend is allocated.
