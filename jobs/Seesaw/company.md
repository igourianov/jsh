# Seesaw

- **Company Type:** SaaS/Product (K-12 learning platform)
- **Stage:** Scale-up (PE-backed)
- **Size:** ~220-300 (third-party estimates, unreliable)
- **Remote Policy:** Remote hiring in Canada, USA and UK per the careers page

## Quick Take
- Mature, widely used K-12 product (25M+ users claimed) with a PE owner and a small, selective engineering org.
- Growth is now partly acquisition-led (Little Thinking Minds, Arabic literacy, 2025), which points to MENA expansion, multi-region and data-residency work, matching the Core Systems JD.
- Concern: little public engineering signal (no blog, no open source) and conflicting headcount data. Verify team size, reporting line and PE exit horizon with the recruiter.

## Milestones
- 2007: Founded by Adrian Graham and Carl Sjogreen. Product launched as a student portfolio app.
- 2021-10: Strategic investment from Providence Equity Partners.
- 2021: Joined the AWS Partner Network, listed in AWS Marketplace.
- 2022-09: Messages feature shut down temporarily after accounts were compromised and an inappropriate image was sent to staff via parent accounts. Passwords reset, feature re-enabled.
- 2024: Product relaunch and rebrand ("keeping you in the learning loop").
- 2025-04-15: Acquired Little Thinking Minds (Jordan, Arabic literacy, founded 2004). First Arabic-language platform planned for 2026.

## Company & Product
Seesaw is a PreK-12 learning platform for teachers, students and families: classroom activities, portfolios, assessment and family communication. It claims more than 25 million educators, students and families worldwide (2025), up from 10M+ monthly users and 75%+ of US schools cited in 2022. Revenue comes from school and district licenses, with a free tier for individual teachers. Following the LTM acquisition it serves 800K+ users at 3,000+ schools, ministries and school groups in MENA. AI-driven learning tools are part of the stated strategy.

- PE-owned since 2021 (Providence Equity Partners). Expect a growth-and-exit orientation, and a possible sale or further consolidation in the next few years.
- Acquisitive in 2025, which signals available capital.
- No layoff news found.

## Red Flags
