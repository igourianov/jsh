## Company

Nearform is an independent digital consultancy of roughly 500 people across 20+ countries, built around open source and now positioning itself as an AI-native engineering partner. It fuses data, engineering and design teams to build intelligent digital solutions, modernize legacy systems and launch new product ventures for large enterprises.
