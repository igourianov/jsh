## Company

Portless is an early-stage (11-50 employees) logistics and supply chain startup reshaping cross-border e-commerce fulfillment. Its model decouples brands from traditional domestic warehousing by shipping orders directly from manufacturing hubs in China to global consumers in as little as 3-5 days, lowering shipping costs and import tax fees. The company reports having shipped over 2.5 million cross-border packages with 99.8% pick-and-pack accuracy and 98% on-time delivery, and positions itself on white-glove customer service and fast response times. Engineering runs a 24/7, globally distributed operation across the USA, Canada, EU, UK and China, building live logistics and supply chain systems with real-world physical dependencies (shipping, tracking, customs). The company describes itself as growing fast, remote-first and asynchronous-friendly.
