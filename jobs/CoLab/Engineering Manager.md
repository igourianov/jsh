## Company

CoLab is a St. John's, Newfoundland based B2B SaaS company building a cloud platform for mechanical engineering design review. The product lets subject matter experts access, evaluate and comment on 2D drawings and 3D CAD models collaboratively, with an AI peer checker called AutoReview that spots common errors and non-compliance with standards. Every review builds a knowledge repository of feedback, decisions and lessons learned that AI agents draw on to flag issues in future designs. Customers include Ford, Komatsu, Johnson Controls, Schaeffler and Polaris, who report outcomes like 40% faster launches, 50% BOM cost cuts and 15% fewer quality escapes in a year. Founded with its first customer in 2019, CoLab has been recognized on Deloitte's Fast 50 and Fast 500 lists and named a Canadian company to watch by The Globe and Mail and Financial Post. The company is remote-first across Canada with optional hybrid work from St. John's. Headcount is 51-200.
