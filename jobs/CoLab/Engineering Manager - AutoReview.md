## Summary

Engineering Manager leading the team that builds AutoReview, CoLab's AI-powered automation of mechanical design review. People leadership role with enough technical depth to pressure-test approaches and estimates. Works with Product to turn broad ideas into a roadmap, makes early feasibility and scope calls, and gives engineers context and autonomy. ## Company

CoLab is a St. John's, Newfoundland based B2B SaaS company building AI for mechanical engineering teams that design cars, medical devices and jet engines. Its platform is where design reviews happen, capturing the rationale behind every change, and its AI uses that history to help manufacturers make better decisions, cut rework cycles and reach market faster. Customers include Bobcat, Triumph Motorcycles and GE Appliances, and it has multi-year AI partnerships with companies such as Bombardier. Started with one customer in 2018, raised a $72M Series C, ranked 57th on Deloitte's Fast 500 and has grown to 250+ people.
