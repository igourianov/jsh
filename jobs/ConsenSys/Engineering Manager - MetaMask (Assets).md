## Company

ConsenSys is a leading blockchain and Web3 software company founded in 2014 by Ethereum co-founder Joe Lubin. The company develops major blockchain products including MetaMask (crypto wallet used by millions), Infura (blockchain infrastructure), Linea (Layer 2 scaling solution), Diligence (smart contract security), and Phosphor (institutional custody). MetaMask is one of the most popular cryptocurrency wallets enabling users to interact with Ethereum blockchain and decentralized applications. ConsenSys operates as a remote-first company with teams across 6 continents, offering competitive benefits including equity compensation, unlimited vacation, flexible working arrangements, and continuous learning platform access. The company emphasizes diversity and innovation in the Web3 space.
