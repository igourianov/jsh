# Guidewire

- **Company Type:** SaaS / Platform (industry platform for P&C insurance)
- **Stage:** Public, mature (NYSE: GWRE)
- **Size:** ~4,066 employees (March 2026), 27 locations worldwide; workforce concentrated in North America (49.6%), South Asia (18.9%), Northern Europe (13.6%)
- **Remote Policy:** Hybrid/remote options vary by team; corporate "Go Flex Global" program allows working from another country up to 30 workdays per 12 months. Some teams report a strict 3-days-in-office WFO policy with pushback on WFH.

## Quick Take
- Financially strong and growing: FY2026 Q3 revenue $372.5M, up 27% YoY, ARR $1.147B (+19% YoY), beating earnings estimates.
- Long, successful cloud transition (InsuranceSuite -> Guidewire Cloud) now largely complete: subscription/support is 66% of revenue.
- Glassdoor sentiment is generally positive (flat hierarchy, good WLB on paper) but with notable team-dependent variance in management quality and remote-work flexibility.

## Milestones
- Founded 2001, headquartered San Mateo, CA.
- IPO January 25, 2012 at $13/share, raised $115M (NYSE: GWRE).
- FY2012: 35% revenue growth, 130 core-system customers.
- 2017: Acquired Cyence (cyber risk modeling/data science), signaling shift toward analytics.
- Multi-year refactor of InsuranceSuite into Guidewire Cloud (SaaS), which caused a temporary revenue-recognition "J-curve" as license revenue shifted to subscription.
- November 7, 2025: Acquired ProNav Technologies (Canada-based knowledge management platform for P&C insurance) for ~$33.4M cash.
- FY2026 Q3 (reported June 2026): revenue $372.5M (+27% YoY), ARR $1.147B (+19% YoY), GAAP EPS $0.82 beating consensus by 10.8%; some deal-timing slippage affected ARR growth in the quarter.

## Company & Product
Guidewire provides an industry cloud platform (InsuranceSuite: PolicyCenter, ClaimCenter, BillingCenter) for property & casualty (P&C) insurance carriers globally. Now largely a SaaS company after a multi-year cloud migration, with subscription and support making up two-thirds of revenue. Full year FY2026 guidance: total revenue $1.46-1.47B, ending ARR $1.229-1.237B.

## Engineering Culture
Maintains an active engineering blog (medium.com/guidewire-engineering-blog) featuring posts from India (Bengaluru) engineering teams on platform scaling, career growth (intern-to-engineer stories), and team culture. Content suggests a large and growing India-based engineering organization. Publishes an annual "Developer Trends and Insights Report" covering AI career paths and tech stacks. No major open-source presence found.

## Tech Stack
- **Languages:** Go (primary, cloud/server-side), JavaScript/TypeScript (jsPolicies policy engine, Sentinel UI)
- **Containerization/Orchestration:** Docker, Kubernetes
- **CI/CD:** TeamCity
- **Monitoring:** Datadog
- **Data/Streaming:** Kafka, Kafka Connect, Debezium
- **Cloud:** AWS (Lambda, ECS, S3, CloudFormation)

## Team Health
Glassdoor: Software Engineer work-life-balance rating 4.5/5; overall company work-life-balance 4.2/5. Reviews describe a positive, collaborative culture with a flat organizational hierarchy. Concerns raised in some reviews about inconsistent management quality: reports of "toxic managers" pushing always-online expectations, a strict 3-day return-to-office policy on some teams, and discouragement of WFH tied to performance perception. Overall sentiment strong but team-dependent.

## Business Stability
Public company (NYSE: GWRE), financially healthy: revenue growing 27% YoY, ARR growing ~19-20% YoY, profitable on non-GAAP basis with GAAP operating income guided at $124-134M for FY2026. Recently completed a small tuck-in acquisition (ProNav, Nov 2025). No confirmed reports of company-wide layoffs found in this research, though broader 2026 US tech layoff wave context exists and some Glassdoor reviews reference past layoffs affecting morale (undated/unspecific).

## Red Flags
- Team-dependent management quality: some reports of toxic/micromanaging managers and RTO friction despite official flexible-work messaging.
- Employee discussions on Blind reference layoff concerns/job security as recently as April 2026, though no specific confirmed Guidewire layoff event was found in this research; worth probing directly if interviewing.
