# AssetWorks Inc

- **Company Type:** Product (vertical SaaS and on-prem, enterprise asset management)
- **Stage:** Enterprise (portfolio company of Volaris Group / Constellation Software)
- **Size:** About 337 employees (Built In, unverified, may be stale)
- **Remote Policy:** Posting is remote (United States or Canada). Offices in Berwyn PA, San Antonio TX, Pittsburgh PA, Glendale CA, Jenkintown PA, Calgary AB and Chippenham UK.

## Quick Take
- Direct employer, not a recruiting agency.
- Mature, low-profile vertical software vendor with 1,000+ public and private sector customers. Held long term by Constellation Software, so stability is high and growth is slow.
- Glassdoor is mediocre (about 3.4-3.5, 62-64% recommend) with themes of weak leadership, vague milestones and confusing management structure. Public information on engineering culture is thin.

## Milestones
- Claims 40+ years in business (own site). Built In lists 1991 as founding year. Third-party sources disagree.
- 2018-07-10: AssetWorks LLC (Pittsburgh) acquired E-Innovative Services Group (Baltimore), maker of eQuip!, strong in aerospace and defense, also state and local government and higher education.
- Portfolio company of Constellation Software (TSX: CSU), via Volaris Group per the job posting. Acquisition date of AssetWorks by Constellation not found.
- No layoffs, funding rounds or major leadership news found for 2025-2026.

## Company & Product
Enterprise asset management vendor for asset and infrastructure-intensive organizations: fleets, buildings, equipment and infrastructure. Customers are mostly cities, counties and state and federal agencies.

- **FleetFocus:** web-based fleet and equipment management. Preventive maintenance, inventory control, fuel data integration (FuelFocus), 200+ standard reports.
- **EAM:** facilities and building operations. Inspections, warranty, compliance. Esri GIS integration and offline mobile workforce.
- **eQuip! (E-ISG):** asset lifecycle tracking, mobile inventories, FAR compliance reporting.
- Also real estate portfolio module, hardware and consulting services.

## Engineering Culture
- No engineering blog, open source organization or public engineering content found.
- The posting describes an AI-first team: over 80% of code AI-generated through spec-driven development with Claude and GitHub Copilot, with a mandate to move to agent-driven development and measure the results.
- One 2023 Indeed review (sales employee) called the development team below average. Single anecdote.

## Tech Stack
- C# and .NET, Angular (or React), TypeScript, Oracle and Microsoft SQL Server (from the posting).
- Legacy product modernization is part of the role.
- AI tooling: Claude, GitHub Copilot.

## Team Health
- Glassdoor: about 3.4-3.5 across about 103 reviews, 62-64% recommend. Recent reviews cite a team overwhelmed at times, confusing management structure, weak leadership and vague milestones. Remote option is a plus.
- Blind: 3.3 across 4 reviews. Work-life balance highest, career growth lowest (2.5).
- Indeed: 3.7 across 19 reviews.
- No engineering-specific rating found. Glassdoor page was not fetchable (HTTP 403), so figures come from search summaries.

## Business Stability
- Backed by Constellation Software, which acquires and holds vertical market software long term. Low risk of closure, moderate risk of cost focus, limited investment in new product and little autonomy over budgets typical of the model.
- Sticky public sector customer base with recurring revenue.
- No layoff reports found. Still hiring as of 2026-07.

## Red Flags
- Glassdoor themes of weak leadership and unclear milestones.
- Low career growth score on Blind.
- Little visibility into engineering culture or team turnover.
- Constellation model: small ownership of decisions, M&A-driven product portfolio (FleetFocus, EAM, eQuip!) likely means fragmented codebases.
- Dated or conflicting company facts online (founding year, HQ).
