## Company

Forward Financing, headquartered in Boston, provides revenue-based financing to underserved small businesses across the United States. Operating since 2012, the company has distributed over $4.1 billion in financing to more than 80,000 businesses. As a FinTech company in the regulated Financial Services space, Forward Financing focuses on serving small businesses that traditional lenders often overlook. The company has been recognized as a "Best Place to Work" by Built In Boston and is Great Place to Work certified. Forward Financing emphasizes a remote-first culture with flexible PTO, comprehensive benefits including medical, dental, vision, paid parental leave, 401k match (US employees), wellness reimbursement, volunteering days, professional development budget, and charitable donation matching. The engineering organization builds and maintains cloud-native platforms on AWS, API-driven backend systems, and manages Salesforce as a mission-critical CRM/ERP platform.
