## Company & Product
Forward Financing provides revenue-based financing to underserved small businesses across the US. They offer fast, flexible working capital in exchange for a percentage of future revenue. The company has funded over $4.5 billion to approximately 87,000 small businesses since founding.

The business model focuses on using proprietary technology and machine learning to assess creditworthiness of businesses that traditional banks often reject. They emphasize same-day funding capability.

Headquartered in Boston with an operational hub in Santiago, Dominican Republic, and team members across the US and Canada. The company is 100% employee-owned and has been consistently profitable.
