# SOTI

- **Company Type:** SaaS/Product (Enterprise Mobility Management)
- **Stage:** Enterprise (private, founder-owned)
- **Size:** ~2,000 employees globally, ~663 in engineering/product (based on tracked data)
- **Remote Policy:** Hybrid (varies by team). Some roles offer full remote, but most engineering roles are hybrid out of Mississauga, ON.

## Quick Take
- Profitable for 29 consecutive years with $357M revenue. 100% founder-owned by Carl Rodrigues and his wife. No external investors.
- Glassdoor rating is 3.1/5 (declining). Recurring themes: chaotic dev process, outdated tech, weak management. Only 49% recommend to a friend.
- Strong product-market fit in enterprise mobility (17,000+ customers, 170 countries) but engineering culture raises concerns.

## Milestones
- **1995** - Founded by Carl Rodrigues in his basement in Canada as a mobile phone control solution
- **2006** - Rejected acquisition offer from Microsoft, chose to remain independent
- **2024 (Oct)** - Opened new $42M CAD global HQ at 1950 Meadowvale Blvd, Mississauga
- **2025 (Aug)** - SOTI SYNC 25 conference held in Montreal
- **2025 (Dec)** - Released SOTI Identity 2026.0, SOTI Connect 2026.0, SOTI Snap 2026.0 with AI capabilities

## Company & Product
SOTI is a Canadian enterprise software company specializing in mobility management solutions. Their core platform, SOTI ONE, includes:

- **SOTI MobiControl** - Enterprise Mobility Management (EMM) for multi-vendor device management
- **SOTI Snap** - Rapid mobile app development for digitizing business processes
- **SOTI XSight** - Device support, analytics and diagnostics
- **SOTI Connect** - IoT and printer lifecycle management
- **SOTI Identity** - Access management

They serve sectors like healthcare, retail, transportation/logistics, field services and emergency services. Revenue is generated through direct sales and a partner channel (resellers, SIs, OEMs).

## Engineering Culture
- No dedicated engineering blog. The company blog at soti.net/resources/blog is product/marketing-focused with occasional tech trend articles.
- Minimal open source presence. The GitHub organization (github.com/soti) has a few repos (GoCD cookbook, PowerShellGet) with no public members listed.
- Glassdoor reviews from engineers describe the development process as "chaotic" with "frequent overtime" and "inconsistent compensation for extra hours."
- Interview process reported as "disorganized and unstructured" with "repeated deep dives lacking clear direction."
- No visible investment in developer relations, tech talks or engineering thought leadership.

## Tech Stack
- **Backend:** C#, .NET (primary), .NET 6, .NET Framework 4.8
- **Frontend:** Angular (mentioned in Full Stack Developer job postings)
- **Database/Search:** Elasticsearch (for device search)
- **Java:** OpenJDK 17 (used for specific components)
- **Testing:** Selenium, Cypress, Playwright, RestAssured, JUnit, TestNG
- **CI/CD:** Jenkins, GitLab CI, Azure DevOps, CircleCI (varies by team)
- **Cloud:** AWS and Azure (both mentioned in job requirements)
- **Infrastructure:** Windows Server-based deployments for on-premises, cloud options available

## Team Health
- **Glassdoor:** 3.1/5 overall (down 10% in last 12 months). Software Engineers rate 3.0/5.
- **Compensation:** 2.8/5 rating on Glassdoor (down 9% in last 12 months)
- **Positives:** Helpful colleagues, engaging work, learning opportunities, family-type culture
- **Negatives:** Management lacks technical competence, chaotic development process, outdated technology stack, poor growth for non-favorites, frequent overtime
- **Recommend to friend:** 49%
- Employee count declined ~2% from Nov 2023 to Nov 2025

## Business Stability
- 29 consecutive years of profitability. $357M revenue.
- Privately held, no debt or VC pressure. 100% founder-owned.
- No layoffs reported in 2025-2026 tech layoff trackers.
- Investing in new HQ ($42M acquisition) and AI features suggests confidence in continued growth.
- Global presence with 12 offices across Canada, US, UK, Ireland, Sweden, India, Australia, Japan, UAE, Netherlands.

## Red Flags
- **Declining Glassdoor scores.** Overall rating dropped 10%, compensation rating dropped 9% in the past year.
- **Chaotic engineering process.** Multiple reviews cite disorganized development, frequent overtime and poor project management.
- **Management concerns.** Reviews note management lacks technical competence, and growth opportunities favor "favorites."
- **Outdated tech stack.** Engineers report legacy technology and slow modernization.
- **Founder-controlled.** 100% ownership by one person means all strategic decisions are centralized. This can be a strength or risk depending on leadership quality.
- **Slight employee decline.** 2% headcount reduction over two years. Not alarming but worth monitoring.
