## Summary

Engineering Manager role for the Platform Team at Onebrief, a defense-tech company building AI-powered collaboration software for military command staffs. The platform team owns shared infrastructure services (databases, data streaming, tooling) that serve the entire engineering organization. The EM is the operational owner of this layer and is responsible for team leadership, platform reliability, IaC standards, GitOps workflows, incident response and cross-functional stakeholder alignment with DevOps, AppSec and Engineering leadership.

- Lead and mentor Platform Engineers; remove blockers and own team growth
- Partner with Cybersecurity, Product and Engineering on secure, high-velocity delivery into cloud-native and air-gapped environments
- Drive standards for IaC, service onboarding, environment management and GitOps
- Ensure observability, reliability, scalability and cost-effectiveness of the platform
- Own incident management, AARs, runbooks and operational maturity improvements
- Act as architecture stakeholder for platform integration across engineering
