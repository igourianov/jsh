## Summary

Engineering Manager for the Delivery Team at Onebrief, a defense-tech company. The Delivery Team is a cross-functional group of Platform, Software and Application Security Engineers that owns every system moving software from development into production. The EM owns the delivery pipeline end to end: release orchestration, CI/CD, environment management and security automation, with the goal of letting Onebrief deploy reliably into mission-critical and air-gapped environments. The role reports to the Director of Infrastructure and is judged on whether engineers can ship confidently, deployments behave predictably across environments and software stays secure and compliant.

- **Coding:** 0% - No core programming language is required; the role centers on owning delivery systems, standards and team leadership. Software engineering background is only a nice-to-have.
- Lead and develop a multidisciplinary team of Platform, Software and Application Security Engineers
- Own CI/CD, release automation and environment management across the company
- Establish standards for tested releases, rollback procedures and operational readiness
- Integrate security controls into the SDLC (SAST, DAST, dependency scanning, artifact integrity, policy enforcement)
- Reduce deployment risk, shorten recovery times and set expectations for release quality, uptime and incident response
- Partner with technical teams on reusable tooling, deployment patterns and repeatable workflows
- Build systems for traceability, access control, change management and compliance across regulated customer environments

## Company

Onebrief is a defense-tech company founded in 2019 that builds AI-powered collaboration and workflow software for military command staffs. The product is positioned as "the operating system for modern command" and runs on NIPR, SIPR and JWICS (the unclassified, secret and top-secret US military networks). The company has raised $320M+ from top-tier investors including Battery Ventures, General Catalyst, Sapphire Ventures, Insight Partners and Human Capital, and is valued at $2.15B. The team blends military veterans from across the forces with engineers from leading software companies. Onebrief operates as an all-remote company, though many employees work alongside customers at military commands around the world.
