# OpenLoop Health - Company Research

## Executive Summary

OpenLoop Health is a telehealth company founded in January 2020 by Dr. Jon Lensing (CEO) and Christian Williams (COO). The company provides full-stack, white-labeled telehealth infrastructure to healthcare organizations, enabling scalable virtual care delivery across all 50 states. OpenLoop is valued at over $1 billion and has raised over $120M in funding, with significant investments in AI technology throughout 2024.

**Headquarters**: Des Moines, Iowa
**Founded**: January 2020
**Valuation**: $1B+ (reported)
**Total Funding**: $120M+ (various sources report $26.1M - $48.5M in tracked funding)
**Employee Count**: 111-216 (fluctuating due to rapid growth)
**Network**: 6,000+ clinicians across 30+ specialties in all 50 states

---

## Products & Platform

### Core Offering

OpenLoop provides a modular, white-label telehealth platform that serves as an operating system for healthcare delivery. The platform enables healthcare organizations to launch and scale compliant telehealth services without building infrastructure from scratch.

**Platform Components:**
- Comprehensive sync & async telehealth capabilities
- API-driven integration architecture
- EHR connectivity and data exchange
- ePrescribing functionality
- Lab ordering and results management
- Clinician management and scheduling
- Data analytics and reporting
- Revenue cycle management (RCM)
- Patient intake automation

**Key Differentiators:**
- Full-stack solution (combines technology platform + clinical network)
- White-label/custom-branded for partners
- HIPAA-compliant and SOC 2 certified
- Plug-and-play integrations
- Coverage across all 50 states
- 30+ specialty areas supported

### Platform Architecture

OpenLoop's platform features a flexible tech stack that connects multiple healthcare systems:
- **EHR Integration**: API-driven connections to electronic health records
- **FHIR Support**: Implements Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources (FHIR) standards for seamless data exchange
- **API-First Design**: Intuitive, API-driven technology allows partners to manage patients and create care plans
- **Modular Design**: Clients can choose à-la-carte services or full-stack solutions
- **Security**: HIPAA-compliant with SOC 2 certification

## Technical Challenges & Engineering Focus

### Core Technical Challenges

1. **Healthcare Compliance & Security**
   - HIPAA compliance requirements across all systems
   - SOC 2 certification maintenance
   - Secure API integrations with sensitive patient data
   - Data isolation in multi-tenant architecture

2. **Scalability**
   - Supporting 120+ active clients with different needs
   - Managing 6,000+ clinicians across the network
   - White-label customization for each partner
   - Multi-tenant architecture requiring secure data segmentation

3. **Interoperability**
   - Integration with diverse EHR systems
   - FHIR/HL7 standards implementation
   - API design for seamless third-party integrations
   - Real-time data synchronization across systems

4. **Telemedicine-Specific Challenges**
   - Low-latency video conferencing infrastructure
   - State-by-state regulatory compliance (50 states)
   - ePrescribing integration and compliance
   - Real-time clinician availability and scheduling

5. **AI Integration**
   - Automating clinical intake workflows
   - Natural language processing for patient support chatbots
   - Predictive analytics for scheduling optimization
   - Voice AI for administrative task automation

### Engineering Priorities (2024-2025)

Based on recent company announcements and job postings:

- **AI Infrastructure**: Building foundational AI capabilities for care delivery at scale

## AI Strategy: HeyRevia Partnership

### About HeyRevia

**HeyRevia** is NOT an internal OpenLoop AI lab, but rather a separate Y Combinator S24 company that has partnered with OpenLoop.

**HeyRevia Details:**
- **Focus**: Voice AI to automate complex phone calls for healthcare providers
- **Founders**: Shaun Wei and David (Wenbo) Zhu (ex-Google AI engineers)
- **Y Combinator**: Summer 2024 batch
- **Product**: HIPAA-compliant AI agents for handling administrative calls
- **Use Cases**:
  - Insurance verification calls
  - Pharmacy coordination
  - Appointment scheduling
  - Patient follow-ups

**OpenLoop Connection:**
- Partnership announced for integrating voice AI into OpenLoop's platform
- CEO Jon Lensing participated in discussions about future of healthcare AI
- Potential integration into OpenLoop's AI Contact Center initiative

### OpenLoop's Broader AI Strategy (2024)

OpenLoop has made significant AI investments across multiple areas:

1. **Clinical Operations Automation**
   - AI-powered patient intake
   - Demographic data collection and routing
   - Care navigation chatbots

2. **Administrative Automation**
   - RCM (Revenue Cycle Management) optimization
   - Clinician scheduling automation
   - Documentation assistance

3. **Future Roadmap**
   - AI shifting from "hype to infrastructure"
   - Automation of tedious medical tasks (documentation, billing)
   - Allowing providers to focus on patient care vs. admin work
