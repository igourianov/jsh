- **Company Type:** SaaS / Platform (white-label digital health infrastructure)
- **Stage:** Scale-up (Series A, $15M raised)
- **Size:** ~136 corporate employees + 1,200 across provider network and four countries
- **Remote Policy:** Hybrid/flexible. Corporate HQ in Des Moines, IA. Engineering hubs in Nashville, TN and San Jose, CA (in-office). Toronto hub being established. Some roles listed as remote (Senior Engineering Manager, Technical Lead).

## Company & Product
OpenLoop is a white-label digital health infrastructure provider headquartered in Des Moines, Iowa. They provide the complete backend for organizations launching or scaling virtual care services. Their platform serves as the clinical and operational backbone for telehealth delivery.

**Core offerings:**
- HIPAA-compliant, customizable EHR/EMR platform
- Provider staffing network (30+ specialties, 15+ languages, 50-state coverage)
- ePrescribing and eLabs
- Scheduling and appointment management
- SOAP note charting
- Payment processing and revenue cycle management
- Licensing, credentialing (NCQA-accredited) and regulatory compliance
- Diagnostic imaging telehealth
- 100+ clinical protocols
- AI integration for clinical workflows

**Clients** include healthcare organizations, employers, retailers and consumer brands looking to add virtual care without building infrastructure from scratch. The platform supports rapid deployment (48 hours for out-of-box launch).

- **Funding:** $15M Series A (March 2023). No publicly announced subsequent rounds.
- **Growth:** Impressive growth metrics (5x YoY in 2025, 700% in 2024) but largely self-reported without independent verification.
- **Revenue model:** B2B SaaS/platform. White-label services to healthcare organizations.
- **Market position:** Claims to be the only full-service white-labeled digital health infrastructure provider.
- **Recognition:** TIME's World's Top HealthTech Companies (2025), 4.9 Trustpilot rating.
- **Risks:** Operating on Series A funding with aggressive growth. No announced follow-on funding in nearly 3 years. Data breach and active litigation create financial and reputational risk.

## Red Flags
- **Data breach (January 2026):** 1.6M health records exposed. Two class action lawsuits filed. For a healthcare infrastructure company, this is a fundamental trust issue with clients and patients.
- **Leadership instability:** Glassdoor reviews consistently cite leadership problems. "Upper management is completely clueless" and "management is just full of empty promises" are recurring themes.
- **High turnover signals:** Reviews mention frequent hiring and firing. Multiple senior engineering leadership roles open simultaneously could indicate turnover at the top.
- **Lack of structure:** Multiple reviews describe "zero structure" and constantly changing direction. This can be expected at a scale-up but the frequency of these complaints is notable.
- **Funding gap:** No announced funding since March 2023 despite rapid growth claims. Could indicate healthy revenue, or difficulty raising at desired valuations.
- **Toronto hub uncertainty:** The Engineering Manager role describes building a Toronto hub from scratch, but OpenLoop operates via Rippling EOR in Canada rather than a local entity. This could mean limited local investment and uncertain long-term commitment.
- **reCAPTCHA solver repo:** An archived public repo for bypassing reCAPTCHA raises questions about engineering judgment and ethics.
