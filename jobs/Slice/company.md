# Slice

- **Company Type:** Platform / Restaurant Tech SaaS and marketplace
- **Stage:** Scale-up (venture-backed, private)
- **Size:** 501-1000 employees (per postings)
- **Remote Policy:** Remote-first, async-friendly. Teams distributed across the US, Canada, UK, Europe and North Macedonia

Not a recruiting agency. Slice is the direct employer.

## Quick Take
- Independent pizzeria platform: online ordering, marketing, POS, collective purchasing. Over 15,000 restaurants per postings.
- No funding round since 2021 and no layoff or acquisition news found. Founder Ilir Sela is still CEO per the latest coverage found. Last public round is 5 years old, so runway and exit pressure are unknown.
- Glassdoor is mixed (about 3.5 of 5). Recurring themes are smart colleagues and learning against shifting strategy, leadership turnover and on-call strain. Stack consolidation toward Python is in progress, which matters for a candidate without Python.

## Milestones
- 2010: Ilir Sela founds MyPizza (later Slice) out of his family's pizzerias in New York
- 2016-07: $3M Series A
- 2017-05: $15M Series B led by GGV Capital
- 2020-05: $43M Series C led by KKR
- 2021-04: $40M Series D led by Cross Creek
- 2023-02: CEO says the goal is to scale and execute like a public company (IPO aspiration)
- 2025-03: Launches "Slice Fresh", a semi-annual innovation release for independent pizzerias
- Total raised: over $100M

## Company & Product
- Founded as MyPizza Technologies, Inc. HQ in New York City.
- Sells a "reverse franchise" model: independents keep ownership and get technology, marketing, buying power and support.
- Products: online ordering (own app and restaurant sites), marketing, order management, POS, supplier purchasing, delivery logistics.
- Company-reported scale (unverified): 9,000+ pizzerias in 2,500 towns per Wikipedia, 15,000+ restaurants per current postings.
- Open roles seen in this repo: Engineering Manager Services, Engineering Manager DevEx, Director of Software Development DevEx.

## Engineering Culture
- No public engineering blog or open source organization found.
- Remote-first with async communication. Hiring loop per posting: 30 min intro, 60 min with an Engineering Director, two leadership and technical interviews, conversation with the Head of RnD, offer.
- Managers are expected to stay close to the technology and own production accountability together with Product, Design, Data and Operations.
- Platform investment is visible: a DevEx team building an internal developer platform with GitOps and LLM-based agentic tooling.

## Tech Stack
- From an engineer interview (date unknown, likely older): Ruby on Rails, React, Python, Node.js, Go, Kotlin, Swift.
- From current postings: services converging on Python, with parts in Go and Ruby.
- Infrastructure per DevEx posting: AWS, Kubernetes, Docker, ArgoCD, GitHub Actions, Terraform, Cloudflare, Datadog.
- Third-party aggregator claims (Hadoop, SES) are unreliable and not used here.

## Team Health
- Glassdoor shows about 3.5-3.6 of 5 across roughly 520 reviews. Two Glassdoor employer IDs exist for Slice and I could not verify which one is the pizzeria company in every case, so treat individual reviews with caution. Glassdoor itself returned 403, so only search snippets were available.
- Positives: smart colleagues, learning opportunities, ownership, good senior engineering pay (one reviewer).
- Negatives: shifting product strategy, frequent leadership turnover, micromanagement, on-call strain and poor work-life balance during security incidents, stack described as lagging competitors, POS connectivity problems.
- Reviews from 2021-2023 are harsher. Some 2025-2026 reviews are more positive about learning and culture, and one says the company changed substantially since 2022.

## Business Stability
- Over $100M raised, last round 2021. Investors include KKR, Cross Creek, GGV.
- No 2026 funding, layoff or acquisition news surfaced, but search coverage of recent months was thin.
- Resilient niche (independent pizzerias), but exposed to restaurant margins and competition from delivery marketplaces and POS vendors.
- Repeated reopening of manager and director roles in DevEx and Services (this repo's records show a rejection followed by a re-listing) may indicate backfills or growth. Worth asking.

## Red Flags
- Leadership turnover and shifting strategy reported on Glassdoor.
- On-call and incident strain reported.
- Posted compensation for Engineering Manager Services is $150-175k CAD base, below the target floor, with no bonus or equity stated.
- Five years since last funding round with no visible exit.
