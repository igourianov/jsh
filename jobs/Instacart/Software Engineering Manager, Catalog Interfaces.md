## Summary

Lead the Catalog Interfaces engineering team at Instacart, responsible for designing data models and APIs that power product information across the platform. The role involves managing 6+ software engineers while building systems that process approximately 8 billion updates daily. Key responsibilities include developing technical strategy for unified APIs serving dozens of internal teams, delivering reliable low-latency services with defined SLOs and mature on-call operations, and partnering cross-functionally with Product, Data, Search/Ads, and Retailer Platform teams. The manager will own Snowflake tables and implement data governance controls, design interfaces for AI/ML system integration, and establish engineering best practices in service-oriented architecture, schema management, and observability. This is a hands-on leadership role focused on team building, technical strategy, and operational excellence in a high-scale e-commerce environment.
