## Summary

Lead Instacart's Catalog Enrichment team, which builds the AI-native platform and pipelines that create, enrich and maintain product attributes across tens of millions of products from 100,000+ retailer locations. The platform blends LLMs, classical ML, deterministic rules, workflow orchestration and human review into configurable pipelines that non-engineers can run, with a roadmap toward autonomous pipeline construction where users express goals in plain language. The manager also owns the ML-facing catalog data layer, including canonical product metadata and policy controls that separate internal model inputs from customer-facing use cases. Heavily cross-functional, partnering with ML, product, search, ads, commerce, operations and retailer teams.

- **Coding:** 10% - hands-on contribution expected in architecture, design reviews and code reviews, especially in new or exploratory areas; no production programming language specified.
- Lead, mentor and grow a team of engineers building the enrichment platform, pipelines and ML-facing interfaces
- Define and execute the technical roadmap, balancing new platform investments against reliability, observability and developer experience
- Build and operate the AI-native enrichment platform and drive evolution toward autonomous pipeline construction
- Own the ML-facing catalog data layer and the policy controls governing how attributes are sourced, shared and licensed
- Advocate for human-readable, auditable governance controls for source prioritization, rights management and licensing
- Boost developer and operator productivity via AI-assisted integration testing, investigation tooling and on-call automation; set and meet SLAs on core attributes

## Company

Instacart is the North American leader in online grocery, partnering with more than 2,200 retail banners representing nearly 100,000 stores to power grocery e-commerce, fulfillment, in-store technology and advertising. The company operates through the Instacart Marketplace, the Instacart Enterprise platform and the Instacart Ads ecosystem, and runs Instacart Health to support nutrition security. Instacart positions itself as building "the operating system for the grocery industry." It is a "Flex First" employer, letting employees choose where they work while staying connected through regular in-person events. A large public company (1,001-5,000 employees on LinkedIn), it offers market-competitive compensation plus new hire and annual refresh equity grants.
