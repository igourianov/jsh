# Robots & Pencils

- **Company Type:** IT Services (digital product and AI consulting, client services)
- **Stage:** Private, PE-backed (Next Sparc)
- **Size:** ~80-200 (sources conflict: 78, 101-200, 149)
- **Remote Policy:** Not stated on company site. Posting is remote (Calgary, AB listed). Delivery centers in the US, Canada, Eastern Europe and Latin America.

## Quick Take
- Not a recruiting agency. A consultancy that bills clients for engineering, design and strategy work, now repositioned as "AI-first".
- Leadership was largely replaced in 2025 (new CEO, COO, CFO, CRO), so the AI pivot is recent and the org is still being reshaped.
- Mid-size PE-owned consultancy: delivery quality depends on client mix and utilization pressure. Glassdoor is mediocre (3.4, 57% recommend).

## Milestones
- 2009: Founded in Calgary, mobile-first app studio.
- 2017-01: Majority buyout and recapitalization by Next Sparc, merged with its portfolio company KINETiQ DIGITAL.
- 2022: One investment recorded by CB Insights, unattributed.
- 2025: Len Pagon Jr. (founder of Brulant, chairman of Next Sparc) became CEO. Followed by new CFO (Tony Antonelli), COO (Nathan Carmon, 2025-08-07), CRO (Patrick Higgins) and EVP Growth (Scott Young).
- 2026-01-08: Opened a Bellevue (Seattle area) studio for generative and agentic AI, hiring.
- 2026-04-23: Hired an industry veteran to lead a retail and consumer goods vertical.

## Company & Product
Digital products and technology consultancy. Modernizes aging platforms, builds cloud-native apps and applies AI. Offerings include Sharpening Sprint, cloud modernization and migration, cloud and AI app development, and agentic AI with AWS (AWS partner, with a VP of AWS Partnership). Industries: financial services, health tech, education, consumer, energy, tech. Claims 300+ apps launched (5 reached #1 in the App Store), 25 patents and published works, 77M+ users reached. Offices in Cleveland, Bellevue, Bogotá, Calgary, Lviv and Toronto.

## Engineering Culture
- Heritage is mobile and design-led engineering. Messaging stresses "deep technical excellence and human insight", mentorship and people-first leadership. This is marketing copy, not verified.
- No public engineering blog or GitHub organization identified in this research.
- Role in question is delivery management, so the relevant culture is client-services delivery under an AI-first mandate.

## Tech Stack
Client-driven. Publicly: AWS (cloud-native, migration, agentic AI), mobile (iOS and Android), web, generative and agentic AI. No single product stack.

## Team Health
- Glassdoor: ~3.4/5 from 89 reviews, 57% would recommend. Interview experience positive for only 34%, difficulty 2.8/5.
- Indeed: 3.3/5 from 6 reviews.
- Review text was not retrievable (Glassdoor returned 403). Themes unverified. Read the reviews directly before relying on this.

## Business Stability
- PE-owned since 2017 (Next Sparc), so a sale or further recapitalization is plausible over time.
- No layoffs or funding news found for 2025-2026. Hiring a new studio and vertical lead suggests growth investment.
- Consultancy revenue is exposed to client budget cycles and AI-driven pricing pressure on services.

## Red Flags
- Full executive team turnover in 2025: strategy and expectations may still be shifting.
- Headcount and HQ data inconsistent across sources.
- Mediocre Glassdoor recommend rate.
- Posting is delivery/project management, not engineering management, with a heavy applied-AI delivery requirement.
