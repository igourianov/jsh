## Company & Product

NextDay AI, founded in 2023 and based in Montreal, specializes in AI-powered entertainment products focused on NSFW conversational AI and character roleplay. Their primary products are Spicychat AI and PixelChat, platforms where users engage with AI characters for roleplay, companionship, and adult content conversations.

The company has achieved significant scale with over 2 million registered users, 52 million monthly site visits, and 35+ million messages per day. This places them in the rapidly growing AI companion/entertainment space competing with platforms like Character.AI and Replika. Their business model includes freemium access with premium subscriptions starting at $4.95/month, leveraging 300,000+ AI characters.

With operations in Montreal, US, Europe, and Asia, the company appears to be scaling globally despite its small size (22 employees).
