## Summary

Senior Engineering Manager owning execution and delivery across multiple engineering teams building AI-powered data connectivity software. The role pairs conventional people leadership with a heavy operating-practices mandate: establish the rhythms, metrics and organizational clarity that make delivery predictable, then develop leads inside the teams who can run day-to-day execution themselves.

Two themes are unusual for the grade. First, a shift-left engineering model where engineers join discovery and solution definition rather than receiving finished requirements, which puts the EM on the hook for Product/Engineering working practices and ownership boundaries. Second, AI runs through both the shipped product and the development process, and the EM is expected to champion AI-assisted development while holding the quality bar.

Operational ownership is stated at discipline level rather than as ordinary on-call: SLOs, error budgets, incident command and postmortem action tracking, plus finding recurring operational patterns and fixing root causes across engineering. Technical stack is Java/Kotlin backend on Azure, with Kubernetes, microservices and React named as pluses.
