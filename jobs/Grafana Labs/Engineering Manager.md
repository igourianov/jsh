## Summary

Grafana Labs is seeking an Engineering Manager to lead a product-focused engineering squad building Observability Solutions on Grafana Cloud. This fully remote role involves conducting regular 1:1s and career development sessions, fostering an inclusive high-performing team culture built on trust, and partnering with Product and Design teams on roadmaps and technical priorities. The manager will translate product goals into actionable engineering plans, improve team collaboration and execution processes, and support hiring and capacity planning efforts. The position emphasizes balancing technical contribution with strategic thinking, collaborating across distributed autonomous teams, and clear communication in multicultural remote environments.
