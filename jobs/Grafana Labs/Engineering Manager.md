## Summary

The Engineering Manager role at Grafana Labs leads a product-focused squad building Observability Solutions on top of Grafana Cloud. The position sits at the intersection of people, technology, and product direction, helping define how teams deliver reliable, high-quality experiences that empower engineers worldwide to test and improve system performance. Responsibilities include managing and developing a distributed engineering team delivering reliable high-quality software, conducting regular 1:1s and career development sessions to guide engineers' professional growth, fostering an inclusive high-performing team culture, partnering with Product and Design to refine roadmaps and align technical priorities, translating product goals into actionable engineering plans and iterative deliverables, continuously improving cross-disciplinary collaboration and execution, and supporting hiring efforts and team capacity planning.

## Company

Grafana Labs is a remote-first, open-source company with over 20 million users of Grafana, the open source visualization tool used globally for monitoring everything from beehives to climate change. The company's dashboards have been spotted at NASA launches, Minecraft HQ, Wimbledon, and the Tour de France. Grafana Labs serves more than 3,000 companies including Bloomberg, JPMorgan Chase, and eBay, helping them manage observability strategies with the Grafana LGTM Stack (metrics via Grafana Mimir, logs via Grafana Loki, traces via Grafana Tempo), available as fully managed Grafana Cloud or self-managed Grafana Enterprise Stack. The company operates as a scaleup with 1,001-5,000 employees, maintaining an innovation-driven environment emphasizing transparency, autonomy, trust, open-source legacy, and global collaborative culture. Benefits include 100% remote work, 30 days annual leave, Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) for all employees, in-person onboarding, and comprehensive compensation packages.
