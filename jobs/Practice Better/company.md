# Practice Better

**Company Type:** SaaS (Practice Management for Health & Wellness)
**Stage:** Scale-up (Growth stage, venture-backed)
**Size:** 11-50 employees
**Remote Policy:** Remote-first, headquartered in Toronto

## Quick Take
- Founded 2014, serves 15,000+ customers in 70+ countries
- Recent growth activity: $27M funding + That Clean Life acquisition ($10M+) in 2023

- Engineering Manager role likely created to address chaos described in reviews
- High risk: joining to fix broken culture vs joining already healthy team

## Company & Product

Practice Better is an all-in-one practice management platform for health and wellness practitioners (nutritionists, dietitians, health coaches). Founded in 2014 and based in Toronto, the company serves tens of thousands of practitioners globally.

In 2023, Practice Better closed a $27M funding round led by Five Elms Capital to support next phase of growth. In July 2023, acquired That Clean Life (nutrition planning platform) in an eight-figure deal, expanding customer base to 15,000+ practitioners across 70+ countries. That Clean Life continues as standalone product while integrating with Practice Better's platform.

Platform enables practitioners to manage clients, scheduling, billing, meal planning, and communications. Core integrations include Stripe (payments), Zoom (telehealth), and Fullscript (supplements), plus 20+ other third-party services.
