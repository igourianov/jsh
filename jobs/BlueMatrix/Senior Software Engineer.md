## Company

BlueMatrix builds infrastructure for the investment research lifecycle: authoring, compliant distribution and downstream consumption. Sell-side firms use it to produce and govern research and to track where it lands; buy-side firms use it as a single entitlement-enforced connection into a machine-readable universe of that research. The company positions itself deliberately below the AI interface layer, as the platform that determines what research is accessible, how it is structured and governed, and how its use is traced.

Founded in 1999, so a long-standing business rather than a startup, despite the SaaS-startup line in the preferred skills. Serves over 1,000 institutional financial firms in more than 50 countries. Offices in Toronto, New York City, Durham, London, Edinburgh and Timisoara, Romania. 51-200 employees on LinkedIn.

The company discloses that AI tools assist in reviewing applications and resumes and in flagging inconsistencies, with humans making final decisions.
