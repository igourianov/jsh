## Summary

Engineering Manager leading one Product Engineering squad (8-15 engineers) at Replicant, an autonomous contact-center AI company. Reports directly to the SVP of Engineering with full budget and headcount authority. The role is explicitly people-first and servant-leadership oriented: hire, grow and manage out engineers, shape team process and norms, and let senior ICs own the code. The manager also sits on a tight peer group of EMs that jointly owns hiring bar, career ladder, roadmap prioritization, testing standards, on-call culture and capacity planning across the whole engineering org. Heavy emphasis on personal, demonstrable AI fluency and on turning individual AI experimentation into team-level throughput and quality gains.

- **Coding:** 0% - explicitly trusts senior ICs to own the code; manager needs enough technical depth for credibility, not hands-on output.
- Hire, grow and performance-manage a squad of 8-15 engineers
- Own budget and headcount planning for the team
- Drive predictable, high-quality delivery tied to customer outcomes
- Champion and model AI-native development practices across the team
- Participate as a peer in a shared engineering-management group setting hiring bar, career ladder, roadmap process, testing standards and on-call culture org-wide
- Travel roughly every six weeks for in-person meetings

## Company

Replicant builds an autonomous contact center: AI agents that answer calls, decide whether AI or a human should handle a given interaction, and manage conversations end to end, running in production for enterprises since 2020. The platform spans telephony and real-time voice infrastructure, speech recognition and voice models, LLM orchestration and failover across providers, a conversation reasoning runtime, a bot-building UI and domain language for conversation design, ML infrastructure/evaluation, and production analytics on bot performance. Roughly 50 people in engineering, ~150-200 employees overall, fully remote, positioning itself against a large, mostly unautomated customer-service market.
