## Company

Counterpart Health is a subsidiary of Clover Health (Nasdaq: CLOV), a physician enablement company focused on Medicare Advantage. Counterpart Health develops Counterpart Assistant, an AI-enabled SaaS tool integrated into primary care workflows that surfaces personalized patient recommendations at point of care to support chronic condition management, earlier diagnosis and value-based care outcomes. Thousands of practitioners use the platform. The company operates with a remote-first distributed team. Clover Health manages Medicare Advantage members across Alabama, Arizona, Georgia, Mississippi, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, South Carolina, Tennessee and Texas.
