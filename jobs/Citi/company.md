# Citi

- **Company Type:** Enterprise (global bank, Citigroup Inc.)
- **Stage:** Public
- **Size:** about 226,000 employees globally (down from 240,000 in 2022). Mississauga is a global operations and technology hub.
- **Remote Policy:** Hybrid, 3 days in office, 2 remote. The LinkedIn "Remote" tag on the posting does not match this.

## Quick Take
- Restructuring-heavy environment: 20,000 global cuts targeted by end-2026, with more severance budgeted this year. Pressure is stated to be on legacy technology and support, transformation and remediation work, and coordination-only management roles.
- Mississauga is a real investment site (Citi bought the building and adjacent land), but Glassdoor reports slow bureaucracy, legacy code and an overcrowded office.
- Regulatory remediation (2020 consent orders, data quality and risk controls) still drives much of the technology agenda. Work is control and compliance heavy.

## Milestones
- 1812: City Bank of New York founded. Citigroup formed in 1998 by the Citicorp and Travelers merger.
- 2008-2009: Financial crisis bailout.
- 2020-10: OCC and Federal Reserve consent orders over risk management, data governance and internal controls. OCC fine of $400M.
- 2021-03: Jane Fraser becomes CEO, first woman to lead a major US bank.
- 2023-09: Fraser announces the largest reorganization in decades, flattening management layers and simplifying into five businesses.
- 2024: Additional $135.6M in penalties from OCC and Fed for slow remediation. The 2024 OCC consent order amendment was later withdrawn, the original 2020 orders remain.
- 2024-2026: Target of 20,000 job cuts by end-2026 (about $2.5B savings). About 1,000 cuts announced 2026-01. Mandatory AI training rolled out to about 175,000 staff. Citi says it is mapping 100+ processes for automation.
- 2026: Citi flagged extra severance spend (about $500M package pulled forward through year end).

## Company & Product
Global bank with institutional (Services, Markets, Banking), US personal banking and wealth segments. Citi Canada runs a large operations and technology hub in Mississauga, and the bank has said it plans to hire hundreds more there. The posting is an Apps Development Tech Lead Analyst (internal grade C13), which sits inside the large internal technology organization supporting these businesses.

## Engineering Culture
- No public engineering blog or notable open source presence found. Engineering culture is that of a large bank IT organization: layered governance, architecture blueprints, formal change control.
- Glassdoor reviewers describe collaborative teams, ownership of complex enterprise banking applications and a focus on modern Java. Complaints: slow bureaucracy, poor communication across management and teams, some hectic periods with long hours.
- Ongoing AI push (mandatory training, process automation) is reshaping how work is scoped.

## Tech Stack
- Reviews point to Java as the core language, with enterprise banking applications and a mix of modern and legacy stacks. Legacy code is a repeated complaint.
- No detailed public stack breakdown found. Ask the team directly.

## Team Health
- Glassdoor Mississauga: about 3.5-3.6 out of 5 from roughly 330 reviews. 62% would recommend to a friend.
- Positives: work-life balance described as good and low stress relative to peers, stability, good compensation and facilities.
- Negatives: bureaucracy, communication gaps, cramped office where finding a workstation is a morning hassle.
- Restructuring has caused senior exits, particularly in technology and wealth.

## Business Stability
- Financially large and public. Not a solvency concern.
- Headcount reduction is the real stability risk for an individual role: cuts are ongoing and explicitly aimed at legacy tech, support and transformation roles.
- Consent orders still partly in effect. Remediation spending is the reason many technology roles exist, and also the type of work described as temporary.

## Red Flags
- Continuing layoffs through year end, with legacy technology and transformation work named as most exposed.
- Posting pay range tops out at $170,800, below the target base.
- Bureaucracy and legacy code repeatedly cited in reviews.
- Regulatory consent orders still open.
