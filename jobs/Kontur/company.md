# Kontur (СКБ Контур)

- **Company Type:** SaaS/Product (B2B ecosystem of 70+ web services)
- **Stage:** Enterprise (private, valued at ~$1.8B in 2023)
- **Size:** ~13,000 employees (June 2025)
- **Remote Policy:** Office, remote or hybrid. 40+ office locations across Russia. Remote options available for many roles.

## Quick Take
- One of the largest and most established Russian IT product companies, founded in 1988. Revenue 48.3B RUB in 2025 (+19.6% YoY), serving 3.1M businesses (35% of all Russian companies).
- Strong engineering reputation, especially in Yekaterinburg. 4.5/5 Glassdoor rating, 95% recommendation rate. Solid .NET/React stack with active open source contributions (152 GitHub repos).
- Compensation consistently flagged as below market (Glassdoor 3.9/5 for comp). Growth may dilute engineering culture as headcount scales rapidly.

## Milestones
- **1988** - Founded in Yekaterinburg as a specialized design bureau
- **1990s** - Pivoted from CAD systems to enterprise accounting software
- **2000s** - Shifted to electronic reporting; built SaaS platform
- **2011-2020** - #1 SaaS supplier in Russia by revenue
- **2021** - Acquired 80% of Staffcop (employee monitoring/DLP software)
- **2022** - Leadership change: Mikhail Srodnih replaced Evgeny Filatov as CEO. Launched import-substitution products (Kontur.Talk, Kontur.Access, Kontur.ID, Kontur.Travel)
- **2023** - Valued at $1.8B by Forbes
- **2024** - Revenue 40.4B RUB. Increased Staffcop stake to 95%. Ranked 15th largest Russian IT company.
- **2025** - Revenue 48.3B RUB. 3.1M clients. Launched Kontur.Egida cybersecurity brand. Became first non-state GosEDO operator.

## Company & Product

Kontur builds a B2B ecosystem of 70+ web services covering electronic document management (Diadoc), accounting, digital signatures, counterparty verification (Focus), product marking, online cash registers, HR management (Gosoblako), procurement, and hospitality (Hotel).

Key product Diadoc processed 1.5B documents in 2025 (+24.5% YoY) and pioneered cross-border EDI with Belarus and Kazakhstan. The company expanded into cybersecurity (Staffcop acquisition, Kontur.Egida brand) and import-substitution tools (video conferencing, remote access, digital ID) after 2022.

Revenue has grown consistently: from 1.3B RUB (2008) to 48.3B RUB (2025), a 21% CAGR over 5 years. The company sponsors the TV show "Svoya Igra" since 2024.

## Engineering Culture

Kontur has a reputation as one of the strongest engineering employers in the Urals region. The culture emphasizes "thinking, developing specialists" over perfection. Key traits:

- **Open source presence:** 152 repositories on GitHub (github.com/skbkontur). Notable projects include retail-ui (React component library, 265 stars), ZstdNet (.NET Zstd wrapper, 323 stars), GroBuf (binary serializer, 67 stars), gremit (Reflection.Emit extensions, 170 stars).
- **Internal education:** Trained ~85,000 people through educational programs (2021). Mentorship system for new hires.
- **Engineering conferences:** Supports professional conferences and has internal tech talks.
- **Career paths:** Both vertical (junior to lead) and horizontal (cross-project) advancement.
- **Interview process:** 6 stages over 2-4 weeks: resume screen, recruiter call, technical assignment (3-7 days), technical interview (1-2 hrs), team/manager conversation, offer.

## Tech Stack

**Backend:** C# / .NET (primary), Python, Java, Go, Node.js
**Frontend:** React, TypeScript, JavaScript. Some teams use Angular and Vue.
**Tools:** Atlassian (Confluence, JIRA), Microsoft Power BI, Kaspersky
**Open source focus:** .NET libraries, TypeScript contract generators, React UI components
**Infrastructure:** Cloudflare CDN

The core stack is .NET + React/TypeScript. C# is the dominant backend language across most products.

## Business Stability

Strong fundamentals:
- Consistent revenue growth (~20% YoY) for over a decade
- 3.1M clients representing 35% of Russian businesses
- Diversified product portfolio (70+ services)
- Self-funded, no external VC dependency
- Expanding into cybersecurity and import-substitution markets

Risks:
- Fully dependent on Russian market. Geopolitical isolation limits international growth.
- Import substitution creates opportunity but also means competing with state-backed alternatives.
- Rapid headcount growth (from ~10K to 13K) could strain culture.

## Red Flags
- **Below-market compensation** is the most consistent complaint across all review platforms. Glassdoor comp rating 3.9/5 is notably lower than other metrics.
- **Quality dilution at scale** - multiple reviewers note that as headcount grows, the average quality of engineers drops.
- **Staffcop acquisition** (employee monitoring software) raises questions about internal surveillance culture, though it appears to be a product line rather than internal tool.
- **No significant international presence** - career growth is bounded by the Russian market.
