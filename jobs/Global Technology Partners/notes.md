# Global Technology Partners

## Research

- IT services and consulting firm, 51-200 employees on LinkedIn. Not a product company. HQ is Marlton, New Jersey, not Toronto: their own contact page lists that as the only office and no Canadian presence is published anywhere. The posting's "1-2 visits/month to the Toronto office" is therefore almost certainly the client's office. See `company.md`.
- Positions itself as an "AI Enabler" with an incubator arm called GTP AI Labs. Heavy marketing language: "agentic autonomy", "data liquidity", "multi-cloud AI optimization".
- Postings are placements into client engagements, not roles on GTP's own product. The AI Architect posting is for a client's legacy benefits administration platform, client unnamed.
- No Glassdoor or comp data gathered yet.

## Impressions

- **Engagements here are contract, not salaried.** The AI Architect posting carries LinkedIn's `Contract` tag and says nothing about duration, rate, benefits or conversion to FTE. Assume day or hourly rate, no benefits, no RRSP match, no equity, no severance until proven otherwise.
- This cuts against the standing objective of a remote EM role at a B2B SaaS or big tech company at 180-200k CAD salaried. A consulting placement is a different kind of job wearing a similar title.
- Titles here may not carry real management scope. The AI Architect posting says "guide a high-performing engineering team" but states no team size and no reporting structure. A contract architect placement often has no direct reports, which would make the "Engineering Manager" half of the title decorative.
- Company description is marketing-dense and light on specifics. Every posting so far describes the client's problem rather than GTP's own engineering.

## Questions

Settle these first on any call with GTP. Everything else depends on the answers:

- **Is this a role with GTP, or a placement?** Direct hire onto a GTP team, or staff augmentation into a client engagement? If a placement: who is the client, and is it a delivery-owned team or bodies on their org chart?
- **Is this enablement or product?** Consulting work on a client's system, or product engineering on something GTP owns? Their AI Labs products are marketed heavily but have no external footprint, so "product" may not be a real option.
- Contract length, rate and whether there is a conversion to FTE
- **How would I be engaged?** Three possibilities and they are not equivalent:
  - **T4 on the agency's payroll.** Temporary employee, taxes withheld, no company or invoicing needed. Simplest.
  - **Sole proprietor.** Invoice under own legal name. No Ontario registration required when using own name exactly.
  - **Corp-to-corp.** Requires incorporation, a corporate return and real accounting. Carries CRA Personal Services Business exposure on a single-client full-time engagement, which is punitive: small business deduction and most expense deductions denied.
  - Whichever it is, it changes the rate needed. Also ask which entity pays, the New Jersey one or a Canadian one, since a US payer adds W-8BEN-E and withholding.
- Whether the role has actual direct reports, and how many
- Who performance and career conversations run through, GTP or the client
- Where the team actually sits, given HQ is New Jersey and the office visits are Toronto
