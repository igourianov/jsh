# Owner.com

**Company Type:** SaaS/Product (Restaurant Technology Platform)
**Stage:** Scale-up (Unicorn - $1B valuation)
**Size:** ~275 employees (grew from ~100 to ~200 in 2024)
**Remote Policy:** Remote-first, work from anywhere

## Quick Take
- Strong product-market fit with 10,000+ restaurants, real measurable impact ($1B+ sales driven, $250M+ fees saved)
- Exceptional Glassdoor rating (4.9/5) with 98% recommend rate - rare for a high-growth startup
- Fast-paced, high-intensity culture - explicitly not a 9-5 environment; expect startup hours
- Engineering Manager role available at $220K-$250K with pre-IPO equity

## Milestones
- **2018**: Founded as ProfitBoss by Adam Guild (high school dropout, Thiel Fellow) and Dean Bloembergen (Forbes 30 Under 30)
- **2021**: Rebranded to Owner.com after major post-COVID pivot from in-store traffic to online ordering
- **March 2022**: Series A - $15M led by Altman Capital
- **January 2024**: Series B - $33M from Redpoint Ventures and Altman Capital
- **May 2025**: Series C - $120M from Meritech and Headline, reaching $1B unicorn valuation
- **Total funding**: $178.7M raised to date

## Company & Product
Owner.com provides an all-in-one platform for independent restaurants to compete with large chains and third-party delivery apps. Often described as "Shopify + HubSpot for restaurants."

**Core Platform:**
- AI-powered website builder with conversion optimization
- Commission-free online ordering system
- Custom mobile apps for restaurants
- CRM and email/text marketing automation
- Loyalty programs
- Zero-commission delivery coordination

**Key Differentiators:**
- Opinionated platform (not customizable) - runs thousands of A/B tests weekly across all sites
- Month-to-month billing only - no long-term contracts
- AI Executives (2025 roadmap): AI-powered CMO, CFO, CTO assistants for restaurants
- Integrates with Toast, Square, Clover POS systems

**Scale:**
- 10,000+ active restaurants
- 60M+ restaurant guests served
- Restaurants see 30%+ growth in online orders in first year
- 2x higher conversion rate vs. industry average

## Red Flags
- **Work-life balance concerns**: Multiple reviews explicitly state this is not a 9-5 job. High-intensity, high-expectation environment. Need to be honest about whether career growth/startup upside outweighs traditional work-life balance.
- **SMB customer base**: Independent restaurants have higher churn rates than enterprise customers. Business model depends on expansion revenue and continuous new customer acquisition.
- **Early-stage processes**: Some systems and processes still being built. May encounter ambiguity and need to build playbooks from scratch.
- **No significant layoffs or negative news identified.**
