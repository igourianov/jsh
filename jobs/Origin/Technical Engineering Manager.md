## Company

Origin is building an endpoint AI observability platform for organizations adopting AI agents at scale. The platform inventories, observes and measures AI agent usage on endpoints, giving organizations visibility into what agents exist, how they're used and what actions they take, aiming to maximize safe, effective AI adoption. Backed by Sequoia Capital, Brightmind Ventures, IA Ventures and other top VC firms. Fully remote team across the US and Canada, ~11-50 employees, security/observability space.
