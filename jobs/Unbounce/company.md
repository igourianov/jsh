# Unbounce - Company Research

## Overview
- Vancouver-based landing page builder and conversion rate optimization (CRO) SaaS, founded 2009.
- Positions itself as "#1 landing page builder and CRO platform," ~15,000 marketers/agencies as customers.
- Owned by Crest Rock Partners (Denver-based PE firm), majority stake.
- Merged with/acquired Insightly (CRM, San Francisco) on July 10, 2024. Insightly now operates as "an Unbounce Company." Combined headcount 200+ across North America and Europe.
- March 2026: launched Generative UI - AI landing page generation from text prompts. Matches the "AI-native product" framing in the JD (this is the product the Landing Page Builder group is building toward).

## Culture / Glassdoor signals
- Overall rating 3.0/5 on Glassdoor, 22% below average for IT industry employers.
- Reviews describe a sharp cultural decline: "used to offer a great workplace culture," 2022 layoffs, morale "at an all-time low" after long-tenured employees let go.
- Recurring themes: gaslighting by new leadership around the Insightly merger, founder departures, multiple rounds of layoffs, continuously changing leadership/strategy, reduced diversity in leadership, cost-cutting expected to continue into 2025-2026.
- This independently corroborates the JD's own framing (recent restructuring, two director departures in June 2026, fractional CTO incoming) - the instability described in the posting is not new, it's a continuation of a multi-year pattern.
