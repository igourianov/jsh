## Summary

Engineering Manager for the Behavioral team at Movable Ink, responsible for building the behavioral intelligence layer of the platform. The team builds systems that detect patterns in customer activity and inactivity (cart abandonment, price drops, loyalty milestones) and translate them into automated messaging triggers. This is a player-coach role blending technical and people leadership. Reports to the Senior Director of Engineering.

- **Coding:** 0% The posting emphasizes architecture, system design and technical leadership. Technologies listed are broad system-level concerns (event-driven, distributed systems, pub/sub) rather than specific programming languages.
- Recruit, mentor and grow a team of engineers building behavioral decisioning capabilities
- Partner with Product to define the roadmap for behavioral triggers
- Drive initiatives from idea to delivery, translating product goals into technical plans
- Lead development of services that transform activity signals into trigger events (cart abandonment, browse abandonment, price drops, back-in-stock)
- Provide hands-on technical leadership for behavioral pattern detection across events and time windows
- Architect systems for short-lived behavioral state and lookback windows
- Collaborate with Data Platform, Messaging and Activation teams
- Ensure behavioral services operate reliably at scale with observability and postmortems
