Engineering Manager role at a Series B EdTech company building software for early childhood educators. The manager leads a full-stack engineering team (Ruby on Rails, React, GraphQL, AWS), drives engineering practices and culture, and partners closely with Product and Design. The role blends people management (1:1s, growth, hiring) with hands-on technical engagement and cross-team collaboration.

- Lead and grow a team of full-stack engineers through coaching, 1:1s, career development and feedback
- Drive continuous improvement of engineering practices, processes and quality

- Collaborate with Product and Design to define, build and ship product experiences
- Stay current with industry trends and champion innovation within the team
- Contribute to engineering and company culture as the organization scales
