## Summary

Monks seeks an Engineering Manager to drive technical execution and quality standards for successful, on-time delivery while partnering closely with Product Management to align technical roadmap with business priorities. This dual-mandate role covers both Technical Delivery and Team Leadership. Responsibilities include driving technical execution of roadmap ensuring on-time high-quality project completion, collaborating with Product Management to manage technical roadmap balancing feature development, technical debt and operational stability, acting as primary technical point of contact for client and internal stakeholders, being accountable for team deliverables quality and defining engineering standards, providing technical guidance and architectural context, attracting and retaining top engineering talent, developing career progression plans with regular feedback, ensuring continuous learning through training opportunities, and helping shape engineering organization vision. The role requires experience with Web and Native (iOS) applications in a client-facing environment.
