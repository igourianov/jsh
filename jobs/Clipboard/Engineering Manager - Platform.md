## Summary

Hands-on Engineering Manager for Clipboard's Platform team, which builds the self-serve developer platform (infrastructure, tooling, processes, education) that lets every engineer design, build, test, deploy and secure software fast, increasingly for AI-augmented and AI-autonomous development. The EM owns cross-cutting problems once for the whole org: paved-path tooling, shared systems (auth, notifications, chat), the hard non-functional tech and production guardrails (with Security). Delivery teams own their areas end-to-end with no QA or DevOps layer, so the platform reduces their cognitive load. The EM balances technical direction, execution and people development, stays close enough to the work to back strong technical calls, and prioritizes where centralizing friction is worth it versus letting teams solve their own problems, including AI-specific risks (agent blast radius, credential exposure, shared-environment contention).
