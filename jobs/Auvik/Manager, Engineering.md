## Company

Auvik builds cloud-based network management software for IT teams and MSPs. The platform automates network discovery, mapping, monitoring, configuration backups, traffic analysis and alerting, giving visibility and control over on-site, remote and multi-site infrastructure. It also extends to endpoints, security devices and SaaS application management, with contextual AI layered on top of real-time infrastructure data to speed up troubleshooting and surface proactive fixes.

Scale claimed: one million network devices and three million SaaS applications across 100,000+ networks, with monitoring for over 10 million devices. 201-500 employees, fully distributed team, headquartered in Waterloo region.
