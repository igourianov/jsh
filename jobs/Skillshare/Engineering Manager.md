## Summary

The role leads a core product engineering team at Skillshare, guiding the company's strategic transition from legacy systems to modern, scalable architecture. Responsibilities include directly managing and mentoring engineers and tech leads, partnering with Product and Design teams on delivery execution, championing Agile practices with rigorous Jira documentation as the authoritative source of truth, maintaining application reliability through SRE principles and proactive technical debt management, and balancing speed of execution with long-term scalability. The position requires active participation in code reviews and technical discussions while owning team application health, recruitment, and contributing to the overall Engineering strategy and vision.

## Required Qualifications

- 6+ years professional experience building highly-scalable web applications
- 2+ years direct experience managing, mentoring, and developing software engineers
- Demonstrated expertise in Agile frameworks, particularly Scrum, with proven track record of driving execution and delivery predictability
- Strong technical foundation and comfort guiding teams using PHP, GraphQL, TypeScript, and React
- Proven experience managing application reliability and operational health, including strategic prioritization of technical debt and upholding SRE or DevOps principles
- Expertise in managing project workflow within tools like Jira, including defining, documenting, and prioritizing detailed user stories and conducting accurate capacity planning
- Extensive technical experience building and operating consumer-facing web applications at scale
- Proven ability to collaborate cross-functionally with Product, Design, and other business stakeholders
- Strong verbal and written communication skills, with ability to articulate technical decisions to both technical and non-technical audiences

## Optional Qualifications

## Company

Skillshare is the leading online learning community for creatives, offering over 30,000 expert-led classes in illustration, design, photography, video, marketing, and more. The platform serves hundreds of thousands of member subscribers globally through a subscription-based model, empowering anyone, anywhere to learn new skills, share expertise, and unlock their creative potential. Backed by top investors including Newspring Capital, OMERS Ventures, and Union Square Ventures, Skillshare's mission is to build the world's most thriving creative learning community. The company operates in the EdTech and B2C subscription space, focusing on enabling creatives to discover new passions, advance careers, and connect with an inspiring community of teachers and peers.
