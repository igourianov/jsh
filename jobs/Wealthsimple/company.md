# Wealthsimple

- **Company Type:** Fintech Platform (investing, trading, saving, banking, crypto)
- **Stage:** Late-stage private, pre-IPO, profitable
- **Size:** ~2,500 employees
- **Remote Policy:** Remote-first / flexible hybrid. Employees choose if/when to come into offices (Toronto HQ). "90 Days Away" program lets employees work abroad up to 90 days/year. Async collaboration norms, outcomes-over-hours.

## Quick Take
- Profitable since 2024, growing fast (AUA doubled $50B to $100B in a year, $124.8B by Q1 2026), well capitalized ($750M raised Oct 2025 at $10B valuation).
- Engineering blog is active and reasonably technical (performance, ledger systems, AI-assisted migrations), but leans toward leadership/strategy content over deep technical writing.
- Glassdoor sentiment for engineers is solidly positive (4.3/5 for SWE) but reviews flag inconsistent work-life balance across teams and internal promotion pay lagging external hires.

## Milestones
- Founded September 2014 (Toronto) by Michael Katchen, Rudy Adler and Brett Huneycutt as a robo-advisor.
- Expanded into trading, crypto, savings/chequing, credit card (waitlist launched 2025, 300k+ signups).
- 2022: Laid off 13% of staff (159 of 1,262) amid market volatility, refocused on core investing/banking/crypto and cut P2P payments, tax, merchant services.
- 2025: Raised $750M at $10B valuation (co-led by major investors including reports of US billionaire-linked investment firm buying employee stock); became first Canadian platform offering 24/5 US stock trading.
- 2025: Profitable full year, 650k+ new clients added, now 3.4M+ clients; roughly 1 in 5 Canadians aged 18-40 use a Wealthsimple product.
- Named 2026 CIX Innovator of the Year.
- Still private, no confirmed IPO date despite years of speculation.

## Company & Product
Wealthsimple is a Canadian financial services platform covering self-directed investing (stocks, ETFs, options, margin, gold, crypto), managed portfolios/direct indexing, chequing and savings accounts, and (upcoming) a credit card. It positions itself as a modern alternative to Canada's "Big Six" banks, targeting younger, digitally-native clients. AUA reached $124.8B in Q1 2026, up 71% YoY.

## Engineering Culture
- Engineering blog (engineering.wealthsimple.com) covers performance/latency work, fintech ledger systems, AI-assisted large-scale migrations, AI-powered static analysis/security tooling, and CTO-level commentary on AI adoption strategy.
- Content mix skews toward leadership/strategic posts alongside some technical deep dives; not as consistently technical as top-tier eng blogs (e.g., Stripe, Netflix).
- References to engineer exchange programs with other fintechs, structured release cycles, and testing rituals ("Thursday-focused testing" mentioned).
- Container-based deployment (Docker/Kubernetes implied); increasing AI tooling investment in security and migration work.

## Tech Stack
- Backend: Ruby on Rails, Java, Python
- Frontend/Mobile: JavaScript/TypeScript, React, React Native (unified mobile app)
- Data: PostgreSQL, Redis, GraphQL, Amazon RDS, Amazon S3
- Infra/Cloud: AWS (EC2, Lambda, CloudFront, Route 53, Elastic Beanstalk, ELB), Docker, Ansible
- DevOps/Observability: GitHub, CircleCI, Datadog, Amazon CloudWatch, PagerDuty, Jira

## Team Health
- Glassdoor: 4.1/5 overall (450 reviews), 4.3/5 for Software Engineers specifically (61 reviews); 82% would recommend to a friend.
- Compensation rated well (4.5/5 for SWEs), but reviews note internal promotions are often lowballed relative to external senior hires ("new hires making $20-40k more than someone 1 level up who got promoted internally").
- Pros cited: collaborative atmosphere, interesting projects, inclusive culture, strong growth opportunities.
- Cons cited: inconsistent work-life balance across teams (some relaxed, some long hours/stressful), on-call burden, expectations to work outside specialized skill set, unrealistic deadlines on some teams, lots of codebases/services to ramp up on, occasional gatekeeping and mid-management friction.

## Business Stability
- Profitable in both 2024 and 2025 (rare among fintechs), with strong revenue growth from AUA doubling.
- Well capitalized: $750M raised October 2025 at $10B valuation.
- Diversifying revenue (credit card launch, 24/5 US trading, advice/spending expansion planned for 2026).
- One layoff event in 2022 (13% of workforce) tied to market volatility; no reported layoffs since, and the company has been in clear growth mode through 2025-2026.

## Red Flags
- 2022 layoffs (13%) show the company is not immune to market-driven cuts, though 2023-2026 trajectory has been growth-oriented.
- Internal promotion compensation compression relative to external hiring, per multiple Glassdoor reviews — worth probing directly on comp banding during interviews.
- Work-life balance and on-call load appear team-dependent; ask specifically about the team's on-call rotation and deadline culture.
- Engineering blog content is more strategy/leadership-flavored than deeply technical in places, which may or may not reflect actual day-to-day engineering rigor.
