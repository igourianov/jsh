# Sprout Social - Company & Product Research

**Research Date:** 2025-12-04

---

## Company Overview

Sprout Social is a global leader in social media management and analytics software, headquartered in Chicago with approximately 1,400 employees worldwide. The company serves approximately 30,000 brands and was named the #1 Best Software Product by G2's 2024 Best Software Award.

**Company Age:** 15 years (founded 2010)

---

## Product Platform

Sprout Social offers a comprehensive social media management platform that operates across all major social media networks and digital platforms.

### Core Product Features (2025)

**Publishing & Engagement:**
- All-in-one inbox with centralized communications from all channels
- Seamless transition between social media channels
- Comprehensive publishing and engagement functionality

**AI-Powered Influencer Marketing:**
- Refreshed, intuitive design with AI-driven natural language discovery
- Topic-led search matching how networks serve content
- Data analysis capabilities to identify creators quickly

**Customer Care:**
- Additional bot channels and customizable workflows
- Compliance and governance capabilities
- Holistic reporting functionality
- Proactive social care innovations

**Platform Integrations:**
- Deep Salesforce collaboration bringing social channels (Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp) directly into Salesforce
- Expansive suite of integrations launched in 2025

**Analytics & Business Intelligence:**
- AI-powered business intelligence
- Employee advocacy solutions
- Advanced analytics capabilities

**Innovation Pace:**
- Released over 200 new product capabilities in 2024 alone

## Technology Stack & Architecture

### Programming Languages & Frameworks
- **Backend:** Java, Python, PHP
- **Frontend:** React
- **Communication:** Slack

### Database Systems
- **SQL:** MySQL
- **NoSQL:** HBase
- **Search:** Elasticsearch/OpenSearch (large distributed setups)
- **Message Queue:** NSQ

### Cloud & Infrastructure
- **Cloud Provider:** AWS (primary), with GCP also in use
- **DNS:** Amazon Route 53
- **Orchestration:** Kubernetes
- **Architecture:** Distributed systems, microservices approach

### Scale Metrics
- **Requests:** Tens of billions processed daily
- **Events:** Tens of billions of asynchronous events daily
- **Social Messages:** Billions ingested every day
- **Deployments:** 1,000+ production deployments per month

### Engineering Practices
- High-availability solutions focus
- Distributed applications and queuing systems
- Enterprise-level production software systems
- Reliable service delivery at scale
