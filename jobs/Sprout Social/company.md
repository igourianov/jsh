# Sprout Social

**Company Type:** SaaS (Social Media Management Platform)
**Stage:** Public (NASDAQ: SPT since December 2019)
**Size:** ~1,300 employees (319 in Engineering, ~22% of headcount)
**Remote Policy:** Hybrid/Remote - supports remote, hybrid, and in-person work across US, with offices in Chicago, Seattle, and Dublin

## Quick Take
- Mature SaaS company with solid product-market fit (30,000+ customers, $405M revenue in 2024)
- Engineering culture emphasizes sustainable practices (canary deploys, beta rollouts, no-hero culture)
- Significant turbulence: ongoing layoffs, CEO transition (Oct 2024), stock near all-time lows (~$9 vs $34 high)
- Glassdoor rating declining (3.7 overall, 3.4 for engineers) with concerns about direction and restructuring

## Milestones
- **2010** - Founded in Chicago by Justyn Howard, Aaron Rankin, Gil Lara, and Peter Soung; $1M seed from Lightbank
- **2015** - Launched Bambu employee advocacy platform; added Spanish/Portuguese support
- **2018** - Series D ($40.5M), reaching $102M total funding
- **December 2019** - IPO on NASDAQ at ~$100M ARR
- **2023** - Acquired Tagger Media (influencer marketing)
- **October 2024** - CEO transition: Ryan Barretto (former President) became CEO; founder Justyn Howard became Executive Chair
- **2025** - Acquired NewsWhip; launched Sprout AI platform; stock declined 65% YTD

## Company & Product
Sprout Social provides a comprehensive social media management platform for businesses. The product suite includes:
- Social media publishing, scheduling, and analytics
- Social listening and monitoring
- Customer care and engagement tools
- Influencer marketing (via Tagger acquisition)
- Employee advocacy (Bambu)

The platform integrates with major social networks and enterprise tools (Salesforce, AWS). Revenue is 99% subscription-based with strong enterprise focus. The company has grown from $30M ARR in 2016 to $385M+ ARR by end of 2023, demonstrating consistent growth. Named a Leader in IDC's 2025-26 report and #1 in 40 G2 2026 Winter Reports.

## Engineering Culture
**Positives:**
- Cross-functional teams spanning mobile, front-end, back-end, QA, and SRE
- Sustainable engineering practices: canary deploys, parallel code paths, beta rollouts
- "Tech-giant scale problems with smaller, supportive teams where every engineer can make an impact"
- Engineers of all levels can lead system design
- Weekly meeting-free focus days
- Active open source presence (67 GitHub repos including java-mcp-sdk, botanist, Twitter4J)
- Suite product means diverse tech exposure without changing companies

**Concerns:**
- Reviews mention "lack of coherent direction from upper management"
- Work thrown away due to sudden priority changes
- Advancement opportunities vary significantly by team

## Tech Stack
- **Languages:** Python, React, Java, PHP
- **Cloud:** AWS (Route 53, deep AWS ecosystem integration)
- **Communication:** Slack
- **Integrations:** Salesforce, various social media APIs
- **DevOps:** Canary deployments, beta rollouts, parallel code paths
- **Open Source Tools:** NSQ (messaging), Tailscale, various internal tools

## Team Health
**Glassdoor Ratings:**
- Overall: 3.7/5 (down 5% over past 12 months)
- Software Engineers: 3.4/5 (lower than company average)
- 45% of engineers would recommend (up 127% recently, suggesting recent improvements)
- Work-life balance: 4.4/5 (highly rated)
- Diversity & inclusion: 4.3/5
- Career opportunities: 3.2/5 (below average)
- Compensation: 3.9/5

**Positive Themes:**
- Strong work-life balance and benefits
- Collaborative product teams
- Friendly, supportive culture at IC level
- Interesting technical challenges
- Remote-friendly with regular team onsites

**Negative Themes:**
- Middling compensation with no yearly raises, market adjustments, or COL increases
- Slow advancement depending on team
- Declining morale after multiple restructurings
- Management turnover and priority whiplash
- Reduced PTO for 2026, constant budget cuts

## Business Stability
**Revenue:** $405.9M in 2024 (22% YoY growth), 99% subscription revenue

**Stock Performance:** Currently trading at ~$9 (near 52-week low of $8.55, down from $34 high). 65% decline YTD in 2025. Trading at 62% below analyst fair value estimates.

**Profitability:** Not profitable; net loss of $62M in 2024 (improving from 2023). Not forecast to be profitable in next 3 years.

**Analyst Sentiment:** Average "Buy" rating with $19-26 price targets, but multiple recent downgrades (Morgan Stanley, Canaccord, Baird all lowered targets).

**Recent Moves:** Acquisitions (Tagger 2023, NewsWhip 2025) and Sprout AI launch show continued investment in product. However, ongoing cost-cutting and layoffs indicate pressure to reach profitability.

## Red Flags
1. **Ongoing layoffs** - Engineering and Success teams hit; described as "pretty constant" with "horrific treatment" of affected employees
2. **Leadership instability** - CEO transition in 2024, multiple management changes causing "chaos and friction"
3. **Priority whiplash** - Reviews cite throwing away weeks of work due to sudden direction changes
4. **Compensation concerns** - No raises, no market adjustments, reduced PTO, reduced bonuses
5. **Stock performance** - Near all-time lows, not profitable, significant uncertainty
6. **Critical personnel exits** - Through layoffs and PIPs with "zero contingency planning"
7. **Declining culture** - Reviews note company "was amazing a few years ago" but has changed significantly
