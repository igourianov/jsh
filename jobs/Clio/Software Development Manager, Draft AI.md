## Company

Clio is a Vancouver-founded legal technology company and the leading cloud-based legal practice management platform, used by hundreds of thousands of legal professionals in more than 130 countries and approved by over 100 bar associations. The Intelligent Legal Work Platform covers case management, billing, client intake, document management and payments, with an increasing AI layer on top. Draft is its AI-powered legal drafting product.

The company is late-stage private, at roughly $5B valuation after a $500M Series G in late 2025 and the ~$1B vLex acquisition, with ARR around $400M and about 1,500 employees. It runs one of the largest and longest-running Ruby on Rails monoliths in the industry and is a member of the Rails Foundation. Hubs in Burnaby, Calgary, Toronto, Dublin, London, New York and Sydney, with a weekly Anchor Day in-office expectation for local staff.
