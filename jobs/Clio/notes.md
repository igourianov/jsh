## Questions

1. The posting mentions "various teams" with new SDM roles. Which teams are hiring and what products do they own?
2. How is engineering performance measured on the team? Are there quantitative targets (e.g. PR counts)?
3. How does the team balance feature delivery vs. tech debt reduction?
4. What does the triad (Design + Product + Engineering) dynamic look like day to day? How much autonomy does the SDM have over technical decisions?
5. What's the remote work experience like for someone not near a hub? How often are non-local employees expected to travel to an office?
6. What's the team size for the role I'd be stepping into?
7. Is this a backfill or a new team? What happened to the previous manager?
8. How would you describe the pace and sustainability of work right now, given the company's growth phase?
