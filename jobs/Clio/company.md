# Clio

- **Company Type:** SaaS / Legal Practice Management Platform
- **Stage:** Late-stage private (Series G, $5B valuation)
- **Size:** ~1,500 employees (as of early 2026)
- **Remote Policy:** Flexible hybrid. Offices in Vancouver, Calgary, Toronto, Dublin, Sydney. Weekly "Anchor Day" in-office program, annual Team Days. Most roles listed as hybrid unless otherwise specified.

## Quick Take
- Dominant player in cloud-based legal tech with strong financials ($400M ARR, 60% YoY growth) and aggressive expansion
- One of the largest Ruby on Rails codebases in the world. Significant tech debt acknowledged in reviews.
- Glassdoor engineering reviews flag unrealistic deadlines, burnout culture, and questionable performance metrics (200 PR/year targets). Management quality varies by team.

## Milestones
- **2008** - Founded as Themis Solutions by Jack Newton and Rian Gauvreau in Burnaby, BC. Debuted at ABA TECHSHOW as first fully cloud-based legal practice management software.
- **2021** - Raised $110M Series E at $1.6B valuation (unicorn status)
- **2024** - Raised $900M Series F at $3B valuation. Revenue crossed $250M ARR.
- **2025 (Nov)** - $500M Series G at $5B valuation. Acquired vLex for ~$1B (largest acquisition in legaltech history). ARR reached $400M. Client base doubled to 400,000 users in 130 countries.
- **2026** - Launched Clio for Enterprise and Clio Work (unified AI workspace). Hiring aggressively across engineering, product, sales.

## Company & Product
Clio is the legal industry's leading cloud-based practice management platform, powering the full client journey from intake to invoicing. The platform includes case management, billing, client intake, document management, payments, and AI-powered features. Clio serves solo practitioners to large firms across 130 countries.

The 2025 acquisition of vLex (1B+ legal documents, AI legal research) signals a major push into AI-powered legal workflows. Clio for Enterprise targets mid-market and large law firms, expanding beyond their traditional SMB base.

The company hosts ClioCon, an annual legal tech conference, and publishes the influential Legal Trends Report analyzing data from its platform.

## Engineering Culture
Clio runs one of the largest and longest-running Ruby on Rails monoliths in the world. The company is a member of the Rails Foundation and actively sponsors the Toronto Rails community.

**Open Source:** 19 repositories on GitHub, mostly Rails-focused utilities: `job-iteration` (interruptible background jobs), `jit_preloader` (N+1 query prevention), `acts_as_tenant` (multi-tenancy), `dead_code_detector`. These are quality, focused libraries that suggest pragmatic engineering values.

**Concerns from reviews:** Engineers report that product decisions often fall onto engineering teams due to limited planning support earlier in the process. Constant context switching across parallel projects is a recurring complaint. The 200 PR/year performance target has been criticized as a blunt metric that doesn't reflect meaningful contribution.

## Tech Stack
- **Languages:** Ruby (primary backend), Python, JavaScript, Scala, C++, PHP, SQL
- **Frameworks:** Ruby on Rails (primary), Backbone.js, Bootstrap
- **Cloud:** AWS (Amazon EC2, Redshift)
- **Data/Analytics:** Apache Spark, Hadoop, Kafka, Airflow, AWS Glue, Segment, Mixpanel
- **Infrastructure:** Cloudflare, LetsEncrypt
- **Identity/Security:** Okta, OneTrust
- **Other:** Salesforce, Zendesk, Marketo, Mailgun

Cross-trained developers come from Java, Python, C++, and JavaScript backgrounds. The company helps developers learn Ruby on joining.

## Team Health
- **Glassdoor Overall:** 4.0/5 (377 reviews)
- **Software Engineer Rating:** 4.2/5 (48 reviews)
- **Work-Life Balance (Eng):** 4.0/5
- **Career Opportunities (Eng):** 4.1/5
- **Compensation (Eng):** 3.3/5 (11% below company average)
- **69%** of engineering employees would recommend to a friend

**Positive themes:** Good diversity and inclusion (4.1), career growth opportunities, strong company mission, good benefits (though wellness benefit was removed in 2024).

**Negative themes:** Compensation not competitive with US standards for experienced talent. IPO pressure driving burnout. Mandatory in-office day introduced in 2024 alongside benefit cuts. Feedback culture described as performative: honest constructive criticism reportedly penalized.

## Business Stability
Strong. $5B valuation with $400M ARR and 60% growth. No layoffs reported. Actively hiring hundreds of roles. Series G funding provides significant runway. The vLex acquisition positions Clio well for the AI-driven legal tech wave.

Risk factor: the company is clearly on an IPO track, which explains the growth-at-all-costs pressure engineers report. Post-IPO dynamics could change priorities.

## Red Flags
- **Tech debt acknowledged but not prioritized.** Reviews state "tons of tech debt with absolutely no desire to go back and fix anything."
- **Blunt performance metrics.** 200 PR/year target for developers is a crude measure that can incentivize wrong behaviors.
- **Burnout culture intensifying.** IPO pressure is driving unrealistic timelines and too many parallel projects.
- **Compensation below market.** Engineering compensation rated 3.3/5, with explicit mentions of not matching US-level pay.
- **Management inconsistency.** "Team culture depends a lot on the team lead." Behind closed doors, blame culture reported for stability issues caused by management decisions.
- **Feedback suppression.** Multiple reviews describe being punished for honest feedback. "If you want to do well at Clio you have to be drinking the Clio cool aid."
