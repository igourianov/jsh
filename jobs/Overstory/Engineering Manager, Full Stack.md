## Company

Overstory is a ~100-person remote-first company building AI-powered vegetation risk management software for electric utilities. The product uses satellite imagery and machine learning to identify and prioritize vegetation encroachment on power lines, helping utilities prevent outages and wildfire ignitions. The team spans the Americas and Europe, operates across 11 countries, and represents 15 nationalities. Customers are electric utility operators in the US and beyond. The company has a strong climate mission focus and an inclusive culture with notable gender and ethnic diversity on the leadership team.
