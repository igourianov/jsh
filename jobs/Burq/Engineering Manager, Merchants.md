## Company

Burq builds last-mile delivery infrastructure for teams needing reliability at scale without manually stitching together carriers and tools. Its AI layer, Pulse AI, runs specialized agents across the delivery lifecycle to detect risk early and protect on-time performance, cost and customer experience. Burq lets merchants quote/select delivery options, orchestrate dispatch across in-house and third-party fleets, automate routing/batching, resolve exceptions (reroutes, refunds), send customer updates and surface performance insights. Customers span enterprise and high-growth operators in grocery, floral and specialty retail. 51-200 employees.
