## Company

MEDFAR Clinical Solutions, founded in 2010 in Montreal by two aeronautical engineers, builds cloud-based Electronic Medical Record (EMR) software for clinics. It was the first company to certify a cloud-based EMR in Canada. Its two products, MYLE (for general practitioners) and PLEXIA (for specialists), give physicians a single intuitive platform to access patient files and data, reduce clerical/administrative burden and support safer, better-informed care decisions. The company is a purpose-driven, high-growth SaaS organization (201-500 employees) focused on digitizing and modernizing healthcare in Canada and internationally, with offices around the world. R&D is done in partnership with doctors and healthcare administrators. Culture is described as transparent, collaborative and innovation-focused. Bilingual French/English operation is core to the company.
