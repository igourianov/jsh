# Paxos

- **Company Type:** Platform / Regulated Blockchain Infrastructure
- **Stage:** Scale-up (valued at $2.4B, conditionally approved for national trust bank charter)
- **Size:** ~287 employees (post-layoffs, previously ~350)
- **Remote Policy:** Remote-first. Employees across the US, UK, Europe and Asia. $500 stipend for remote setup. Annual in-person company forum.

## Quick Take
- Heavily regulated fintech building stablecoin and tokenization infrastructure for major clients (PayPal, Mastercard, Nubank, Interactive Brokers). Strong institutional credibility.
- 20% layoff in June 2024 (65 people) despite $500M+ on balance sheet. Narrowed focus to stablecoins and tokenization. Signals strategic pivot, not financial distress.
- OCC conditionally approved national trust bank charter in December 2025. This is a significant regulatory milestone that few crypto companies have achieved.

## Milestones
- **2012** - Founded as itBit (Bitcoin exchange) by Charles Cascarilla and Rich Teo
- **2015** - Rebranded to Paxos Trust Company. Received limited-purpose trust charter from NYDFS.
- **2018 (May)** - Secured $65M in funding
- **2018 (Sep)** - Launched Paxos Standard stablecoin (later renamed Pax Dollar / USDP)
- **2019 (Sep)** - Launched PAX Gold (gold-backed token)
- **2020 (Feb)** - SEC no-action letter for blockchain settlement service
- **2020 (Jul)** - Launched crypto brokerage (Revolut as first client)
- **2020 (Oct)** - PayPal partnership announced
- **2021 (Apr)** - Reached $540M total funding
- **2022** - Licensed by Monetary Authority of Singapore
- **2023 (Aug)** - Began issuing PayPal USD (PYUSD) on Ethereum
- **2024 (May)** - Extended PYUSD to Solana
- **2024 (Jun)** - 20% workforce reduction (65 employees). Narrowed focus to stablecoins and tokenization.
- **2025 (Jun)** - Launched Paxos Labs startup incubator for DeFi integration
- **2025 (Nov)** - Acquired Fordefi (MPC wallet and custody technology)
- **2025 (Dec)** - OCC conditionally approved national trust bank charter

## Company & Product
Paxos is a New York-based regulated blockchain infrastructure company. Its core business is enabling enterprises to tokenize, custody, trade and settle digital assets in a compliant manner.

Key products include stablecoin issuance (USDP, PYUSD for PayPal, PAXG gold token, Global Dollar USDG), crypto brokerage APIs and settlement services. The company positions itself as the regulated infrastructure layer that lets large financial institutions offer crypto products without building the compliance and technology stack themselves.

Clients include PayPal, Mastercard, Interactive Brokers, Nubank, Mercado Libre, Revolut and OANDA. The company publishes monthly reserve reports on all issued digital assets.

Mission: "Enable the instant movement of any asset to anyone in a trustworthy way."

## Engineering Culture
Paxos maintains an engineering blog at eng.paxos.com and has open-sourced several projects on GitHub (134 repos total, though most are private). Notable public repos include stablecoin smart contracts (USDP, PYUSD, BUSD, PAXG), a Redis-backed asyncio Python library (subconscious, 253 stars) and cross-chain contracts in Rust.

Engineering practices include Scrum/Agile with bi-weekly sprints, CI/CD pipelines with thorough testing across environments, and infrastructure as code. The team selects languages per problem domain rather than enforcing a single stack.

The company uses FAANG-style leveling for career progression, which Glassdoor reviews note can make promotion bureaucratic.

## Business Stability
- **Funding:** $535-540M total raised from investors including RRE Ventures, Liberty City Ventures and Coinbase. Valuation $2.4B.
- **Balance sheet:** $500M+ cash despite layoffs, indicating financial strength.
- **Regulatory:** OCC national trust bank charter (conditional, Dec 2025). NYDFS trust charter (2015). MAS license (2022). This regulatory moat is a significant competitive advantage.
- **Strategic direction:** Post-2024 layoffs, narrowed focus to stablecoins and tokenization. Acquired Fordefi (custody/wallet tech) in Nov 2025. Launched Paxos Labs incubator in Jun 2025.
- **Growth:** 3% headcount growth in the year following layoffs, suggesting stabilization.

The company is well-capitalized and regulatory-first, which provides durability. The narrowed focus post-layoffs is a positive signal of strategic clarity. The national bank charter pursuit indicates long-term institutional ambitions.

## Red Flags
- **2024 layoffs (20%)** despite strong balance sheet. Could indicate previous over-hiring or strategic misalignment. The narrowed focus afterward suggests the company was spread too thin.
- **Glassdoor: engineering direction lacking.** Multiple reviews mention frequent business plan changes and unclear engineering direction. For a manager, this could mean navigating ambiguity and shielding teams from context-switching.
- **Low interview experience rating (33% positive).** Could indicate a disorganized or poorly calibrated hiring process. Worth probing during interviews.
- **Ops burden on engineers.** Reviews mention high operational load. Could signal understaffed SRE/platform teams or immature tooling.
- **Crypto industry volatility.** While Paxos is better positioned than most (regulated, institutional clients, stablecoins), the broader crypto market affects hiring, morale and business priorities.
