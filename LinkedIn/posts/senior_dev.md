What makes a senior engineer?

Lately my LinkedIn timeline is being flooded with both non-technical, surface level takes and AI slop on this topic. Naturally, I have to respond.

An intermediate level engineer is someone who can execute well on technical tasks, given detailed product and architectural specifications.

A senior engineer is someone who can understand value and can make decisions.

How to test for that during an interview? Very simple.
Ask them for a strong opinion that they may have, especially if it would be unpopular. Play devil's advocate with them. Have them argue their point to you.

Doesn't matter what it is
• Tech debt vs features
• TBD vs Gitflow
• Functional vs class components in React
• Spaces vs tabs

Having strong opinions and being able to defend them is the only true test of seniority.
