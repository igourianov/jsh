# EDITORIAL VERSION

Does every engineer on your team need to design scalable systems?

This implicit test for seniority hides an organizational time bomb.

You hire all seniors (or near seniors), and have yourself an all-star team. Short term this is great. But in the long term this team inevitably falls apart.

If every engineer is a senior, you create a too-many-cooks situation. Since there aren't enough scalable systems for everyone to design, a large portion of your team will either get bored and leave, or devolve into petty squabbles over interesting work. Endless ego-driven debate over decisions that do not matter much. Disengagement and sabotage. 

The reality:
• Someone needs to develop boring business features
• Someone needs to fix those pesky bugs
• Very few resources are needed to design scalable systems

All-senior teams don't work long term. You need a good spread of levels for everyone to have interesting work and a room to grow.

Build diverse, organizationally stable teams instead of engaging in elitism.
