# Atlas HXM

**Company Type:** SaaS/Platform (Employer of Record)
**Stage:** Scale-up (Series B funded)
**Size:** ~500+ employees globally, ~2,200 including contractor network
**Remote Policy:** Fully remote/distributed (company's core mission is enabling global remote employment)

## Quick Take
- Pioneer in the EOR space (coined the term "Employer of Record" in 2015), now the largest Direct EOR with wholly-owned entities in 160+ countries
- Significant leadership instability: 3 CEOs, 3 CFOs, multiple CTOs in under 2 years; founder stepped down weeks before major layoffs
- Glassdoor rating of 2.5/5 with only 28% recommending as employer - well below industry average of 3.8
- Strong funding ($220M total) and revenue growth (300% 2021-2022) but concerning internal culture signals

## Milestones
- **2015**: Founded by Rick Hammell as Elements Global Services; pioneered first 100% direct EOR model
- **2017**: Expanded to 80 countries; opened UK and India offices
- **2019**: Reached 100 countries; launched ApprovPay platform
- **2020**: $20M Series A from Guidepost Growth Equity; expanded to 135 countries
- **2022**: $200M Series B from Sixth Street; rebranded to Atlas HXM; launched HXM platform
- **2023**: Founder Rick Hammell stepped down as CEO; Jim McCoy appointed as new CEO; 150+ layoffs (15% workforce); multiple leadership changes
- **2024-2025**: Continued layoffs every 4-5 months per employee reports; Chris Capriccio current CTO

## Company & Product

Atlas HXM is a global Human Experience Management (HXM) platform providing Employer of Record (EOR) services. The company enables businesses to hire talent anywhere in the world without establishing local legal entities.

**Key Products:**
- Global Payroll - tax and payment processing across 160+ countries
- Compliance Management - local labor laws and cross-border regulations
- Employment Contracts - localized, legally compliant contracts
- Employee Benefits - matching local market standards
- Onboarding - automated process with native language contracts
- Learning & Development - access to 9,000+ courses
- Mobile Apps - iOS/Android for employee self-service
- Expense Management - international expense claims and reimbursements

**Differentiator:** Unlike competitors who use third-party partners, Atlas owns its legal entities in all 160+ countries, enabling direct control over service quality and faster issue resolution.

**Target Market:** Fast-growing tech companies, startups, and remote-first teams needing international employment infrastructure.

**Pricing:** ~$599/employee/month (higher than competitors like Deel and Remote)

## Red Flags

1. **Severe Leadership Instability:** 3 CEOs, 3 CFOs, multiple CTOs in under 2 years. Founder stepped down weeks before 150+ layoffs.

2. **Continuous Layoffs:** Reports of layoffs every 4-5 months since 2023. "Hundreds have been let go in the last two years despite leadership's promises of growth."

3. **Poor Employee Sentiment:** 2.5/5 Glassdoor rating, only 28% recommend employer, 29% positive outlook - all significantly below industry averages.

4. **Product Team Gutted:** Reports of large portion of product team laid off along with CTO exit, suggesting potential technical leadership vacuum.

5. **Cultural Disconnect:** "For People, By People" tagline at odds with reported internal culture of "revolving door" and "hire-and-fire" mentality.

6. **Communication Issues:** Non-US employees report being "left even more in the dark" about company direction and challenges.

7. **No Engineering Visibility:** No public engineering blog, no GitHub presence, limited technical culture signals for a "technology company."
