# Engineering Manager - OpenLoop Health

**Source URL:** https://openloophealth.com/careers/5015404008

**Location:** United States, Hybrid, Remote

**Company:** OpenLoop Health

## Company Overview

OpenLoop Health is a white-label telehealth infrastructure provider co-founded by Dr. Jon Lensing (CEO) and Christian Williams (COO). The company delivers scalable clinical, legal, and financial solutions for healthcare organizations expanding virtual care.

**Headquarters:** Des Moines, IA (317 6th Ave, Suite 400)

## Core Responsibilities

**Team Leadership:**
- Lead and mentor software engineers (backend, frontend, full-stack, or platform roles)
- Conduct 1:1s, performance reviews, and professional development discussions
- Build a culture of ownership, psychological safety, continuous learning

**Technical Ownership:**
- Oversee design, development, and maintenance of secure telehealth platform features
- Guide technical decision-making and architectural choices
- Ensure on-time delivery through cross-functional partnerships
- Maintain code quality standards

**Cross-Functional Collaboration:**
- Partner with Product Management on requirements and prioritization
- Coordinate with Security, Clinical, and Operations teams on regulatory compliance
- Align with other Engineering Managers on processes and infrastructure

**Operational Excellence:**
- Own team metrics for delivery, quality, reliability, and productivity
- Improve engineering processes and workflows
- Support incident response and system resilience improvements

## Required Qualifications

- 5+ years software engineering experience; 1–3+ years in engineering leadership
- Strong modern web development background (Node.js, Python, React, AWS, microservices)
- Proven team scaling in high-growth environments
- Ability to lead complex technical initiatives
- Excellent communication and interpersonal skills

## Preferred Qualifications

- Telehealth or highly regulated domain experience
- Knowledge of HL7, FHIR standards, and HIPAA compliance
- Experience with distributed/hybrid teams
- DevOps, CI/CD, cloud infrastructure, or security expertise

## Benefits

OpenLoop emphasizes a relatively flat organizational structure with core values of Autonomy, Competence, and Belonging, encouraging employee autonomy and idea-sharing.
