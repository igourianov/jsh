## Role Summary

DocSend, a rapidly expanding Dropbox division experiencing "double-digit year-over-year growth," seeks an Engineering Manager to lead approximately 11 engineers. The team focuses on expanding Virtual Data Room capabilities and integrating AI-driven features into deal workflows.

## Key Responsibilities

- Lead a full-stack product engineering team of ~11 direct reports
- Collaborate with Product Manager and Designer on roadmaps and sprint planning
- Drive customer results through Agile execution and standard processes
- Support engineering excellence by balancing technical rigor with business objectives
- Develop team talent through mentoring and inclusive hiring practices
- Build collaborative, high-accountability team culture

*Note: On-call rotation participation required; core and non-core business hours may apply.*

## Requirements

- BS/MS/PhD in Computer Science or related technical field (or equivalent coding experience)
- Minimum 3 years managing engineering teams
- Minimum 5 years software engineering or equivalent technical experience
- Demonstrated collaboration with Product Managers on strategic direction
- Proven experience running standard Agile processes

## Preferred Qualifications

- Ruby on Rails and React web application development
- High-ambiguity environment experience (startups or new initiatives)
