## Analysis

This role presents significant tech stack and domain challenges for Ilia:

**Tech Stack Mismatch:**
The role requires hands-on involvement with Kotlin, Golang, and Rust - languages Ilia has zero experience with. While the coding requirement is estimated at only 10%, the role explicitly requires "proven experience building production services" in these languages, suggesting this is non-negotiable background experience, not just nice-to-have.

**Domain Gap:**
SDK development and enterprise integrations are core to this role. While Ilia has built enterprise products, there's no evidence of:
- Shipping multi-language SDKs
- Designing APIs for external developer consumption
- Owning developer experience end-to-end (docs, samples, integration guides)
- Streaming/codec optimization work
- Enterprise authentication implementation (OAuth, SAML)

**Strong Leadership Fit:**
The people management, team scaling, and enterprise delivery aspects are excellent matches. Ilia has exactly the kind of Fortune 500 experience and team-building track record this role needs.

**Ramp-up Considerations:**
Even with strong leadership skills, the tech stack transition from .NET/C# to Kotlin/Golang/Rust would require significant ramp-up time. Combined with learning SDK design patterns and streaming concepts, this could be a 6-12 month learning curve before being fully effective in the technical aspects of the role.

**Recommendation:**
This role would be a stretch. The leadership fit is strong, but the technical domain and language requirements create substantial barriers. Would likely face rejection in early screening due to lack of Kotlin/Golang/Rust and SDK experience.
