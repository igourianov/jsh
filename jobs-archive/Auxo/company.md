## Company & Product

Auxo (also known as Auxo Growth Partner or Auxo Recrutement) is a Montreal-based recruitment and HR boutique agency founded in 2020. They specialize exclusively in working with venture-backed tech and SaaS startups across the United States and Canada.

The company operates under the philosophy of "speed and precision" - emphasizing rapid hiring without compromising candidate quality. They position themselves as more than recruiters, aiming to act as an extension of a startup's HR division to find culture-fit "A-players."

Key team members include Jean-Philippe Martineau (CRHA) and Kevin Buckingham (Co-Founder - Tech Recruitment Operations).

## Services

Auxo offers tailored solutions for startups at different growth stages:
- Technical Recruitment
- HR Consultation
- RPO (Recruitment Process Outsourcing)
- HRIS and ATS Implementation
- Staffing
- Fractional leadership roles (Part-time Head of People, CTO, HR Coordinator)

They focus on engineering, product, and leadership roles at startups.
