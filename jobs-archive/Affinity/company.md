# Affinity

**Company Type:** B2B SaaS / Relationship Intelligence Platform
**Stage:** Scale-up (Series C, $120M raised)
**Size:** ~240 employees (151 US, 83 Canada, 7 UK)
**Remote Policy:** Remote-first with hubs in San Francisco, Toronto, and New York

## Quick Take

- Strong culture scores (92% Great Place to Work, 4.4/5 Glassdoor) with emphasis on trust and autonomy
- Recent executive team turnover (new CEO, CTO, CFO, CMO) - could signal transformation or instability
- Cross-team communication challenges cited as engineering scales - typical growth pains
- No layoffs reported; 93% retention rate suggests stability

## Company & Product

Affinity is a relationship intelligence platform founded in 2014, headquartered in San Francisco. The platform serves 3,000+ customers in private capital (VC, PE, investment banking) and professional services. It automatically captures communication data (emails, calendar) and uses AI to map organizational networks and surface relationship insights.

Key differentiator: Automated data capture vs. manual CRM entry. The platform processes trillions of interactions to identify warm introduction paths and relationship strength.

Recent focus areas include AI/automation features and API improvements. The company acquired Nudge (relationship intelligence) in a recent M/A deal.

## Engineering Culture

**Interview process:** Includes coding challenge and system design. Average 16 days, 2.72/5 difficulty rating, 44.8% positive experience.

**Team structure:** Engineering teams organized by product area. Senior Director role reports to CTO (Rebecca Campbell, recently appointed).

**Values:**
- "We Are Owners" - accountability for outcomes, not just tasks
- "Growth Mindset" - embrace feedback, learn from setbacks
- "Respectful Candor" - direct, constructive conversations
- "Taste the soup" - engineers expected to use the product firsthand

**Challenges noted:** Cross-team communication and coordination on cross-cutting concerns can slow velocity. Employees mention re-organizations and questions about engineering direction clarity.

**Open source:** No significant public repos from Affinity directly. Third-party SDK wrappers exist (Python, Node.js). Company has 41 repos on GitHub, mostly forked tools.
