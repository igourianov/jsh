## Summary

This is a Technical Lead Manager (TLM) role on Jerry's Marketplace Automation team. The team owns the "last mile" of insurance policy purchase: complex, multi-step orchestration with external insurance provider systems that largely lack modern APIs.

Phase 1 is heavily IC-weighted — refactoring a tightly coupled legacy automation system into a fault-tolerant, decoupled architecture. Phase 2 shifts toward building AI-first internal tools to scale from 5M to 50M users. The role reports directly to the co-founder/CTO with no management layers.

Despite the EM title, the role is explicitly 80% coding / 20% management.

- Own and evolve the insurance marketplace automation framework (async state management, fault tolerance, external interface orchestration)
- Lead incremental refactoring of legacy automation systems; pair with engineers on new architecture standards
- Design and implement next-generation AI-first internal tooling
- Manage a small engineering team with direct CTO reporting line
