# Ada Health

**Company Type:** SaaS / HealthTech AI Platform
**Stage:** Scale-up (Post-Series B)
**Size:** ~225 employees
**Remote Policy:** Not specified (offices in Berlin, Boca Raton, London, Toronto)

## Quick Take
- Reached profitability in 2023 with 260% YoY revenue growth, but Glassdoor rating is low (2.8/5) with only 25% positive business outlook
- Two major layoffs (40% total) in 2023-2024 indicate management struggles despite revenue growth
- Limited engineering culture visibility: no public engineering blog, minimal GitHub presence (4 repos), suggests low investment in engineering brand

## Company & Product

Ada Health, founded in 2011 and headquartered in Berlin, develops AI-powered clinical diagnostic tools. The company's mission is to "Uncover the Undiagnosed" through Patient Finder, their proprietary AI platform that covers 30,000+ ICD-10 health conditions and engages ~100 million health visitors monthly.

**Product:** Patient Finder accelerates diagnosis and patient access to therapy through customizable care pathways (telehealth, provider directories, lab tests, discussion guides). The platform has been validated in leading medical journals (The Lancet Digital Health, BMJ Open, Journal of Medical Internet Research).

**Clients:** Major life sciences companies including MSD, Bayer, Kyowa Kirin, ResMed, Sanofi, and Biogen.

**Financial trajectory:** After raising $201M in total funding (Series B: $90M-$120M in 2021), Ada Health achieved profitability in 2023 with $75M annual revenue (as of July 2025). However, the company conducted two major layoff rounds (40% workforce reduction) in 2023-2024, primarily affecting tech, product development, marketing, and medical teams in Europe/UK.

**Recent developments (2024):** Expanded leadership team with new CPO (Nick Altebrando) and SVP of Business Development (Yury Rozenman) in June 2024, followed by additional US-focused leadership hires in October 2024.

## Engineering Culture

No dedicated engineering blog, minimal GitHub activity (4 public repos: medical case report corpus, Okta tools, Epic integration docs, deprecated Docker container). Suggests low investment in engineering brand and developer community engagement.

**Team environment (from Glassdoor):**

**Work flexibility:** Flexible work arrangements mentioned as a positive, but specific remote policy not disclosed.

## Business Stability

**Funding History:**
- Total raised: $201M
- Series B: $90M-$120M (May 2021, led by Leaps by Bayer)
- Reached profitability: 2023
- Annual revenue: $75M (as of July 2025)

**Growth Trajectory:**
- 2023: Achieved profitability with 260% YoY revenue growth
- 2023-2024: Conducted two major layoff rounds (40% workforce reduction, ~50 people in 2023 layoffs)
- 2024: Multiple leadership hires focused on US expansion

**Sustainability Assessment:** Mixed signals. Strong revenue growth and profitability are positive, but repeated large layoffs (40% total workforce) despite revenue growth suggests:
- Overhiring followed by aggressive cost-cutting
- Poor resource planning and management execution
- Potential pivot in business strategy or market challenges
- Company responding to industry changes with repeated "nimble" adjustments (per company response to Glassdoor review)

The company acknowledges needing to "remain nimble in an industry that changes often," which may indicate ongoing volatility.

## Red Flags

- **Severe layoffs despite profitability:** 40% workforce reduction across 2023-2024 despite 260% revenue growth and achieving profitability suggests management dysfunction or business model instability
- **Low employee confidence:** 2.8/5 Glassdoor rating, only 25% positive business outlook, only 49% would recommend to friend
- **Leadership capability concerns:** Multiple reviews cite founders lacking understanding of how product/engineering organizations should work
- **Engineering underinvestment:** "You need more engineers to better balance out the work load," infrastructure reliability issues ("Stuff was always breaking")
- **Organizational instability:** Frequent restructuring tied to management changes creates team disruption
- **No engineering culture visibility:** No engineering blog, minimal GitHub presence (4 repos), suggests weak engineering brand investment
- **Potential location mismatch:** Berlin HQ with offices across 3 continents but unclear remote policy. Integration and Onboarding role may require European timezone alignment.
