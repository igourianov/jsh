## Company

Upbound is the creator and primary maintainer of Crossplane, a CNCF open-source project that extends Kubernetes into a cloud-native control plane framework. The company builds on top of Crossplane to offer Upbound Cloud, a hosted multi-tenant control plane platform targeting Fortune 500 companies and platform engineering teams. Their product vision is an "AI-native Control Fabric" where control planes become programmable and ecosystem-driven, enabling infrastructure to be managed by both humans and AI agents. Crossplane has 100M+ downloads and is used by 1,000+ teams globally. Upbound is Series B, backed by GV (formerly Google Ventures), Altimeter Capital and Intel Capital, with $69M raised to date. The company operates in the infrastructure and developer tooling space alongside cloud providers and ISV ecosystem partners.
